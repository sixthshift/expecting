**The hospital doesn't register the birth for you.** You do that yourselves, with your state or territory's Births, Deaths and Marriages registry, using the forms the hospital or birth centre gives you.

The deadline is **60 days after the birth** — that's how myGov puts it for every state and territory. (Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says the ACT allows 6 months; check your own registry.) Each state's process and forms differ slightly, and some ACT hospitals are trialling registration through myGov.

Registering is free; the birth certificate costs extra. You'll need the baby's full name settled — which is a good reason to have a shortlist before the birth.
