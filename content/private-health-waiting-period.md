If you'd like to give birth as a private patient, timing matters more than most people realise. Health funds can make you wait up to **12 months** before they pay pregnancy and birth (obstetrics) benefits, and the pregnant parent has to have served the full wait *before* being admitted to hospital.

A few things catch people out:

- Many cheaper hospital policies don't include obstetrics at all, or only cover you as a private patient in a public hospital.
- To cover your newborn from birth, you may need to move to a family or single-parent membership ahead of time. Each fund has its own rules, so ask yours.

If you're already pregnant and not covered, public care through Medicare is still there for the whole pregnancy and birth.
