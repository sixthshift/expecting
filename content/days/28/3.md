title: Which way the baby is lying

From here your midwife or doctor will start talking about how the baby is lying, and two words get mixed up. Presentation is the part facing down: cephalic is head first, which most babies are, and breech is bottom or feet first. Position is which way the back of the baby's head faces: anterior, towards the front of your belly; lateral, to your side; or posterior, towards your back.

Anterior is ideal for a vaginal birth, because the smallest part of the head goes first. A baby who stays posterior through labour may make it a little longer, with more back pain, and being on hands and knees may help with that. But babies usually move into a good position, even late in pregnancy, and position often changes with contractions.

Presentation is usually checked by feeling your belly around 36 weeks. Most babies are head first by 37 weeks.
