---
title: This baby's own pattern
read: [baby-moving-less]
---
By now the baby has a pattern of their own, and they keep moving every day right up to the birth. There's no number of kicks to reach: counting kicks or keeping a chart is no longer recommended. What matters is a change from this baby's pattern: moving less and less, movements that aren't as strong, or none at all.

Don't try to check for yourself. A home doppler or an app can't tell you the baby is well, and eating or drinking something doesn't make a baby move.

If the movements slow down, weaken, change or stop, contact your midwife, doctor or hospital birth unit straight away, not the next day; if you can't reach them, go to your nearest hospital birthing or emergency unit. They'll usually check the baby's heart rate on a CTG. Call again every time it happens: you are not wasting their time. If you need urgent medical help, call 000.
