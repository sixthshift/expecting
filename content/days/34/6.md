---
title: How long labour takes
read: [length-of-labour, card-nobody-knows-how-long]
---
Nobody can tell you how long your labour will be, and the ranges are wide. The first stage is the longest. Its early, latent phase can last many hours or even days; the active phase may take 8 to 18 hours for a first baby, and 5 to 12 if you've had a baby before. From full dilation, the birth is generally expected within 3 hours for a first birth, or 2 if you've given birth before, and an epidural can make that stage longer.

A first baby is often the longest. If you've had a baby before, you can expect this labour to be quicker, which may help you plan when to go to hospital.

The evidence on what's normal and what's slow isn't strong. If the birth isn't close after 2 hours of the second stage in a first birth, or up to an hour if you've had a baby, your care team calls a doctor to review.
