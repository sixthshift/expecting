---
title: When the bump drops
read: [card-the-bump-drops, term-engagement, term-lightening]
---
By 36 weeks the uterus reaches all the way up to your ribcage, and in this last month you might feel breathless at times as the baby presses against your diaphragm.

Then, at some point, the baby's head moves down into your pelvis: engagement, a sign they're getting ready to be born. With a first baby the head has often moved down by about 36 weeks, though it can happen any time until the birth; if you've had babies before, it may not engage until you have contractions.

You might notice the bump has moved down slightly, which is what people mean by the bump dropping, or lightening. The breathlessness should ease as the baby moves lower. The trade is more pressure low in your abdomen and pelvis, and probably more trips to the toilet.
