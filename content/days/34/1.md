---
title: Perineal massage, from now
read: [perineal-massage, card-perineal-massage-from-34]
---
Most people can start perineal massage around now, at 34 or 35 weeks, and not before 34. It stretches the perineum, the space between the vagina and the anus, and may reduce the chance of a tear or an episiotomy at the birth, especially a first birth. It's your choice, and some people choose not to.

Once or twice a week is enough, for up to 5 minutes; more often is usually not recommended. You or the partner can do it, with clean hands and guided by you. It may burn and feel uncomfortable at first, and usually gets easier after 2 to 3 weeks; it shouldn't hurt.

It isn't for anyone with a low-lying placenta or a short cervix, bleeding from the vagina, an infection such as thrush or herpes, or severe blood pressure problems, and your midwife or doctor can say when to start.
