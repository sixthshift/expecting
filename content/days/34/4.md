---
title: Breathing through it
read: [breathing-and-relaxation]
---
Relaxation methods can help ease labour pain: they may distract you, help you focus away from the pain, and make the room calm. Breathing techniques, music, yoga and mindfulness are all options, and hypnobirthing is among the other non-medical methods.

It's more than distraction. When you're relaxed your body works better, and your labour hormone (oxytocin) and natural pain-relief hormones (endorphins) are released more readily. Fear, tension and resistance are a normal response when you're unsure what to expect, and RANZCOG's advice is to learn how to relax, stay calm and breathe deeply: something to practise in the weeks that are left.

Music is a good distraction, but it can turn suddenly and unexpectedly annoying, so be ready for that. And none of it rules anything out: you can use it alongside medical pain relief, and whether to use pain relief at all is your choice.
