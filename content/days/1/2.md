---
title: What 6+3 means
read: [term-gestational-age, term-completed-weeks]
---
How far along you are has a name, gestational age, and it's written as weeks plus days: 38+1 is 38 weeks and 1 day. It's counted from the first day of your last period to today, and it can also be worked out by ultrasound, or from a clinical assessment during the pregnancy.

The weeks are completed weeks. The Australian Institute of Health and Welfare counts the length of a pregnancy that way, so "28 weeks" on a record means 28 whole weeks have finished, and it stays 28 until the next week is complete.

So when you see 28 weeks on your notes, that's 28 behind you, and the next one under way.
