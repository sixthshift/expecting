---
title: Folic acid, before it's needed
read: [folic-acid, term-folate]
---
Folate is a B vitamin found in foods such as green leafy vegetables; folic acid is the version you take as a supplement. It matters now, before a pregnancy, because it helps prevent neural tube defects such as spina bifida, and those form in the first weeks, often before you know you're pregnant. Enough folate before and during the first 3 months prevents most cases, though not all.

The advice is a daily supplement with at least 400 micrograms, from at least a month before trying, through the first 3 months. In Australia most come as 500 micrograms, alone or in a pregnancy supplement; general multivitamins usually have less. Bread flour, except organic, has some added, about 120 micrograms in three slices: a safety net, not the whole answer.

If you didn't start before, the advice is to start as soon as you can once you know.
