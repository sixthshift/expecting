---
title: When the dose is higher
read: [high-dose-folic-acid]
---
Most people take 0.5 mg of folic acid a day. Some need ten times that, 5 mg, because the chance of a neural tube defect is higher for them.

Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says your doctor may recommend 5 mg if you have diabetes, are living with overweight or obesity, take anticonvulsant (epilepsy) medicine, have a family member affected by a neural tube defect, or are at risk of poor food absorption. MotherSafe adds a neural tube defect of your own, a family history on either parent's side, and some antipsychotic medicines.

For some, including a multiple pregnancy, a BMI over 30, malabsorption or inflammatory bowel disease, MotherSafe says 5 mg should be considered throughout the pregnancy, not only the first months. If any of it fits you, it's a question for your doctor: whether you need the higher dose, and for how long.
