---
title: Why week 1 comes before conception
read: [card-two-weeks-pregnant-before-conception, term-lmp]
---
If the weeks look strange, this is why. A pregnancy is counted from the first day of your last period, not from conception, and it's common to conceive about 2 weeks after a period. Most babies are born about 38 weeks after conception, which is how it adds up to 40 weeks from the start of that last period.

Midwives and doctors call that first day the LMP, the last menstrual period. Your gestational age is measured from it, and your due date is about 40 weeks after it.

It works best with a regular cycle, and can be harder to rely on if yours is longer, shorter or irregular. Then an early ultrasound can work out the dates instead.
