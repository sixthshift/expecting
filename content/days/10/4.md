---
title: The booking visit
read: [booking-visit]
---
Your first appointment at the hospital where you're booked to give birth is the booking visit, often with a midwife, and it's longer than the ones after it. Timing varies: the Royal Hospital for Women in Sydney, for one, usually sees people at about 14 weeks.

Expect a lot of questions: your health, earlier pregnancies, operations, medicines and allergies, your family's health, and whether you smoke, drink or use drugs. Blood pressure, height and weight are measured, and blood tests are offered. There's also a screen of how you're going, asking about stress, depression and anxiety, support at home and work, and family violence; you don't have to answer, and what you say is kept in confidence.

You'll go through the tests on offer and their costs (none are compulsory), and your model of care: who looks after you, where, and how many visits. The Royal Hospital for Women asks you to bring the results your GP organised.
