---
title: Takeaway, and the next day's lunch
read: [takeaway-and-eating-out, leftovers]
---
Takeaway is still on. Freshly cooked food eaten hot is the safest choice, and the foods to skip are the same ones you'd skip at home: cold deli meats and cold cooked chicken, soft cheeses unless cooked, store-bought sushi and raw seafood, pre-made salads and sprouts, and soft serve. Hot chicken is fine if you eat it straight away.

The sauces are the part people miss. House-made mayonnaise and aioli are raw-egg foods; commercial ones are fine. Hummus and tahini are on the avoid list too. And food should be kept either cold or hot, not lukewarm, because at warm temperatures most food-poisoning bacteria grow very quickly.

What doesn't get eaten becomes leftovers, on a shorter clock than usual: straight into the fridge, covered, eaten within a day rather than the usual 3 to 4, and reheated until steaming hot all the way through. Rice and pasta can carry bacteria whose toxins heat doesn't destroy, so they're reheated only once.
