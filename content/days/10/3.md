---
title: A sugar test, early
read: [early-diabetes-test]
---
Most people meet the glucose test at 24 to 28 weeks. Some are offered a first look much sooner, in this first trimester, if they have a higher risk of high blood sugar.

The risks include a weight above the healthy range, being over 40, a family history of type 2 diabetes, having had a large baby before, PCOS, and some ethnic backgrounds. For them, an HbA1c blood test is recommended in the first trimester. It's an ordinary blood test, with no fasting needed.

If you've had gestational diabetes in an earlier pregnancy, or the HbA1c result suggests it, the next step is an oral glucose tolerance test before 20 weeks. Either way, an early test doesn't replace the later one: every pregnancy still has the glucose tolerance test at 24 to 28 weeks.
