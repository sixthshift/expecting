---
title: What NIPT can tell you
read: [nipt]
---
Some of the baby's DNA passes into your blood, and from around 10 weeks a blood test, NIPT, can check it. It estimates the chance of Down syndrome, Edwards and Patau syndromes and sex chromosome conditions, and it can tell you the baby's sex, if you want to know.

It identifies about 99% of babies with Down syndrome, more than combined screening's 85 to 90%. It's still a screen: a higher-chance result needs CVS or amniocentesis to confirm it. It doesn't cover every genetic condition, and it doesn't include the 11 to 13 week scan, which is arranged separately if you want it.

It costs around $450 to $800, and neither Medicare nor private health insurance covers it. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby suggests talking to your doctor, midwife or a genetic counsellor first, and giving yourselves some time to decide. Choosing no screening at all is a choice too.
