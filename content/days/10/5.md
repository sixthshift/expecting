---
title: Peanuts and the other allergy foods
read: [peanuts-and-allergens]
---
If allergies run in your family, it can seem sensible to cut out peanuts and the other foods babies react to. The advice is the opposite: it's fine to eat highly allergenic foods in pregnancy if you haven't had an allergic reaction to them yourself.

The common allergy foods are egg, cow's milk, peanut, tree nuts, sesame, soy, wheat, fish and other seafood. Cutting them out hasn't been shown to prevent allergies in your baby, and the Australasian Society of Clinical Immunology and Allergy advises against avoiding them. Avoiding them may even mean lower weight gain in babies. A balanced diet with plenty of wholegrains, fruit, vegetables, legumes and nuts is the most useful thing you can do, especially if allergies run in the family.

The one food to keep avoiding is one you're truly allergic to yourself. Sesame dips such as hummus, tahini and halva are on the pregnancy avoid list, but for food-poisoning risk, not allergy.
