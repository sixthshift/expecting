---
title: Car seats and capsules

By law a baby up to 6 months must travel in an approved rear-facing seat that meets AS/NZS 1754. A capsule is made for that stretch, newborn to about 6 months, so hiring one is an option. Kidsafe's hire includes fitting, and capsules for low birth weight and premature babies, which many restraints can't take. It gives you breathing space before buying a seat that may not suit your car or baby; the other way is a convertible seat, rear-facing from birth.

New is best where possible, because a second-hand seat's history is unknown. A hand-me-down should be undamaged, under 10 years old and carry the AS/NZS 1754 sticker, and one that has been in a crash must be thrown away.

Whichever it is, it has to be installed properly; an authorised restraint fitting station can help. A baby is never swaddled in one, and a rear-facing seat can't go where there's a passenger airbag.
