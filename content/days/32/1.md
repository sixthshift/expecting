---
title: Scans after the morphology
read: [growth-scans, low-lying-placenta]
---
The morphology scan is the routine one; any scan after it is for a reason. If yours found the placenta low, within 2 cm of the cervix or covering it, the repeat scan comes around now, at 32 to 34 weeks, to see where it has ended up. Often it has moved up away from the cervix as the uterus expanded. If it was in a normal position early on, it's likely to stay there.

Other reasons for an extra scan are pregnancy complications, or a bump that isn't growing as expected. It can look at the baby's growth and size, the placenta and your cervix. It's safe and pain-free, and the full report goes to your doctor or midwife to talk through with you.

With a low placenta, contact your doctor or midwife immediately about any vaginal bleeding, and tell them. If it's heavy, or comes with pain or contractions, call 000 or go to emergency.
