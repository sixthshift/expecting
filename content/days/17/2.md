---
title: Fingerprints, and teeth already
read: [card-fingerprints-at-20-weeks]
---
At 17 weeks there are tiny lines on the baby's fingers that will become their fingerprints, unique to them. By 20 weeks the fingerprints are there.

So is a good deal you'd never guess. By 20 weeks the baby has permanent teeth in their gums, underneath the baby teeth, and if it's a girl, there are already eggs in her ovaries. Cartilage throughout the body is turning into bone, and the bone marrow is starting to make blood cells.

It's a strange thought to hold this week: the teeth this baby will one day chew with as an adult are already there, halfway through the pregnancy, tucked under the ones that will be their first.
