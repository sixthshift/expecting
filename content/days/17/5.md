---
title: If you've started snoring
read: [snoring]
---
Pregnancy hormones can give you a blocked, stuffy nose, and the partner may notice something new at night: snoring, sometimes for the first time in your life.

Snoring can also be a sign of something more. With gasping or pauses in your breathing, it can be obstructive sleep apnoea, where your airway partly or completely blocks while you sleep and you briefly stop breathing, often without knowing. Other signs are tossing and turning, breathing through your mouth, waking unrefreshed or with a dry mouth or headache, and feeling sleepy in the day.

See your doctor or midwife if you started snoring in pregnancy, since it can point to other problems affecting you and the baby, or if you have more pauses in your breathing or other signs of sleep apnoea. The partner sees this one better than you do, and at the appointment can describe your sleep. The doctor may refer you for an overnight sleep study.
