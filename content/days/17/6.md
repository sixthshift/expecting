---
title: Colouring your hair
read: [hair-dye]
---
If your hair is growing thicker and you want a change, you can colour it. The idea that dyeing your hair harms the baby is a myth: the chemicals aren't easily absorbed through the scalp, so the baby's exposure is minimal, and no harmful outcomes have been noted in pregnancy.

Some people choose to wait until after the first trimester; that's a choice, not a rule, and you're past it now anyway.

To lower the exposure further, the suggestions are a semi-permanent vegetable dye, left on for the minimum recommended time, and a hairdresser rather than colouring at home. At home, open windows, gloves and washing your hands before eating all help.
