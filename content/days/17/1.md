---
title: Front or back
read: [card-anterior-placenta]
---
The placenta can grow on the front wall of the uterus or the back, and where yours sits changes how you feel the baby. With an anterior placenta, on the front, movement tends to be felt at around 20 weeks and may not feel as strong. With a posterior one, at the back, movements are often felt earlier and stronger. You're also more likely to feel them early if this isn't your first pregnancy.

So if a friend felt kicks at 16 weeks and you've felt nothing yet, where your placenta sits may be part of it.

Every baby is unique, so once you feel them, get to know your baby's own pattern. If you're ever concerned about it, or notice the movements decreasing, contact your midwife, hospital birth unit or doctor immediately. Don't wait until the next day.
