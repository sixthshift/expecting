---
title: For the partner, the mood swings
read: [card-mood-swings-not-personal, card-partner-left-out]
---
There may be mood swings in pregnancy, and Pregnancy, Birth and Baby tells partners to try not to take them personally. Pregnancy can be exhausting, physically and emotionally, and it's a time of adjustment for both of you.

If the partner feels you have the bigger role and they're on the outside, MensLine says there are ways to get more involved, starting with asking you what help you need, and helping with the housework, cooking and shopping. Looking at baby equipment together is another.

Talking about how you're each feeling is the other half of it. If you're very anxious, or seem depressed about the pregnancy, the partner's part is to encourage you to ask for help.
