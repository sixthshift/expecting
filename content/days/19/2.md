---
title: Bigger feet, looser joints
read: [card-feet-grow, ligaments-and-joints-in-pregnancy]
---
Around now you may find your feet grow. Pregnancy hormones soften your ligaments so they can stretch to carry the extra weight, and feet are among the joints affected, though you notice it most in your pelvis and lower back.

Along with a changing posture and weight gain, softer ligaments can mean aches, back and pelvic pain, and feeling unsteady. A physiotherapist can teach you exercises that strengthen your muscles.

Back, pelvic or hip pain is one to see your doctor about, as some other causes can be serious. See your doctor immediately for pain without movement, an unexpected fever, bleeding or fluid leaking from your vagina, weight loss without trying, trouble controlling your bladder or bowels, numbness or weakness in your legs, or after a fall or injury; from 20 weeks, contact your hospital straight away for any bleeding or if your waters may have broken.
