---
title: Your skin, now
read: [skin-in-pregnancy, sunscreen]
---
Higher hormone levels and extra blood flow change your skin. Some people find it "glows"; others get dry, blotchy or more sensitive skin, or acne. Almost everyone gets stretch marks, red, pink or purple lines, and no skin product has been proven to prevent them. Most changes improve or go after the birth, and the usual ones aren't dangerous for you or the baby.

If your skin is itchy, even without a rash, seek medical advice: itchy skin can be a sign of a more serious condition that can harm the baby.

Most moisturisers and cosmetics are safe, though products with vitamin A (retinol), hydroquinone or tretinoin are best avoided. It's never safe to sunbathe. Sunscreen is advisable, since avoiding sunburn matters as much now as ever; the ingredients are generally absorbed only minimally and don't pose a risk.
