---
title: A good time for the dentist
read: [dental-work, bleeding-gums]
---
Hormone changes make gums more likely to swell, bleed and become inflamed, and vomiting and reflux can wear away tooth enamel. Severe gum disease has been linked with premature birth, lower birth weight and pre-eclampsia.

The advice is to see a dentist at least once in pregnancy, ideally in the first or second trimester, and the second may be the most comfortable: less likely to be sick than in the first, and an easier fit in the chair than in the third. Check-ups, fillings, root canals and extractions can be done at any stage, and most dental x-rays are safe; tell the dentist you're pregnant.

If your gums bleed, keep brushing gently, with a soft brush. After vomiting, rinse with plain water and wait at least 30 minutes before brushing. A toothache, or changes in your mouth that worry you, mean seeing your dentist as soon as possible.
