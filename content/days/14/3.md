---
title: Heartburn
read: [heartburn, antacids]
---
About 8 in 10 pregnant people get heartburn or indigestion. Hormones relax the muscle that keeps your stomach contents down, and the growing uterus pushes against your stomach. It won't cause problems for the baby.

Smaller meals eaten slowly, nothing for 2 or 3 hours before bed, and a raised bedhead help. Antacids work quickly and are safe in pregnancy at no more than the recommended dose; see your doctor if it gets severe, persistent or worse.

If what feels like heartburn comes with chest pain or shortness of breath, call 000. Seek immediate care for vomiting blood, losing weight, or painful or difficult swallowing. After 20 weeks, heartburn that antacids don't fix can be a sign of pre-eclampsia: contact your doctor, midwife or hospital immediately if they don't, or if it comes with severe pain below the ribs, sudden swelling of your hands, face or feet, a headache simple pain relief doesn't fix, or vision problems.
