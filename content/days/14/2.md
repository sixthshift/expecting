---
title: New moles and skin tags
read: [card-skin-tags, moles-changing]
---
Around now you might notice new moles or skin tags. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says they're nothing to worry about, though you can chat to your doctor or midwife if they bother you. Existing moles can change too: stretching skin and hormones can make them larger and darker, particularly on your tummy and breasts. They aren't more likely to become cancerous because you're pregnant, and they should be back to how they were within 12 months of the birth.

There's still a very small risk of any mole becoming melanoma, so keep checking them. An itchy mole is a sign to look out for. See your doctor about a mole that isn't round, has an irregular border, has different colours in it, is larger than 6 mm, is raised, or has changed in size, shape or colour during the pregnancy.
