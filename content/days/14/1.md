---
title: The best months for a trip
read: [travel-in-pregnancy, long-drives]
---
In most cases it's safe to travel while pregnant, in Australia or overseas, and the second trimester, weeks 13 to 26, is usually the best time: the risk of complications like miscarriage is lowest then, and nausea and vomiting are less common.

It may not be safe with a high chance of complications. MotherSafe names placental problems, an incompetent cervix, previous premature labour, diabetes, twins or more, and high blood pressure in this or a previous pregnancy. That's why the advice is to see your doctor or midwife before planning a trip, and not to go if they advise against it. Things can go wrong quickly in pregnancy, so where the nearest hospital with maternity care is matters.

On the road, sitting still for a long time raises the chance of a blood clot, so the advice is 5 or 6 hours a day at most, with a break at least every 2 hours to stretch, move and drink water.
