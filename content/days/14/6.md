---
title: Strength work, lighter
read: [weights, abdominal-exercises]
---
Strength training is recommended in pregnancy: the Australian Government guidelines recommend it at least 2 days a week, as light resistance training or bodyweight exercises. What to leave is heavy lifting. Your ligaments loosen in pregnancy, so heavy weights carry more chance of injury. The guidelines also rule out anything that gives you pain or discomfort, and after 16 weeks, anything done lying on your back.

Sit-ups and crunches drop out too. Your abdominal muscles stretch over the growing baby and can't work as they did: the Royal Women's Hospital calls them ineffective. They're also done on your back, where the baby can rest on major blood vessels and make you dizzy.

The core still gets worked, just differently: on hands and knees, breathe out and draw the lower part of your tummy in firmly without moving your spine, hold, and let go, building up to 10 seconds, 10 times.
