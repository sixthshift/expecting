---
title: Pain at the front of the pelvis
read: [pelvic-girdle-pain]
---
Pelvic girdle pain, once called SPD, is pain in the bones and joints of your pelvis. It's very common, usually starts between 14 and 30 weeks, often worse on stairs, rolling over in bed or getting out of the car. It's not dangerous for you or the baby, and usually goes within 3 months of the birth.

What helps: knees together getting in and out of the car, stairs one at a time, smaller steps, a pillow between your legs in bed, and a heat or ice pack. See your doctor to check it's PGP; they may refer you to a physiotherapist.

See your doctor immediately for pain that comes on without movement, or with a fever, bleeding or fluid leaking from your vagina, weight loss without trying, or after a fall or injury. Pain with trouble controlling your bladder or bowels, or numbness or weakness in your legs: call 000.
