---
title: Why sleep gets harder
read: [insomnia]
---
Sleep problems affect about 1 in 2 pregnancies and may get worse as pregnancy goes on. It's often lighter and you wake more, and late in pregnancy toilet trips, heartburn, kicks and discomfort all break it up.

What helps: a pregnancy pillow, regular bed and wake times, short naps, caffeine only in the morning, a quiet, dark room with no clock to watch and no screens for an hour before bed and drinking less in the evening. Herbal remedies and melatonin aren't recommended in pregnancy; speak with your doctor before taking anything.

See your doctor or midwife if you're worried trouble sleeping is affecting your or the baby's health, you've started snoring, heartburn is causing severe discomfort, or you're still struggling after trying these. Heartburn that comes with swelling of your hands, feet or face, a headache or distorted vision can be pre-eclampsia: contact your doctor, midwife or hospital immediately.
