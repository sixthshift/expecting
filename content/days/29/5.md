---
title: The partner's whooping cough booster
read: [partner-whooping-cough-booster]
---
This one is the partner's. Whooping cough is serious in babies under 6 months, and when a young baby catches it, the source is often someone in their own household. Vaccinating the people around a baby is called cocooning, and vaccinating both parents is estimated to cut the baby's risk of whooping cough by 51%.

The Australian Immunisation Handbook recommends a booster for the partner if their last whooping cough vaccine was more than 10 years ago, because protection wanes over that time; the same goes for anyone else living with or caring for the baby. The timing is at least 2 weeks before they have close contact with the baby.

Whether it's free for partners isn't covered by the Handbook, so the cost is a question for their GP.
