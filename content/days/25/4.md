---
title: When it isn't just heartburn
read: [pain-under-the-ribs]
---
Heartburn and indigestion are common in pregnancy, and antacids can relieve them quickly. Pain under the ribs that doesn't fit that pattern is worth taking seriously.

After 20 weeks, severe pain below the ribs or in the upper tummy, and heartburn that antacids don't fix, can be signs of severe pre-eclampsia, a serious condition that raises your blood pressure and can affect your kidneys, liver and brain. It can bring pain under the ribs with a feeling of heartburn, so the two are easy to mix up. Its other signs: a headache simple pain relief doesn't fix, blurred vision or flashing lights, and sudden swelling of your face, hands or feet.

After 20 weeks, contact your doctor, midwife or hospital immediately if you have pain under your ribs. If it comes with a severe headache or sudden swelling, seek medical attention urgently or go to emergency. A headache with changes in your vision: call 000 for an ambulance.
