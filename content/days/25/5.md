---
title: Stretch marks
read: [stretch-marks]
---
Around 9 in 10 pregnant people get stretch marks, often in the third trimester as the tummy grows, which is about to start. They're red, pink or purple lines, sometimes itchy at first, most often on the abdomen but also the thighs, breasts, hips and upper arms. They're more likely if you gain weight quickly. They won't disappear after the birth, but they fade over time to silver or white.

No skin product is scientifically proven to prevent them. Oils or creams that are safe in pregnancy won't stop them but can make your skin feel soft. They aren't harmful, so they don't need treating; prescription vitamin A creams such as tretinoin can harm the baby and shouldn't be used. Check with your doctor, midwife or pharmacist before using any cream.

If they bother you, talk to your doctor or midwife at your next visit.
