The tape measure has a job at your visits now. Fundal height is the distance from your pubic bone to the fundus, the top of your uterus, and your doctor or midwife may measure it at each antenatal visit from 24 weeks. In centimetres it should be similar to the number of weeks you're pregnant: at 25 weeks, about 25.

It's a simple check on the baby's growth. It can differ, for example if you're carrying more than one baby. A smaller than expected measurement could be a sign that the baby is growing slowly or that there's too little amniotic fluid; a larger one could mean the baby is larger than average. In both cases your doctor will monitor you and the baby carefully.
