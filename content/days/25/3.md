---
title: Choosing a pram
read: [pram, second-hand-baby-gear]
---
A pram is for a baby too young to sit up, and usually keeps them lying flat; a stroller is usually for older babies and toddlers, though many adjust to lie flat too. Under 6 months, a stroller should recline to at least 130°.

Prams and strollers should meet the Australian Product Safety Standard, based on AS/NZS 2088. That means a 5-point harness, a tether strap on the handle, red parking brakes out of your child's reach, locks so it can't collapse, a frame that won't tip, and clear warning labels. After that it's comfort, steering and how it folds.

Second-hand can save money, as long as it's safe: harness, brakes and tether working, labels readable, instructions there, and not on the Product Safety Australia recalls list. And a dry cloth over one on a hot day is a danger: under a canopy it can be up to 4 degrees hotter than outside.
