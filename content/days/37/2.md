---
title: Waters broken, labour not started
read: [waters-before-labour]
---
In about 1 in 12 pregnancies after 37 weeks, the waters break before labour starts. Most people go into labour on their own within a day or two, but once the membranes have broken, infection can reach you and the baby.

So there's a choice. RANZCOG recommends induction within 24 hours where practical, sooner with Group B strep; planned early birth leads to fewer infections, without more caesareans. Some choose to wait, with a clear plan for review, checking their temperature every 4 hours while awake.

While you wait, green, brown or blood-coloured fluid means calling your doctor, midwife or birthing unit immediately for advice, or going to hospital. Call your midwife, doctor or hospital immediately if the waters smell bad, or you bleed, have a temperature of 37.5°C or more, feel unwell, notice the baby's movements change, or have regular painful contractions. If you've been told the baby isn't head first, call triple zero (000).
