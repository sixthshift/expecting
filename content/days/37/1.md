---
title: When the waters break
read: [waters-breaking, card-waters-colour, card-waters-not-first]
---
For most people the waters break during labour, but for some they go before it starts. It may be a gush or a trickle, can feel like wetting yourself, usually isn't painful, and you may not feel it at all. The fluid is usually clear, but can be yellow, straw-coloured or pink.

What comes next is simple: a pad, a look at the colour, and a call to your midwife, doctor or birth centre, who'll tell you when to come in.

Green, brown or blood-coloured fluid means calling your doctor, midwife or birthing unit immediately for advice, or going to hospital. Call your midwife, doctor or hospital immediately, too, if the waters smell bad, or come with bleeding, a temperature of 37.5°C or more, feeling unwell, a change in the baby's movements, or regular painful contractions or belly pain. And if you've been told the baby isn't head first, call triple zero (000) for an ambulance.
