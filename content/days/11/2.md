---
title: Paracetamol, and asking first
read: [medicines-in-pregnancy, paracetamol]
---
It helps to know where you stand before the next headache or fever. Many medicines are safe in pregnancy; a small number can harm the baby. So the rule is to ask your doctor or pharmacist before you take any, over-the-counter and complementary ones too.

Paracetamol is the recommended treatment for pain or fever in pregnancy, category A in Australia. Untreated pain and fever can themselves pose risks to the baby. The dose is 2 × 500 mg tablets every 4 to 6 hours, no more than 8 in 24 hours, at the lowest effective dose for the shortest time, and not alongside other medicines that contain it, such as cold and flu tablets.

With a fever, the advice is plenty of fluids and rest, and to contact your doctor if it goes on. For a question at any hour, healthdirect (1800 022 222) puts you through to a registered nurse.
