---
title: When you can feel your heartbeat
read: [racing-heartbeat]
---
You may start noticing your own heart: pounding, sinking, skipping a beat, or fast and fluttering, in the chest or the neck. These are palpitations. They're common and usually not serious, and they can come with exercise, feeling unwell, anxiety or stress, caffeine, some medicines including asthma relievers or conditions such as anaemia, as well as heart problems.

If you know the cause isn't serious, sitting and resting somewhere quiet and loosening tight clothing can help, and noting what sets them off helps your doctor. If you've had palpitations that have passed, see your doctor so your heart can be checked; with a family history of heart rhythm problems, you need an urgent review.

But in pregnancy a racing heart is one of the signs to act on: contact your doctor, midwife or hospital immediately if your heart is racing or you're short of breath. With chest pain, shortness of breath, dizziness or light-headedness, or fainting, call 000.
