---
title: Boy or girl, and how you'd find out
read: [card-sex-myths]
---
People will have theories. The shape of the bump, a wedding ring swung over your tummy to see which way it turns, how active the baby is: you may hear all of them about now, and none of them are proven to work.

What does work, if you want to know, is a test. NIPT, the blood test available from 10 weeks, can reveal the baby's sex along with its screening results. The morphology scan, usually at 18 to 20 weeks, may be able to tell you too, depending on how the baby is lying.

Either way, it's only if you'd like to know.
