The 12-week mark is next week, and with it the question of who to tell. There is no right answer and no rule. Many people wait until the end of the first trimester because of the risk of miscarriage, and Pregnancy, Birth and Baby suggests the more useful question is how you'd handle one if it happened. Some people would be too distressed to talk about it; on the other hand, if people know, support may be there if it does happen.

Who hears, when and how is best decided together. Close family and friends may prefer to be told in person, and telling them first means support early. Announcing on social media gives you little control over who sees it, and can draw advice you didn't ask for. Friends who've lost a baby or are trying to get pregnant may find the news hard: telling them privately, before others, may help.
