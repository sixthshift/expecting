---
title: Who looks after you
read: [first-antenatal-visit]
---
"Antenatal" just means before birth, and the first antenatal visit is best before 10 weeks, ideally at about 6 to 8. Most people start with their GP, who confirms the pregnancy, can organise the routine blood tests, explains your options and gives you a referral to a maternity service or obstetrician.

The options are about who your main carer will be: a public hospital maternity team, a midwife or midwife group, shared care between different healthcare professionals, or an obstetrician. Your midwife or doctor will tell you about the tests and how much they will cost.

The GP isn't the only way in. You can make an appointment directly with a midwifery service in your area, and you may not need a referral if the midwife is part of a group practice or private practice. Your GP can tell you what's available locally.
