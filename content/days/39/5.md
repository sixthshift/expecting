---
title: Before you leave the hospital
read: [going-home]
---
Before you take the baby home, there's a list. For you: a health check, eating and drinking, passing urine, any pain managed, and moving and walking. For the baby: a health check and a feeding plan, vitamin K and hepatitis B considered, the birth registration paperwork, and their health record book given and explained. Then transport and a car seat, and safe sleep talked through.

If you go home the same day or have a home birth, a midwife will come to you, and your midwife may arrange a home visit anyway. Then your care passes to a child health nurse, first seen about 10 days after you get home, who checks on you both and records the baby's weight and measurements.

Some parents keep nappies, wipes and spare clothes within easy reach and freeze a few meals beforehand, and a friend or relative staying a few days can help. It's fine to limit visitors.
