---
title: The first checks
read: [newborn-checks, card-apgar-is-quick]
---
The baby's first check comes a minute after the birth. The Apgar score rates heart rate, breathing, colour, muscle tone and reflexes at 1 and 5 minutes, sometimes 10, each from 0 to 2. Blue hands and feet are normal in a newborn, which is why the top score is usually 9, and 7 or more at 5 minutes shows the baby is coping well; a lower score means extra help, most likely with breathing. It reflects how the baby is at birth, and isn't meant to predict how their brain will develop.

The baby is weighed and measured, and the numbers go on growth charts. In the first days come a hearing screen and the heel prick test, each with your permission first.
