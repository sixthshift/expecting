---
title: How long you'll stay
read: [hospital-stay, card-home-in-four-hours]
---
It might be a few hours or a few days. After an uncomplicated vaginal birth in a public hospital or birth centre, you may be able to go home 4 to 6 hours later, if you and the baby are well. After a caesarean it's between 24 and 72 hours. An assisted birth, or you or the baby being unwell, can mean longer, and private hospitals generally allow a longer stay than public ones.

Going home early from a public hospital usually means midwives visit you at home, with care for you both and breastfeeding advice; most public hospitals offer at least one home visit, then hand over to your child health nurse and local doctor.

Stays vary by hospital, and so do visiting hours, so it's worth asking yours what to expect.
