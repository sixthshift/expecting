---
title: Why whooping cough, why now
read: [whooping-cough-vaccine]
---
Whooping cough can be very serious, even deadly, for young babies, and they can't have their own first dose until they're 6 weeks old. The vaccine in pregnancy covers that gap. Your antibodies cross the placenta, so the baby has some protection from birth until their own vaccines start.

It's recommended between 20 and 32 weeks of every pregnancy, a window that opened last week, though it can be given up until the birth. It's free under the National Immunisation Program, though some providers charge a consultation fee, and it can be given at the same visit as the flu and RSV vaccines.
