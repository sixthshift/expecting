---
title: Dreams, and very strange ones
read: [vivid-dreams, card-vivid-dreams]
---
You may be dreaming more than you normally would, and the dreams can be unusual, or nightmares. Sometimes that's related to stress, or to sleep that has become inconsistent.

They tend to get stranger from here. From about week 26 and into the third trimester, vivid dreams are common, due to pregnancy hormones and sleep that's more disturbed. You're also more likely to remember them, because you're waking more often, to go to the toilet for one.

A regular sleep schedule, different sleep positions or a pregnancy pillow may help. And if dreams are disturbing you, it can help to talk to the partner or a friend, or to consider seeing a counsellor; if you feel low, worried or on edge most of the time, see your doctor or midwife.
