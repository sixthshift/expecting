---
title: The seatbelt, with a bump
read: [seatbelt-position]
---
The seatbelt still goes on, the pregnancy way: the lap belt over your hips and under the bump, and the shoulder sash above the bump and between your breasts. Not wearing it correctly raises the risk of being injured, and a blow to the abdomen can make the placenta separate from the uterus. Moving the seat well back, as far from the dashboard or wheel as is safe, reduces an airbag's impact.

After any accident, however minor, see a doctor or midwife as soon as possible, even if you feel fine; if you're RhD negative you may need an anti-D injection. After any injury to your abdomen, go to the nearest hospital and tell them you're pregnant, straight away if the baby is moving less or you have bleeding or pain. With abdominal pain, a firm or tightening uterus or bleeding after an injury, call 000, ask for an ambulance and tell them you're pregnant.
