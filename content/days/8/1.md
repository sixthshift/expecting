---
title: When it's more than morning sickness
read: [severe-vomiting]
---
Most people have some nausea and vomiting in pregnancy, and with ordinary morning sickness you can usually still eat and drink enough. Hyperemesis gravidarum (HG) is more severe and doesn't let up. It makes it hard to eat and drink enough, and can lead to dehydration, weight loss, low levels of vitamins and minerals, depression and anxiety. It's a serious medical condition, and there is treatment, including medicines: you deserve to be heard and to get it.

If you can't keep fluids down at all, go to your local emergency department; they can give you IV fluids. Contact your doctor, midwife or hospital immediately if you have severe nausea or vomiting.

Contact your midwife or doctor if you're vomiting often or can't stop, if ongoing nausea makes it hard to eat and drink enough, or if you're losing weight.
