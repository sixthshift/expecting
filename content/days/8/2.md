title: A workout on the couch

---
To meet the baby's growing demand for blood, your heart enlarges and pumps faster, and the volume of your blood rises as the baby grows. The Royal Women's Hospital says you're already doing an aerobic workout just by being pregnant. It also narrows the range between your resting heart rate and a safe maximum, which is why exercise is best kept to a moderate intensity.

You need more iron in pregnancy as well. Your haemoglobin is checked with a blood test at your first visit, and usually again at about 28 weeks.
