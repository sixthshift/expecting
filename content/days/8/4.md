---
title: More discharge than usual
read: [vaginal-discharge]
---
More discharge than usual is normal in pregnancy. It's called leucorrhoea and comes from higher levels of oestrogen and progesterone. It should look like your usual discharge, just more of it: clear or white, without a strong smell.

See your doctor if it's unusual for you, as it may be an infection: if it looks green, yellow, brown or grey, smells bad, is thick and lumpy like cottage cheese, thin and runny, or frothy, or comes with itching or pain. Thrush and bacterial vaginosis (BV) are both more likely in pregnancy, and untreated BV can lead to premature birth or miscarriage, so getting the right treatment matters. Your doctor may do a swab and prescribe treatment.

Contact your doctor or midwife immediately if you have any bleeding or fluid leaking from your vagina.
