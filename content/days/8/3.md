---
title: A cold, or the flu
read: [cold-or-flu, cold-and-flu-tablets]
---
A cold isn't dangerous for you or the baby and usually gets better on its own in a few days. The flu is more serious: in pregnancy you're more likely to catch it and to become seriously unwell, especially in the second and third trimesters. If you think you have the flu, see a doctor when you first notice symptoms, because an antiviral works better the earlier you take it.

For a cold, the usual box of cold and flu tablets is best left on the shelf. Many contain an oral decongestant, which isn't recommended in pregnancy, and some contain paracetamol, which can take you past the daily limit if you're already taking it. It's better to treat each symptom on its own: paracetamol for aches, a saline spray for your nose, warm drinks with lemon and honey for your throat.

If you're not sure what's safe, ask your doctor or pharmacist.
