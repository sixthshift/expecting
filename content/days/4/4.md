---
title: Drinks before you knew
read: [alcohol-before-you-knew]
---
Plenty of people drink before they know they're pregnant. MotherSafe says the risk to the baby is likely to be low if only small amounts were drunk before you knew. The risk goes up with frequent drinking and large amounts over a short time, so binge drinking may be especially harmful, and chronic heavy drinking raises the risk of fetal alcohol spectrum disorder (FASD).

Once you know, it's safest to stop, and stopping at any time in the pregnancy reduces the risk to the baby.

If you're worried, say so to your doctor or midwife. NOFASD Australia gives confidential information and support on 1800 860 613, and in New South Wales MotherSafe can talk through your own situation on 1800 647 848, or (02) 9382 6539 in Sydney. If it's hard to stop, the Alcohol and Drug Information Service is a 24-hour confidential line on 1800 250 015.
