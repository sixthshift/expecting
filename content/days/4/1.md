---
title: Implantation
read: [term-implantation, term-blastocyst]
---
By the end of this week the blastocyst, the fertilised egg in its layers of cells, has travelled down the fallopian tube and implants itself in the wall of the uterus. Those layers go on to form different parts of the baby and the placenta.

Implantation is the point conception is complete. If it doesn't happen, a pregnancy doesn't occur: your hormones decrease and the lining of your uterus sheds as your period. If it does, the placenta starts forming at the same time, and keeps developing right through the pregnancy.

So by the time a period would be due, the embryo has implanted, and the placenta has already begun.
