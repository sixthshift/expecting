---
title: An organ you grow, then give birth to
read: [card-you-grow-an-organ]
---
Something else begins with this pregnancy: a whole new organ. The placenta is temporary. It begins developing when you first become pregnant, attaches to the lining of your uterus, and delivers oxygen and nutrients to the baby through the umbilical cord. It also makes the hormones needed for pregnancy, for the birth and for breastfeeding.

Once the baby is born, it's no longer needed. It's pushed out naturally, or with help from your midwife: that's the third stage of labour, also called the afterbirth.

So there are two births at the end of this, really: the baby, and then the organ you grew to keep them going.
