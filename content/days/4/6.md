---
title: What the test is looking for
read: [term-hcg, confirming-the-pregnancy]
---
A pregnancy test looks for hCG, a hormone made by the placenta, in your urine or blood. Home tests usually detect it about 2 weeks after conception, and most can pick it up on the day your period is due. For a more reliable result, test first thing in the morning, when there's more hCG in your urine, and check the expiry date. If it's negative and your period still hasn't come, test again in a few days.

The hormone keeps rising: hCG is highest towards the end of the first trimester, then decreases.

Home tests aren't always correct, so after a positive one, see your doctor or midwife. They'll confirm it with a blood or urine test; a blood test is more sensitive than a home test, and a positive one almost always means you're pregnant.
