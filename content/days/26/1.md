---
title: The bloods around 28 weeks
read: [third-trimester-bloods, full-blood-count]
---
Many blood tests are done at your first visit, but a few come round again near the third trimester. As with every test, the choice is yours, and your doctor or midwife should explain why each one is recommended.

A full blood count, at about 26 to 28 weeks, checks your haemoglobin again for anaemia, and your platelets. Anaemia happens in about 1 in 4 pregnancies, mostly from low iron, because your blood volume increases and your body needs extra iron. Screening for syphilis is recommended again between 26 and 28 weeks: untreated it can lead to miscarriage or stillbirth, and caught early it's easy to treat with antibiotics.

The glucose tolerance test may fall around the same time. And if your blood group is RhD negative, there may be more tests relating to it.
