---
title: Getting the pets ready
read: [pets-and-the-baby, card-pets-need-prep]
---
Animals are creatures of routine, and it can be hard to predict how one will react when a baby comes home. Planning helps: changes to their feeding and care are easier made before the baby arrives, and pet care shared between you where possible.

For a dog, obedience training and "recall" practice, so they come immediately when called, are the place to start. Then come walks on the lead beside the pram, at the same pace, with the lead never tied to the pram; a supervised look and sniff at the bassinet, pram and change table; and rooms that are out of bounds, with baby gates if needed. Playing recordings of a baby crying, slowly louder, can help too.

A vet check during the pregnancy is worth it as well, since pets can pass on infections and parasites.
