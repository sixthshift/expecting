---
title: What the classes cover
read: [antenatal-classes]
---
Antenatal classes, or childbirth education, cover labour, pain relief options, birth, feeding and the first days with a newborn. A course around 28 weeks is the usual timing, so they're coming up soon, and they often fill up quickly.

They're for both of you: a birth support partner can come along, and the classes help prepare you both for the birth. You'll also meet other people expecting babies around the same time.

Your midwife, obstetrician or GP can tell you about the classes at your hospital or birth centre. Costs vary: Medicare doesn't cover them, but some hospitals run free classes, and private health insurance may pay something towards them.
