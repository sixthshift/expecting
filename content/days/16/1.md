---
title: Waiting for the first kick
read: [not-felt-baby-move-yet, card-second-pregnancy-shows-earlier]
---
Movements usually start to be felt by about 20 weeks, earlier if this isn't your first pregnancy, perhaps because you know the feeling and what to look out for. So if there's nothing yet, there's time.

The first ones are easy to miss: fluttering, like butterflies, or bubbling, swishing, rolling or tiny kicks. They get more noticeable as the weeks go on, and by about 28 weeks they should be regular.

If you haven't felt the baby move by 24 weeks, contact your doctor or midwife. Once you do feel them, and at any point after, if the movements slow down or you're concerned about their pattern, contact your midwife, hospital birth unit or doctor immediately; don't wait until the next day. If you can't reach them, go to your nearest hospital birthing or emergency unit.
