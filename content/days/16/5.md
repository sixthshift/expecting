---
title: A job that gets harder as you grow
read: [working-while-pregnant, heavy-lifting]
---
What wasn't a risk at work early on can become one later. That's why your employer's risk assessment isn't a one-off: it's repeated at different stages of the pregnancy, or if your work changes.

Heavy lifting is the classic one. Pregnancy hormones loosen your ligaments, which makes injuries more likely, and none of the Australian sources gives a weight limit: SafeWork NSW's rule is that if something seems too heavy, or you feel dizzy or faint, ask for help or have someone else do it.

If your usual job isn't safe, every pregnant employee, casuals included, can move to a safe job at the same pay and hours, and if there isn't one, you may get no safe job leave. And if a task makes you dizzy, sore or unwell, or you're not sure it's safe, the advice is to stop and get advice from your doctor or midwife.
