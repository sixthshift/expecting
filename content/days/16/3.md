---
title: About 14 centimetres
read: [baby-size-and-growth]
---
At 16 weeks the baby is about 14 cm long, and by 20 weeks about 21 cm. Every baby grows at their own pace, so any size for a week is only an average, and the fruit in each week's note is a picture, not a measurement.

Size gets checked in two ways. Early on, the sonographer measured from the baby's head to bottom to date the pregnancy; later scans measure the head, the thigh bone and the abdomen to estimate size. And from 24 weeks your doctor or midwife may measure your bump at each visit, from your pubic bone to the top of your uterus. In centimetres it should be about the number of weeks you're pregnant.

If it's smaller or larger than expected, your doctor will monitor you and the baby carefully. If you notice your tummy isn't growing as you expected and you're worried, speak with your doctor or midwife.
