---
title: A back carrying more
read: [posture-and-back-care, back-pain]
---
Backache affects up to 7 in 10 pregnant people, especially in the third trimester. Its causes build from now: hormones loosen the ligaments around your lower back and pelvis, and the growing bump drags your spine forward.

Posture helps: standing and sitting tall, not slumping, and drawing your tummy muscles in around the baby when you walk or lift. It feels like hard work at first, but should lessen the pain.

Call 000 for back pain with tummy pains or cramps, pressure in your pelvis or lower back, or fluid leaking (signs of preterm labour); with lost feeling, weakness or tingling in your limbs, or trouble controlling your bladder or bowels; or with very heavy bleeding, strong pain or feeling very unwell. For back pain with any vaginal bleeding, contact your doctor, midwife or hospital immediately. See them about pain that disrupts your day, is new to you, or comes with painful urination or blood in your urine.
