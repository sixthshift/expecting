---
title: The first vaccine, on day one
read: [hepatitis-b-birth-dose]
---
The baby's first vaccine comes on their first day or so: hepatitis B, free for every infant. It's the first of 4 doses, with the others at 2, 4 and 6 months as part of a combined vaccine. The birth dose is given once the baby is medically stable, preferably within 24 hours of birth, though it can be given within the first 7 days. Missed by then, it isn't caught up; the baby has a 3-dose course instead.

It's for every newborn, not only babies of a parent with hepatitis B: it allows for delays or errors in testing in pregnancy, and protects against infection in the first months from household members or close contacts who have it. Newborns tolerate it very well, and it can be given at the same time as vitamin K.

If you're known to have chronic hepatitis B, the baby must also have hepatitis B immunoglobulin, on the day of birth.
