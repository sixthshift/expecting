---
title: The things people suggest
read: [overdue, raspberry-leaf]
---
Waiting, you'll hear plenty of ways to bring labour on: nipple stimulation, castor oil, walking, sex, spicy food, hypnosis, acupuncture. The evidence for all of them is anecdotal, there isn't enough to show they work, and the advice is to talk to your doctor or midwife before trying any.

Raspberry leaf tea is the other one. It's likely safe in pregnancy and while breastfeeding, but no study has found it helpful. As with any herbal tea, it's a good idea to check with your doctor or pharmacist first, and the general advice is no more than 2 cups a day.

What does happen, if labour doesn't come, is what you're offered: closer checks, perhaps a sweep, then an induction, usually offered somewhere between 41 and 42 weeks. And through the waiting, tell your midwife or doctor straight away if the baby's movements suddenly speed up, slow down or stop.
