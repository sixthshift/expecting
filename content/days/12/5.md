---
title: If you keep forgetting things
read: [brain-fog, card-brain-fog]
---
Walked into a room and forgotten why? "Pregnancy brain" is a real thing. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby lists changes to brain function and memory among the symptoms caused by the hormonal changes of pregnancy, in the same list as nausea, food cravings and constipation. It's sometimes called brain fog.

It's most noticeable in the third trimester, so if it hasn't arrived yet, it may still come, and if it has, you're in good company.

Tiredness is worth telling apart. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby lists reduced concentration and alertness among the possible complications of severe or ongoing tiredness, and that's the point to act on: if you're exhausted and more rest doesn't help, see your doctor or midwife.
