---
title: Hearing the heartbeat
read: [the-babys-heartbeat, fetal-heartbeat-check, card-home-dopplers]
---
The baby's heart has been beating since about week 7, and from about 12 weeks your doctor or midwife may be able to listen to it through your tummy, with a handheld Doppler or a Pinard horn, an ear trumpet. They're listening for whether it's too fast or too slow, responding as expected, and showing the right pattern for the baby's age.

It's natural to want to hear it at home too. Home dopplers aren't approved for sale in Australia and aren't recommended: only trained professionals can tell what they're hearing, and at home they can falsely reassure you when the baby isn't well, which can delay urgent care and has been linked to serious harm, including baby deaths.

If you're ever worried about the baby's movements, contact your midwife or doctor immediately; don't wait until the next day. If you can't reach them, go to your nearest hospital birthing or emergency unit.
