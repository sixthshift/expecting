---
title: If a result says higher chance
read: [high-chance-result, cvs]
---
Screening results may come back around now. If one says higher chance, the first thing to know is what that means: a screening test estimates the chance of a condition such as Down syndrome. It can't diagnose one. A high-chance result means a difference is more likely, not that it's there.

Your doctor or midwife will talk you through what comes next. You'll be offered genetic counselling, to understand what each test can and can't tell you, and a diagnostic test that can say for certain. One is CVS, which takes a very small sample of the placenta, usually between 11 weeks and 13 weeks 6 days; the other is amniocentesis, from 15 weeks. Both carry a small risk of miscarriage.

Every step is your choice, and there's no single right decision. Worry is common here. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby's maternal child health nurses are on 1800 882 436, 7am to midnight, every day.
