---
title: What happens at every visit
read: [what-happens-at-each-visit, antenatal-visit-schedule]
---
From around now the appointments settle into a pattern, and each one has the same check at its heart: your blood pressure, how you are in general, the baby's heartbeat (possibly from now on), a talk about the baby's movements and, from 20 weeks, a measure of your tummy to follow the baby's growth.

For a first baby with no problems, expect about 10 appointments; about 7 if you've had uncomplicated pregnancies before. More if anything complicates, and you can always see your doctor or midwife between times with a problem or a worry. Scans and extra tests come at set points: the ultrasound at 18 to 22 weeks, a glucose test at 24 to 28, blood tests at 28.

They're also the place for your questions, about symptoms, the birth or the baby, and for help with diet, mental health or problems at home.
