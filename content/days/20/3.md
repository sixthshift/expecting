---
title: What the first movements feel like
read: [baby-movements]
---
The first movements you feel have an old name, quickening, and people describe them as fluttering, like butterflies, or bubbling, swishing, rolling, tumbling or tiny kicks. Most people feel them by about now, between 16 and 24 weeks. With a placenta at the front (an anterior placenta, which the scan will mention), they may come later and feel softer.

They become more noticeable as the weeks go on, and by about 28 weeks they settle into a pattern. There's no number of kicks to count; what matters is getting to know this baby's own pattern. If it slows, weakens or stops, contact the midwife or birth unit straight away, not tomorrow.
