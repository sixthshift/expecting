---
title: Getting to know the movements
read: [baby-movements]
---
First movements usually show up somewhere between 16 and 24 weeks: flutters, swishes, tiny kicks. By about 28 weeks they become regular, and babies keep moving every day right up to the birth.

What surprises people is what matters about them. There's no set number of kicks to count, and kick charts are no longer recommended; what matters is getting to know this baby's own pattern. If the movements slow, weaken or stop, the advice is to contact the midwife or birth unit straight away, not tomorrow. You are not wasting their time.

Two things that don't help: home dopplers and apps can't tell you the baby is well, and eating or drinking something to get the baby moving doesn't work.
