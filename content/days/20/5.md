---
title: What the baby can hear
read: [the-babys-senses]
---
Hearing is developing now, from about 15 weeks to 24, and it starts on the inside: your heartbeat, your digestion, your voice. By 24 weeks sounds from outside the uterus are getting through too, and by about 25 hearing is fully working, when the baby may react to noise and be soothed by your voice.

So talk to them. Talking, singing, playing calming music and gently rubbing the bump all count, and the partner can do every one of them. It can feel silly at first; you get used to it.
