---
title: What the scan is looking at
read: [morphology-scan]
---
The morphology scan is a detailed ultrasound, sometimes called the fetal anomaly scan, and it is thorough: the sonographer works through the baby's head and brain, spine, heart, abdomen, arms, legs, hands and feet, then your placenta, the umbilical cord and the amount of amniotic fluid around the baby.

The partner can come along. Depending on the baby's position, it may be able to tell you the sex, if you want to know. If anything needs a closer look, you'll be referred for further testing, such as another ultrasound or a diagnostic test.
