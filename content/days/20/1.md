---
title: What the scan is looking at
read: [morphology-scan]
---
The morphology scan is a detailed ultrasound, sometimes called the fetal anomaly scan, and it is thorough: the sonographer works through the baby's head and brain, spine, heart, abdomen, arms, legs, hands and feet, then your placenta, the umbilical cord and the amount of amniotic fluid around the baby. It can take a while, and the baby's position decides how easy it is.

If you want to know the sex, it can often tell you then; if you'd rather not know, say so at the start. If anything needs a closer look, you'll be referred for further testing, such as another ultrasound or a diagnostic test.
