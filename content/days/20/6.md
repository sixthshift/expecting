---
title: Why your skin is darker
read: [skin-darkening, linea-nigra]
---
Pregnancy hormones make the skin produce more melanin, so anywhere already pigmented can darken: moles, freckles, the areolas, armpits, inner thighs. A dark line can run from the belly button down, the linea nigra, and brown patches on the forehead, cheeks, nose and upper lip (melasma) are very common, usually from the second or third trimester.

Neither causes any problems for you or the baby, and both are likely to fade after the birth. Sun makes both darker, so SPF50+ and a broad-brimmed hat help. Check with your doctor or pharmacist before any treatment: creams with hydroquinone or tretinoin may harm the baby. Show your doctor or midwife any brown patch that isn't on your face.
