---
title: Why your skin is darker
read: [skin-darkening, linea-nigra]
---
Pregnancy hormones make the skin produce more melanin, so anywhere already pigmented can darken: moles, freckles, the areolas, armpits, inner thighs. Many people get a dark line from the belly button down, the linea nigra, and some get brown patches on the forehead, cheeks, nose and upper lip (melasma), usually from the second trimester.

It's harmless and likely to fade after the birth. Sun makes both darker, so SPF50+ and a broad-brimmed hat help. Check with your doctor or pharmacist before any treatment: creams with hydroquinone or tretinoin may harm the baby. Show your doctor or midwife any brown patch that isn't on your face.
