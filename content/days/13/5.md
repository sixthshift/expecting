---
title: Telling work
read: [telling-your-employer, pregnancy-discrimination-at-work]
---
No law says you have to tell your employer by a particular time, though your award, agreement or contract may have its own rules, and it's a good idea to tell them before they hear it from someone else. The deadline that does exist is later: at least 10 weeks' written notice before parental leave starts.

Once they know, your employer has obligations: assessing the safety risks of your job to the pregnancy, moving you to a safe role if needed, and discussing more suitable hours.

You can't be fired, demoted or treated differently because you're pregnant. Under the Fair Work Act pregnancy is a protected attribute, and even an employer who thinks they're helping, say by cutting your hours, can be discriminating. The Fair Work Ombudsman, on 13 13 94, can tell you your rights. If you're ever dismissed, you have 21 days to apply to the Fair Work Commission.
