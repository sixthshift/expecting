---
title: The middle of it
read: [term-second-trimester, how-your-body-changes]
---
The second trimester is the middle part of pregnancy, from about week 13 to week 26, and its changes have two drivers: hormones, and the size and pressure of the growing baby.

Nausea and vomiting usually improve by around week 14, though if it's severe you may need to go to hospital. Hormones soften your ligaments, most noticeably in the pelvis and lower back, which can mean back pain and feeling unsteady; a physiotherapist can teach exercises that strengthen your muscles. Feet and ankles may swell, especially after long standing or in hot weather; swelling early in the day, or in your fingers, hands or face, is one to speak to your midwife or doctor about. Breasts get larger, heavier and more tender; significant breast pain, redness or a lump is one to raise with your midwife or doctor.

If you're ever unsure whether a change is normal or something to worry about, speak to your doctor.
