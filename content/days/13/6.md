title: Everyone has an opinion
read: [everyone-has-advice, grandparents-and-boundaries]
---
Once people know, the advice starts: family, friends, and even strangers. Most of it comes from people who want to feel useful or supportive, and some of it is helpful. Some of it is overwhelming or conflicting, and it can chip away at your confidence.

There are easy ways out. Deflect with humour, say "thanks", give a nod and move on, or ask the person to help with something instead. If you need to be firmer, it helps to have a couple of sentences ready and practised, thanking them for caring and saying you'll work it out. The partner can be your advocate.

It's fine to put limits on family help if it feels like they're taking over, and to explain gently that some decisions are only for the two of you to make. Whose advice fits is something you'll work out as you go.
