---
title: Eating a bit more, and wanting odd things
read: [how-much-more-to-eat, card-eating-for-two]

You don't need to eat for two. In the first 3 months you probably didn't need many extra kilojoules, but from the second trimester you'll need a little more: Pregnancy, Birth and Baby's version is a bit more vegetables, lean protein and wholegrains every day. Larger meals can get hard to manage, so smaller meals and healthy snacks through the day may suit you better.

This is also when cravings tend to arrive. Up to 9 in 10 pregnant people get at least one, mostly in the second trimester, and regular healthy meals, water and balanced snacks in the pantry help.

If aversions or appetite changes stop you getting enough iron, folate and iodine, talk to your doctor or maternal health nurse. And if you crave things that aren't food, such as soap, clay, ice or chalk, speak to your midwife or doctor: it's called pica, and it's been linked to low iron.
