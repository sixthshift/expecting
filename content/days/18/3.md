---
title: For the partner, being there
read: [partner-going-to-appointments]
---
Pregnancy, Birth and Baby's first suggestion for partners is simple: offer to go to the appointments. The scan is the obvious one, but the ordinary check-ups count too. Later they're a chance to listen to the baby's heartbeat and learn what will happen at the birth, alongside antenatal classes together and a tour of the place the baby will be born. Its other suggestions are doing their own reading and sharing what they learn, and talking about how you're both feeling.

Time off is the practical part. You're entitled to time off for check-ups, scans and tests. The partner's own leave, to be there for the birth, depends on their award, agreement or contract.

It's common for partners to feel helpless when you're uncomfortable or in pain, or unsure about parenting, and to believe they should be "the strong one". The advice for them is to look after their own health and speak up before feelings build.
