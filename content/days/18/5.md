title: The first nappy, already loading
read: [card-first-poo-is-already-made, term-meconium]

Here's something few people expect at the halfway mark: the baby's first poo is already being made. From about now, the bowel is filling with meconium, which will become their first few poos after the birth: sticky, thick and dark green, in the first few days. It's the first nappy you'll change, and it has been loading since week 18.

Sometimes meconium is passed before birth, such as during labour. It turns the amniotic fluid green, and since it may be a sign the baby is in distress, they'll be monitored more closely.

That's why the colour of the waters matters when they break. If the fluid is green, brown or blood coloured, call your doctor, midwife or birthing unit immediately for advice, or go to hospital.
