---
title: Questions for the scan
read: [questions-to-ask-at-appointments]
---
The morphology scan is close, and like every test and scan, it's one you can ask about. Your doctor or midwife is there to support you, so don't be afraid to ask.

The useful questions are the same for any test. Is it routine, and why might I need it? What are the benefits and the risks? Is there an alternative, and do I have to have it? When will I get the results, what happens after them, and what are the chances of a result being wrong? Is there anything to do to prepare? Are there any costs?

It's your right to be fully informed about any test you're offered and to decide whether you have it: that's informed consent. And it's a good idea for the partner to come along.
