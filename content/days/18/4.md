---
title: How much weight
read: [weight-gain-in-pregnancy]
---
How much weight to gain depends on where you started. The guide ranges go by your BMI before pregnancy: for a BMI of 18.5 to 24.9, a total of 11.5 to 16 kg, about 1.5 to 2.3 kg a month through the second and third trimesters; more if you started below that, less if above. The Royal Women's gives lower BMI cut-offs if you're of Asian background. They're a guide only: your midwife, doctor or dietitian may recommend something different for you, and your gain is checked at your visits.

Not all of it is the baby. Your body builds extra tissue and holds more fluid, including the amniotic fluid, the placenta and a larger volume of blood.

Other things change it too: morning sickness, having twins or more, changes in appetite, and fluid retention, especially later in pregnancy. And BMI doesn't necessarily show your health. If you're concerned about your weight gain, talk to your doctor or midwife.
