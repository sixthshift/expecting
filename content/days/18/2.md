---
title: What the scan costs
read: [paying-for-scans]
---
Medicare helps with the cost of routine pregnancy scans, the morphology scan at 18 to 22 weeks among them, but it doesn't always pay the whole bill. If the practice bulk bills, you pay nothing. If it charges more than Medicare covers, Medicare pays part and you pay the rest, the gap. Whether there's a gap, and how much, depends on where you go.

Extra scans are usually only done if they're needed, such as for a complication or worries about the baby's movements. 3D and 4D keepsake scans are a choice some people make for non-medical reasons; healthdirect suggests discussing them with your obstetrician or GP.

If costs add up, the Medicare Safety Nets can lower your out-of-pocket costs for out-of-hospital services once you reach a threshold, and couples can combine their costs by registering as a family.
