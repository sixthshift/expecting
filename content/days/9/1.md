---
title: If there's bleeding after sex
read: [bleeding-after-sex]
---
Sometimes there's a little bleeding after sex, and it can be harmless. One cause is cervical ectropion, where the cells on the surface of the cervix change and bleed more easily, for example after sex; it doesn't affect the pregnancy. But bleeding has other causes too, so it needs checking: contact your doctor or midwife, and note how much you bled and its colour. Your doctor might advise no sex for a while, even once it has stopped. Speak to your doctor, too, if there are also severe cramps, unusual discharge, sharp pain or fever after sex.

Call 000 and ask for an ambulance for heavy bleeding (soaking a pad or more an hour, passing clots, or you can't contain it), strong pain, or feeling very unwell. Contact your doctor urgently or go to the nearest emergency department for strong pain in your tummy or shoulder, feeling dizzy or faint, shortness of breath, or discharge that smells bad.
