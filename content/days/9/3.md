---
title: Herbal tea, two cups
read: [herbal-tea, peppermint-tea, chamomile-tea]
---
When coffee stops appealing, herbal tea is often what takes its place. It has no caffeine, but it is a form of herbal medicine, and natural doesn't necessarily mean safe: the amount of each herb varies, a tea may hold ingredients that aren't on the label, and what's in it can cross the placenta or interfere with other medicines. Large amounts of some herbs could harm the baby or trigger contractions.

So the advice is moderation: no more than 2 cups a day, mixing up the types so the baby gets less of any one herb, and checking with your doctor or pharmacist first, especially if you take prescription medicine. Peppermint is one of the most used, can ease nausea and wind, and studies show it doesn't harm you or the baby; just avoid very large amounts.

Chamomile is less certain. There isn't enough information to know it's safe, so it's best not drunk in large quantities.
