---
title: When the cord is cut
read: [delayed-cord-clamping, card-cord-waits]
---
In the third stage the cord is clamped and cut, and that doesn't hurt you or the baby. It usually happens after the cord has stopped pulsing, about a minute after the birth, which lets the baby receive more blood from the placenta and may lower the chance of iron deficiency later. If you or the baby is unwell at birth, it may need to be cut straight away.

If you'd like it left connected for longer, or the partner wants to cut it, that's something for the midwife to know in advance, and for your birth preferences.

One thing doesn't go with waiting: cord blood banking needs the cord clamped early, to collect as many stem cells as possible, so you can't have both. If you were hoping for both, it's one to talk through with your midwife.
