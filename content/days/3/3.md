---
title: The brain and spine come first
read: [term-neural-tube-defect]
---
The neural tube, which becomes the baby's brain and spinal cord, forms in the first 4 weeks of pregnancy, often before you know you're pregnant. A neural tube defect is when it doesn't fully close. Spina bifida is one, where the backbone doesn't fully close and leaves part of the spinal cord exposed; anencephaly and encephalocele are others.

That's why folic acid matters so early. Taking it while planning and for the first 3 months can reduce the risk by 70%, and it's in these weeks, now, that the tube is closing. Most of these defects can be diagnosed on the ultrasound at around 18 to 20 weeks.
