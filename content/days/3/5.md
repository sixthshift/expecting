---
title: No safe time to drink
read: [alcohol]
---
From this week a pregnancy may have begun, and nobody knows it yet. Australia's guidelines are that anyone pregnant or planning a pregnancy should not drink alcohol, to prevent harm to the baby. You'll hear that one glass is fine; the guidelines don't say that.

When you drink, alcohol passes from your blood through the placenta, and the placenta can't stop it reaching the baby. There's no safe time, no safe amount and no safe type of alcohol in pregnancy, and the more you drink, the more unsafe it is. Drinking while pregnant increases the chance of miscarriage, stillbirth, premature birth and fetal alcohol spectrum disorder (FASD), whose effects are life-long.

The partner stopping or cutting down too is a real support, and a non-alcoholic drink is an easy swap. If stopping is hard, talk to your doctor or midwife.
