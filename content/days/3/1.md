---
title: Conception, and the journey after
read: [conception-to-embryo]
---
Conception is when an egg is fertilised by a sperm, usually 1 or 2 days after you ovulate. Over the next 4 to 5 days the fertilised egg travels along the fallopian tube to the uterus, dividing as it goes: a zygote, then 2, 4 and 8 cells, then a blastocyst. When it implants in the wall of the uterus, conception is complete and the pregnancy begins. If it doesn't implant, your hormones fall, the lining sheds and you get your period.

What grows from it is more than a baby. The embryo becomes your baby, and alongside it come the placenta, which feeds the baby with nutrients and oxygen from your blood, the cord that links them, and the amniotic sac that protects the baby.

Once the embryo implants, the placenta starts making hCG, and a home pregnancy test usually detects it about 2 weeks after conception.
