---
title: The hormones start first
read: [term-progesterone, pregnancy-hormones]
---
As soon as you conceive, progesterone rises, and it's what stops you having a period. It's important for the baby's growth, and once the embryo implants your body makes it with the help of hCG, the hormone the placenta makes and a pregnancy test detects.

Hormonal changes lead to physical symptoms and can affect your mood. They can cause nausea and vomiting, cravings and constipation, and they soften the ligaments, which can affect your pelvis, lower back and balance.

Little of it shows this week, but it has begun, and hCG keeps climbing: it's highest towards the end of the first trimester, then gradually decreases.
