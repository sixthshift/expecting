---
title: Yoga and Pilates, adjusted
read: [yoga, pilates]
---
Yoga and Pilates both build strength, and both have been shown to improve mental health through focused breathing and mindfulness. The way in is a class designed for pregnancy, or an instructor who knows you're pregnant.

Some poses stop suiting now. Lying on your back is out after the first trimester: the baby's weight may press on your blood vessels, which can cut off supply to the baby or make you feel faint. Hot or humid rooms are out too. Relaxed ligaments make a pushed stretch an easy injury, so warming up and down matter. Your centre of gravity is changing, so moves that could unbalance you, make you jump and bounce, or change direction suddenly aren't for now; walking lunges and wide squats aren't recommended.

Pain or discomfort is the sign to stop. And if a bulge develops down the middle of your tummy, abdominal exercises are best avoided.
