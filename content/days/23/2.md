title: Who does what
read: [arguments-and-stress]

A baby changes every relationship in some way, and it's normal to argue now and then. One way to take the heat out of what's coming is to talk early about the things that tend to cause friction: how you'll share the household tasks now and after the birth, money worries, how you'll make time for yourselves and each other, your parenting styles, and how you each feel about what's ahead.

Pick a time when you're both calm and won't be interrupted, and it's fine to stop and come back to it. Say it from your side ("I feel" rather than "you always"), listen to understand rather than to fix, stick to the one problem, and agree a next step together.

Conflict isn't the verdict on a relationship; the repair afterwards matters most. Say what you appreciate about each other. And if it becomes a real struggle, Relationships Australia is on 1300 364 277.
