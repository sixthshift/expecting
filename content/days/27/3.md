---
title: Hiccups, and practice breathing
read: [card-baby-hiccups, the-babys-lungs]

That small, rhythmic twitch may be hiccups. The baby has been able to hiccup since about 18 weeks: it's the diaphragm contracting, and it's normal.

The baby is also practising breathing, though they get all their oxygen from the placenta until birth, and the amniotic fluid they swallow and pass back out helps their lungs develop. Somewhere between weeks 23 and 28 the lungs start making surfactant, the substance that lets them stay inflated once they're breathing air, and by week 28 they're mature enough to breathe air. They're the last thing to be ready: the lungs are fully mature by week 37, when the baby is considered full term.
