---
title: Hiccups, and practice breathing
read: [card-baby-hiccups, the-babys-lungs]
---
That small, rhythmic twitch may be hiccups. The baby has been able to hiccup since about 18 weeks: it's the diaphragm contracting, and it's normal.

The baby is also practising breathing, though they get all their oxygen from the placenta until birth. The lungs have been making surfactant since about 23 weeks, the substance that lets them stay inflated once they breathe air, and breathing movements may be seen on an ultrasound from about 28 weeks.
