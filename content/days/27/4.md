---
title: Side sleeping, from next week
read: [sleep-in-pregnancy]
---
From 28 weeks, next week, doctors recommend going to sleep on your side, either side, at night and for daytime naps. Lying on your back in later pregnancy presses on major blood vessels and can reduce blood flow to the baby, and research shows sleeping on your side can reduce the risk of stillbirth.

The position you fall asleep in is usually the one you stay in longest, so that's the one that counts. Changing position in the night is normal: if you wake on your back, just roll onto your side and go back to sleep.

What makes it easier: knees bent with a pillow between them, another under the bump, and a pregnancy pillow if you'd like one. This week is a good one to try it, before it counts.
