---
title: Restless at night
read: [leg-cramps, restless-legs]
---
Two things can start keeping your legs awake. Cramps, a sudden tightening in the calf, usually at night: pull your toes hard up towards you, rub the muscle (a job for the partner), or walk about. Stretching your calves and a warm bath before bed may help. And restless legs, an urge to move them with a crawling, tingling or aching feeling, worse in the evening, more likely in the third trimester, and usually gone after the birth; see your doctor or midwife if it's disturbing your sleep.

Leg pain can sometimes be a blood clot: contact your doctor, midwife or hospital immediately for pain or swelling in one leg, pain that won't go away, or a leg that's red or warm.
