---
title: Restless at night
read: [leg-cramps, restless-legs]
---
Two things can start keeping your legs awake. Cramps are a sudden, painful tightening, usually in the calf and usually at night. Pulling your toes hard up towards you, rubbing the muscle (a job for the partner) or walking about eases one, and stretching your calves and a warm bath before bed might help. If they're bothering you or won't go away, talk to your doctor or midwife.

Restless legs are an urge to move them, with a burning, tingling, aching or crawling feeling, worse in the evening and more likely in the third trimester; most of the time it goes after the birth. See your doctor or midwife if it's disturbing your sleep.

Leg pain can sometimes be a blood clot: contact your doctor, midwife or hospital immediately for pain or swelling in one leg, pain that won't go away, or a leg that's red or warm.
