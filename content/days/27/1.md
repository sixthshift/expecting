---
title: The third trimester starts about now
read: [the-trimesters]
---
Most people mark the third trimester from about week 27, though you'll see the line drawn a week either way. From here the skin and ligaments keep stretching, you tire more easily, sleep gets harder, and heartburn and breathlessness are normal parts of it.

Check-ups settle into a rhythm: about every 4 weeks until 36 weeks, then every 2. Around 36 weeks, in a first pregnancy, the baby may drop lower into your pelvis. Babies born from 37 weeks are full term.
