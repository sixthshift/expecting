---
title: The third trimester starts about now
read: [the-trimesters]
---
The third trimester runs from about week 27 until the baby is born, though you'll see the line drawn a week either way. From here the skin and ligaments keep stretching, you tire more easily, sleep gets harder, and heartburn and breathlessness are normal parts of it.

Check-ups settle into a rhythm: about every 4 weeks until 36 weeks, then every 2. Around 36 weeks, if it's your first baby, the baby may move lower into your pelvis. Babies born in weeks 37 to 42 are full term, and the trimester ends whenever the baby is born.
