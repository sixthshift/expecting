---
title: Talking to the bump
read: [the-babys-senses]
---
By about 26 weeks the baby can suck, see, hear and taste. Their eyes have responded to light since about 24 weeks, and by about 25 weeks their hearing is fully working: they may react to noises and be soothed by your voice. Some research suggests very early language development may start before birth.

Talking and singing to the baby, playing calming music, and gently touching or rubbing the bump are all ways in, and the partner and others close to you can do the same. A hand run firmly over your tummy might bring a movement in reply. It can feel silly at first, but you get used to it, and bonding in pregnancy can help the baby's development after they're born.
