---
title: A hand on the bump
read: [the-babys-senses]
---
By about 26 weeks the baby can suck, see, hear and taste, and might move in response to a hand run firmly over your tummy. Their eyes have responded to light since about 24 weeks, and their hearing is fully working: your voice can soothe them.

This is a good week for the partner to try it: a firm, slow hand across the bump, and talk while you do. The baby might answer with a kick, or might not; talking and touching the bump are part of bonding either way.
