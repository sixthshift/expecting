---
title: The partner's leave
read: [partner-parental-leave]
---
Parental leave isn't only the pregnant parent's. If the partner works, they have their own entitlement and their own deadlines, on the same rules.

To take unpaid parental leave, they need to have, or be going to have, responsibility for the baby's care, and to have worked for their employer for at least 12 months when it starts. Each parent can take up to 12 months. The partner's must start and end within 24 months of the birth, as one block, flexible days or both, and needn't line up with the pregnant parent's, though it can.

The notice is the same: in writing at least 10 weeks before the leave starts, confirmed at least 4 weeks before. For a child born from 1 July 2026, 20 days of the family's Parental Leave Pay are reserved for the partner, and up to 20 days can be taken at the same time, so you can be paid while you're both home.
