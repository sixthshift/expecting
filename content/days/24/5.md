---
title: A safe cot
read: [cot-and-safe-sleep-standards]
---
The cot doesn't need to be the latest model, just safe. Since 19 January 2026, every cot sold new in Australia must meet the mandatory safety standard for infant sleep products. Second-hand cots aren't covered, though one can still meet the standard: it's noted in the instructions, often on a label, and a cot you're in any doubt about isn't one to use.

A tape measure tells you the rest. The gaps between the bars should be 5 to 9.5 cm, with no other gaps of 3 to 5 cm or over 9.5 cm, no parts sticking out more than 5 cm, and no footholds for climbing.

One firm, flat mattress, fitting within 2 cm of the sides and ends, and nothing else soft: no bumpers, pillows, doonas, loose bedding, sheepskin or soft toys. It belongs in your room for the first 6 to 12 months, away from curtains and blind cords.
