---
title: Labour, if it starts early
read: [signs-of-preterm-labour]
---
Labour usually starts from around 37 weeks, but in Australia about 8 in every 100 babies are born before then. If it starts early, it may be possible to slow it down or stop it, and each day the baby stays inside, the better their health is likely to be.

The signs: cramps like period pain, low backache or pressure in your pelvis, frequent or loose poo, a change in discharge or a bloodstained show, fluid leaking, and contractions. Before 37 weeks, any of them means contacting your midwife, doctor or nearest birthing unit immediately. Call them straight away, too, for bleeding from your vagina, green or brown waters, or strong pressure in your vagina or bottom or an urge to push.

Back pain with other signs, such as cramps, pelvic pressure or fluid leaking, means calling 000 immediately for an ambulance. So does thinking the baby may come before you can reach your hospital or birth centre.
