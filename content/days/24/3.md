---
title: What you actually need
read: [what-you-need-for-the-baby]
---
Newborns don't need much other than love, milk and warmth, and you don't have to buy it all new: second-hand, borrowed and pre-used furniture and equipment save money.

The essentials are few. Somewhere safe to sleep: their own cot or bassinet, meeting the safety guidelines, with a clean, firm mattress that fits tightly against the sides. A baby capsule, secured correctly in the car before the baby arrives. A way to feed, nappies and somewhere to change them, and clothes: all-in-ones, wraps for swaddling, and singlets, beanies and socks for the season.

After that, a pram, bought or borrowed, a bath or the kitchen sink, and a spare cot sheet for wash day. And some preparation isn't gear at all: meals cooked and frozen, and a list of support numbers, made while you're calm.
