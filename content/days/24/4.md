---
title: Running short of breath
read: [breathlessness]
---
The growing uterus pushes up against your diaphragm and leaves less room for your lungs. From around 20 weeks you may feel breathless, especially on your back; lying on your side helps. It's normal, and it eases in the last month as the baby moves lower.

Talk to your doctor or midwife if you're very out of breath, or breathless with less effort. Contact your doctor, midwife or hospital immediately if you're short of breath or your heart is racing, at any time in pregnancy. See a doctor straight away or go to emergency if it comes with swollen ankles, fever and a cough, wheezing or coughing blood, or is worse lying down or getting worse.

Call 000 if you're short of breath and can't speak more than a few words, have pain in your chest, arm or jaw, sweaty pale skin or blue lips, or are drowsy or confused, or if it's very bad and came on quickly.
