---
title: Leaks, and the pelvic floor
read: [leaking-urine]
---
Leaking a little wee when you cough, sneeze, laugh or exercise is very common: about 1 in 3 people have some in pregnancy or after the birth, and it's most likely in the third trimester, as the growing baby strains your pelvic floor.

Pelvic floor exercises help, ideally 3 to 6 sessions a day, and after 16 weeks they're best done lying on your left side rather than flat on your back. A continence professional or physiotherapist can guide you on using the right muscles. Leaking that starts in pregnancy might not get better on its own, so it's worth getting help now, and leaks you can't control are worth raising at your next visit.

If you think your waters are leaking, as a gush or a slow trickle, contact your midwife or doctor straight away. Anything green or brown needs care straight away, even if you thought it was wee.
