---
title: Pain down the leg
read: [sciatica]
---
Sciatica is pain that starts in your back and runs down your leg along the sciatic nerve, through your hip and buttock to your knee or foot, usually on one side, sometimes with numbness or weakness. It's often worse when you cough, sneeze, twist or bend. In pregnancy, changes to your muscles and ligaments may make it more likely. Mostly it isn't serious, and many people recover within a few months.

Stretches, walking or swimming, good posture, changing position often and bending your knees to lift all help, and so can a physiotherapist. Paracetamol is considered safe; don't take ibuprofen unless your doctor advises it.

See your doctor if you have it, and straight away if it's severe lying down or at night, or comes with a fever or unexplained weight loss. With lost feeling or weakness in your limbs, pins and needles, numbness around your genitals or bottom, or trouble controlling your bladder or bowels, call 000.
