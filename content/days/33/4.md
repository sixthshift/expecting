---
title: What happens to the placenta
read: [card-no-lotus-no-capsules]
---
After the birth, what you do with the placenta is your choice, and in most cases it's safe to take it home if you follow basic health and safety rules. If you bury it, bury it deeply enough that animals won't dig it up.

Two ideas come up often. Eating the placenta, or having it encapsulated, has no proven health benefits, though you may choose to for cultural, spiritual or personal reasons. A lotus birth, keeping the cord attached so the baby stays connected to the placenta, has no evidence of health benefits either, and may increase your chance of complications; some still choose it for spiritual or cultural reasons.
