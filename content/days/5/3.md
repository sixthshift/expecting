---
title: Things taste like coins
read: [card-metallic-taste, food-cravings-and-aversions]
---
A strange metallic taste is one of the early signs: sour or coin-like, sometimes even when you're not eating (dysgeusia). Foods can taste different, and ones you usually like may put you off.

Aversions are more likely in the first trimester. Common ones are alcohol, coffee and tea, meat, fatty food, spicy food, eggs and strong smells. If you go off something important to your diet, it's worth trying again later: it usually settles as the pregnancy goes on. Cravings mostly come later, in the second trimester, and no, they can't predict the sex.

See your doctor if nausea stops you eating well, especially with vomiting, being unable to keep food or fluids down, or weight loss. And if you crave things that aren't food, such as chalk or clay, speak to your midwife or doctor.
