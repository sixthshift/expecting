---
title: Embryo, then fetus
read: [term-embryo, how-your-baby-develops]
---
The name changes as the baby grows. There are three stages: germinal, from fertilisation to implantation, in weeks 2 to 4; embryonic, from week 4 to 10, when the important organs and body structures form; and fetal, from week 11 until birth. From about week 9 doctors and midwives may start to say fetus; until then, the word is embryo.

So this is the embryonic stage, the one where the body's structures are laid down. By week 12 the baby is fully formed, with all organs, muscles, limbs and bones in place, and fills your whole uterus. The kidneys and digestive system are starting to work by then, and the baby swallows the amniotic fluid around them and passes it back out as urine.
