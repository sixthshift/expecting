---
title: The toilet, more often
read: [frequent-urination]
---
Needing to wee more often is normal, and common at every stage of pregnancy. Early on it's hormonal changes; later it's the growing uterus pushing against your bladder.

What can help: less caffeine and fewer fizzy drinks, and pelvic floor exercises, which everyone benefits from, during pregnancy and after. A continence physiotherapist can help you train.

If you feel stinging, burning or pain when you wee, talk to your doctor or midwife: it may be a urinary tract infection, which needs treatment as soon as possible. And if how often you need to go worries you, or it's affecting your daily life, mention it at your next visit; there are treatments and supports.
