---
title: A due date, not a due day
read: [estimated-due-date, card-only-five-percent-on-the-day]
---
Once you know, the date comes next. Your estimated due date is around 40 weeks after the first day of your last period, and it is only an estimate: the exact timing of a birth is unpredictable, and only about 5 in 100 babies are born on the day. Babies are full term from 37 weeks, and most are born between 37 and 42.

An early ultrasound can give a different date. If it does, your doctor or midwife will explain which is most accurate for you.

An accurate date still matters, even if the baby ignores it: it helps your health team monitor the pregnancy and the baby's growth.
