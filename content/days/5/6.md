---
title: Listeria, and why the fridge isn't enough
read: [listeria-foods]
---
A lot of the food advice comes down to one bacterium. Listeria causes listeriosis, which is rare but can be serious in pregnancy: it can cause miscarriage, premature birth or stillbirth, and pregnancy lowers your immune system, so you're more likely to catch it.

What makes listeria different is that it keeps growing in the fridge. So the risky foods are ready-to-eat ones that have been chilled and stored: deli and packaged sliced meats, cold cooked chicken, sushi, sashimi and smoked seafood, soft cheeses unless thoroughly cooked, refrigerated pâté, pre-cut fruit and salads, rockmelon, sprouts and soft-serve.

The rule underneath it all: freshly cooked and eaten hot is safe, because cooking food thoroughly kills it. Symptoms usually start about 3 weeks after eating, but can start anywhere from 3 to 70 days, and they can be as mild as a flu: fever, headache, tiredness, aches. If you get them, seek medical attention immediately.
