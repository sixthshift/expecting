---
title: Tender, heavier breasts
read: [sore-breasts]
---
Sore or swollen breasts are an early sign of pregnancy. In the first trimester they become tender, larger and heavier. They're getting ready to make milk, so you may also notice more visible veins, and your nipples and areolas may darken and get bigger. Small bumps on the areola, the Montgomery glands, may become more noticeable; they make an oily substance that helps protect the nipples.

A supportive bra may help with the discomfort. The Royal Women's Hospital suggests having new bras properly fitted so they're comfortable and not too tight, and avoiding hard underwire.

The changes keep going: later in the pregnancy you may notice a yellowish fluid, colostrum, leaking from your breasts. If you have significant pain, redness or a lump, speak to your midwife or doctor.
