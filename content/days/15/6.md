---
title: The partner's way in

Around now the baby is starting to hear: your heartbeat, your digestion, your voice. By 24 weeks sounds from outside the uterus get through too, and that's the partner's opening.

Some partners feel left out in pregnancy, with the attention going to the person who's pregnant. It's a normal feeling, and the ways in don't have to wait for the birth. Reading and talking to the baby gets them used to the partner's voice. Once the kicks come, the partner can feel them as often as they can, and massage the bump if you're happy for them to. The scans, the appointments and the antenatal classes are theirs to come to as well.

Supporting you is a way in too: taking on some household jobs or making the meals so you can rest, asking how you're feeling and really listening to the answer, and checking in with their own needs while they're at it.
