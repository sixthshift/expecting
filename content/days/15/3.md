---
title: Phones, laptops and the microwave
read: [microwaves-and-devices, computer-screens]
---
The phone in your pocket, the laptop on your knee, the microwave at work: they're fine.

ARPANSA, Australia's radiation protection agency, reviewed 18 studies of radiofrequency exposure, mostly mobile phone use, and pregnancy outcomes, and found no or little association, though the evidence was of low certainty. Phone use wasn't linked to preterm birth or low birth weight; there was a small association with a baby being small for gestational age. It says there's no established evidence of health effects from the fields around computers, and that laptops and tablets give off very little. Microwave ovens have no established health effect when used as the manufacturer says and kept in good working order.

At a desk all day, regular breaks help, and you can talk to your employer about help with posture. If something at work makes you feel dizzy, sore or unwell, the advice is to stop doing it and get medical advice from your doctor or midwife.
