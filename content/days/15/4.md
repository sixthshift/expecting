---
title: A nose that won't behave
read: [blocked-nose, nosebleeds]
---
A blocked or runny nose without an infection is pregnancy rhinitis. It affects about 1 in 5 pregnant people, most often in the second trimester, and usually clears within 2 weeks of the birth. Saline sprays are drug-free and safe. Call 000 for breathing problems that are very bad and come on quickly, and see your doctor as soon as possible for any unexplained breathing problems.

Nosebleeds are more common too. Sit up, lean forward and pinch below the bridge for 10 minutes.

For a severe one that won't stop, contact your doctor immediately or call 000. Go to hospital or call an ambulance if it lasts over 20 minutes, there's a lot of blood, you've swallowed blood and vomited more than once, it followed a knock to the head, or you have trouble breathing or feel weak or dizzy. If one starts again, get medical advice, and see your doctor or midwife if they're frequent.
