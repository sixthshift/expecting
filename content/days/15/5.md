---
title: Thicker hair, for now
read: [hair-and-nails-in-pregnancy, card-thick-hair]
---
Around now hair and nails can grow faster, which Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says is all very normal. Hormones such as oestrogen keep each hair in its growing phase for longer, so you shed less and your hair gets thicker. You may grow hair in new places too, on your abdomen, lower back or thighs.

Enjoy it, because it doesn't last. Around 2 to 4 months after the birth the shedding catches up, which can be worrying, but you will not go bald. It can carry on for up to 6 months, and your hair should be back to normal by the time the baby is about a year old. See your doctor if the loss seems excessive, or it hasn't settled after a year.

If you ever take something for hair loss, minoxidil and spironolactone shouldn't be taken in pregnancy because they can harm the baby. Straightening treatments with formaldehyde, a known carcinogen, are best avoided too.
