---
title: Itchy rashes
read: [pregnancy-rash]
---
A few itchy rashes come with pregnancy, none of them harmful to you or the baby. Eczema, dry, scaly, red skin, is the most common skin condition you can develop in pregnancy. Prurigo is a dry, bumpy rash over the stomach, arms or legs, usually starting in the first or second trimester. And polymorphic eruption of pregnancy is a red, bumpy, itchy rash in or near stretch marks on the tummy, usually between 36 and 39 weeks.

Moisturising often, one short, cool shower a day and loose, soft clothes ease the itch, and your pharmacist can check that any cream or antihistamine is safe.

But see your doctor or midwife about any itchy rash or itchy skin, because other causes of itch, such as intrahepatic cholestasis of pregnancy, can harm the baby and need to be ruled out. After 20 weeks, extreme itchiness, especially of your hands and feet, is a call to your doctor, midwife or hospital immediately.
