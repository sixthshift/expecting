---
title: Into the water
read: [swimming]
---
With more weight to carry, the water can take some of it. Swimming is among the medium-intensity aerobic activities recommended in pregnancy, and swimming and aqua-natal classes can help support your weight. To protect your joints, the Royal Women's suggests a pool or fitball class rather than walking long distances.

Heat is the catch. Intensive exercise can raise your core temperature to a level that's unsafe for the baby, so the advice is moderate effort, and water before, during and after. Heated pools and spas are out: the Royal Women's names spas and hydrotherapy pools as unsafe at high temperatures.

In a class, the instructor needs to know you're pregnant and how many weeks. Scuba diving waits for after: Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says to avoid activities with large changes in pressure.
