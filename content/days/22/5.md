---
title: For the partner, the short list
read: [partner-learning-the-signs]
---
Most of what happens in a pregnancy is the pregnant parent's to feel and describe. But the partner may be the one who notices something has changed, and the one with a free hand to make the call.

So there's a short list worth knowing by name: a change in the baby's movements, bleeding from the vagina, a severe headache or changes to vision, fluid leaking from the vagina, a fever, and itching. Each has its own entry, saying how urgent it is and who to call.

And one rule for both of you: if either of you is worried, call. The advice is to ring for support and guidance any time you feel concerned. That's why the hospital's or birth unit's numbers belong in both phones, so neither of you is ever hunting for them.
