---
title: Help for an aching back
read: [chiropractic-and-osteopathy, acupuncture]
---
With backache common now, you may wonder about hands-on help. A physiotherapist can help you manage it, with stretches and exercises that suit you.

Chiropractic and osteopathy are a question to ask your doctor or midwife first. There's some evidence they might reduce back and pelvic pain, but the evidence on their safety in pregnancy is limited, and there are reports of negative effects after spinal manipulation. Before any manipulation the therapist should assess you to make sure it's safe, and AHPRA's website shows who is registered.

Acupuncture is generally safe in pregnancy if a qualified professional does it. There's some evidence it might help with back and pelvic pain. Your acupuncturist needs to know you're pregnant, as some points aren't safe to use, and healthdirect says to speak to your doctor first. Mild pain or bruising from the needles is the most common side effect.
