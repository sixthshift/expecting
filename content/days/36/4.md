---
title: Early labour can take days
read: [early-labour, card-labour-can-take-days]
---
Early labour, the latent phase, is when the cervix shortens, thins and begins to open, and it can last many hours or even days, with contractions regular or irregular, or barely felt. If your pregnancy has been normal and you're comfortable, you can usually stay home, and research shows people labour much better if they do: rest, light snacks and water, a warm shower or heat packs, gentle movement.

You can ring the birth unit any time you're unsure. Call straight away if your waters break, you're bleeding from your vagina, labour starts before a planned caesarean, or the baby is moving less or differently; if the waters are greenish or bloody, contact your hospital immediately. Call 000 if you think you won't make it to your place of birth in time, or for heavy bleeding, a severe headache or blurred vision. And before 37 weeks, signs of labour mean calling your midwife or doctor and going straight to hospital.
