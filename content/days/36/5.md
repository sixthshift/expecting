---
title: The gas, which you hold yourself
read: [nitrous-oxide-gas, card-gas-side-effects]
---
The gas is nitrous oxide mixed with oxygen, breathed through a mouthpiece or mask during contractions only. It works best started about 30 seconds before a contraction or right at its start, breathing deeply at your normal rate, and stopping as it eases. It can take a few contractions to get the hang of it.

It can reduce pain and anxiety and help you relax, though for some it only dulls the pain, and it doesn't work for everyone. It can be used at any stage, you can move between contractions, and it clears from your body within a few minutes. It has no known harmful effects on the baby and doesn't affect breastfeeding.

The side effects are yours: dizziness, light-headedness, nausea or vomiting. Too much can make you drowsy, which is why only you hold the mouthpiece, so you can stop the moment you've had enough.
