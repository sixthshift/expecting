---
title: The dating scan
read: [dating-scan, term-crown-rump-length, term-ultrasound]
---
The dating scan is most often done between 6 and 8 weeks. It's an early ultrasound, done through your tummy or through the vagina (transvaginal); the sonographer recommends which. Ultrasounds in pregnancy don't harm you or the baby or increase the risk of miscarriage.

It measures the crown–rump length, the baby's length from head to bottom, and compares it with population data. Early on, most babies of the same age are about the same size, so it can give an accurate estimated due date. It can also confirm whether there's more than one baby, check the baby is growing as expected and that the pregnancy isn't ectopic, and check where the placenta is.

Medicare covers it, though there may be out-of-pocket costs depending on where you go, and the partner can come too.
