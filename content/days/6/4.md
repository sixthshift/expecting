---
title: First-trimester tired
read: [tiredness]
---
It's common to feel tired in pregnancy, and in the first trimester it's because of all the changes happening in your body. Energy often improves after 14 weeks. It can come back towards the end, with more weight to carry and less sleep.

Resting when you can, asking for help with chores, gentle exercise, regular balanced meals with enough iron and protein, and avoiding caffeine later in the day can all help. Most of that list the partner can make easier.

See your doctor or midwife if you're exhausted and more rest doesn't help, if you start snoring or have more pauses in your breathing during sleep, if your sleep is disturbed by a health condition, or if you're feeling anxious or depressed. Tiredness can also be a symptom of anaemia, low iron, which is common in pregnancy because your body needs more iron.
