---
title: Morning sickness, at any hour
read: [morning-sickness, ginger]
---
Despite the name, morning sickness, nausea with or without vomiting, can happen at any time of day. It's most common between about 6 and 14 weeks. For many people it settles by the second trimester, though it can last to about 14 to 20 weeks, and for some, right through.

Ginger tea can help ease nausea, and should be safe in moderation: the advice is herbal tea up to 2 cups a day, checked with your doctor or pharmacist first, especially if you take prescription medicine. For severe sickness, though, ginger is no longer suggested. Ginger beer is a different thing: it's a fermented drink to avoid.

If you can't keep any fluids down, go to your local emergency department: they can give you fluids through a drip. For vomiting that's severe or won't stop, contact your doctor, midwife or hospital straight away.
