title: The partner's first trimester

For the partner. Finding out you're going to be a parent can be wonderful, or emotional and confusing, or scary, and all of that is normal. Some couples don't announce the pregnancy until the end of the first trimester, which makes these quiet weeks: time to get used to the idea of being parents.

Pregnancy can be exhausting, physically and emotionally, and what helps is mostly being there: help and a listening ear, coming to appointments, talking about how you're both feeling, and not taking mood swings personally. With morning sickness, small amounts of food often; if they're vomiting often and you're concerned, contact the doctor. Eating well and being active together counts too, as does cutting back on alcohol and not smoking.

Your own feelings count too: feeling helpless when they're sick, pressure if you're the only income, worry about the birth or bonding. They're worth talking about openly, with your partner, family or friends, before they build.
