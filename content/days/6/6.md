---
title: CMV, the virus few have heard of
read: [cmv]
---
Cytomegalovirus (CMV) is a common virus that most people catch without symptoms. If you catch it for the first time while pregnant, it can cross the placenta and infect the baby, and that's more likely in the first half of pregnancy. There's no vaccine, and many people haven't heard of it.

It's in saliva, urine and other body fluids, and a common way pregnant people catch it is from young children. So the advice is hygiene: washing your hands after nappies, wiping noses, feeding and handling toys; gloves for nappies; not sharing food, cups, cutlery or toothbrushes with young children or putting their dummy in your mouth; no kisses on the lips; and cleaning surfaces and toys with saliva, urine or mucus on them.

A test isn't routine, but the guidelines recommend one as early as possible in the first trimester if you have young children at home who go to childcare, or work in childcare.
