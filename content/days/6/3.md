---
title: The pain to know about
read: [cramping-and-pain-in-early-pregnancy]
---
Implantation can bring some cramping and light bleeding. But pain or bleeding in the first trimester can also be a sign of a problem, such as a miscarriage or an ectopic pregnancy, so vaginal bleeding or belly pain in these months is always one to get medical help for.

An ectopic pregnancy is one growing outside the uterus, usually in a fallopian tube. About 1 in 100 pregnancies is ectopic, and it usually shows up 6 to 8 weeks after your last period, around now. If the tube tears it can cause serious internal bleeding, which can be life-threatening.

Its signs are vaginal bleeding or spotting, tummy pain, back pain, pain in the tip of your shoulder, nausea and vomiting, a fast heart rate, and dizziness or feeling faint. With any of them, go to the nearest emergency department or call 000 for an ambulance. The same for heavy bleeding, strong pain or feeling unwell.
