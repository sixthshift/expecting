---
title: The medicines you take every day
read: [prescription-medicines-ongoing]
---
If you take something for an ongoing condition, such as asthma, depression, diabetes, epilepsy or high blood pressure, the advice is the same for all of them: don't stop it until you've spoken with your doctor. Some prescription medicines must not be taken in pregnancy and others need care, but stopping some treatments can also cause problems for you and the baby.

So your doctor weighs the medicine against the condition. They may change the medicine, change the dose, stop it, or leave it as it is. Pregnancy itself can change how your body processes medicines, which can change how much you need.

If you're planning a pregnancy and take regular medicines, before is a good time for that conversation. For questions in between, healthdirect's nurses are on 1800 022 222, 24 hours a day.
