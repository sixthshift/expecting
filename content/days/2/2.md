---
title: Acne medicines and planning ahead
read: [isotretinoin]
---
One medicine needs planning around. Isotretinoin (Roaccutane, Oratane) can cause birth defects: its consumer medicine information says that if you fall pregnant while taking it there's an extremely high risk of a severely deformed baby, and not to use it if you're pregnant or for at least one month before you plan to fall pregnant. If you're taking it and planning a pregnancy, talk to the doctor who prescribed it. If you become pregnant while taking it, the CMI says to stop and tell your doctor immediately.

Some creams count too. MotherSafe says tretinoin, a prescription acne cream, shouldn't be used in pregnancy; over-the-counter retinol is best paused, though past use isn't a concern.
