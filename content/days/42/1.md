---
title: Your first days after the birth
read: [first-days-mum, card-afterpains, card-bleed-six-weeks]

Feeling elated, exhausted and drained after the birth is normal. So is bleeding: the blood, lochia, is bright red and heavy for the first few days, like a heavy period, maybe with clots; then lighter and reddish-brown, then white or yellow, for up to 6 weeks. The advice until your 6-week check is maternity pads or period underwear, not tampons or cups, which may increase the chance of infection. Afterpains, cramps like period pain as your uterus shrinks back, are more likely if this isn't your first baby and more noticeable while breastfeeding; a warm pack may help, or paracetamol 30 minutes before a feed.

Tell your midwife about a bad smell. If you're soaking a pad every hour or two or passing large clots, go to your hospital's emergency department straight away; call 000 if you gave birth in the last 24 hours, or feel unwell, dizzy or clammy, your heart is racing or you're short of breath.
