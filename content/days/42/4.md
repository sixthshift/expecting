---
title: The Parent Pack
read: [proof-of-birth, medicare-enrol-baby]
---
After the birth, the hospital or your midwife gives you a Parent Pack, and inside it is a small piece of paper that does a lot of work: the Newborn Child Declaration. Its back page, once your doctor or midwife completes the Proof of Birth declaration, is your proof of birth.

That page finalises a Parental Leave Pay or Family Tax Benefit claim made before the birth, which needs telling Services Australia within 56 days of the baby coming into your care. It goes with a new claim for those payments. And it's how the baby is enrolled in Medicare: through the claim, or on its own in myGov. Services Australia then adds the baby to your Medicare card and the Australian Immunisation Register, and a new card follows in 3 to 4 weeks.

It can't be reissued. Lost, it means a birth certificate or a letter from the doctor or midwife instead, so it's worth keeping somewhere safe.
