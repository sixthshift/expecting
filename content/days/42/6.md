---
title: The baby's book, and a digital record
read: [my-health-record-for-baby]
---
Two records start when the baby is born. The first is a coloured book in the newborn pack you get in hospital: blue in the ACT, NSW, SA and Tasmania, red in Queensland, green in Victoria, purple in WA and yellow in the NT. It holds the baby's growth and development, vaccinations and health issues from birth until school, and it goes to every appointment: the doctor, the child health nurse, the immunisation clinic, the dentist, and childcare or school later. Health professionals write in it, and you can too, medicines or milestones. It's a permanent record, worth keeping safe.

The second is My Health Record, a secure digital record that you and the baby's healthcare providers can add to: immunisations, allergies, health issues, and your own notes on milestones. You can get one for the baby through the Newborn Child Declaration in the pack, or when you enrol the baby in Medicare.
