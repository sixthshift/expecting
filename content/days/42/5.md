---
title: Registering the birth is yours to do
read: [registering-the-birth, birth-certificate]
---
The hospital won't register the baby's birth for you; that's the parents' job. You register with the Births, Deaths and Marriages registry of the state or territory where the baby is born, it's free, and in most places it's within 60 days (6 months in the ACT). You'll need the baby's given names to do it.

It matters beyond the record. Registration gives the baby a legal name and identity, and Services Australia may not be able to pay you until the birth is registered, or you've applied to register it.

A birth certificate is a separate thing: optional, not automatic, and a paper one costs extra in most states (Victoria sends a free digital one). It's the baby's proof of identity for things like childcare, school, a bank account and a passport, and you can order it with the registration. A decorative commemorative certificate isn't a legal document.
