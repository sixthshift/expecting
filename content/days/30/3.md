---
title: The first milk, already made
read: [leaking-colostrum, card-colostrum-leaking, term-antenatal-expressing]
---
Colostrum is your first milk: thick, sticky and usually yellowish, though it can be thinner, clearer or white. Your breasts have been making it for months, and later in pregnancy it may leak from your nipples, more so as the birth approaches. That's normal, and breast pads from the supermarket or pharmacy help. It's not always obvious, either, so you may notice nothing at all.

Expressing it before the birth is called antenatal expressing, and the timing matters: you shouldn't express colostrum before 36 weeks. It isn't advised if you're at risk of a premature birth, because stimulating your breasts may trigger premature labour. Whether expressing in late pregnancy is a good idea for you is a question for your doctor or midwife.
