---
title: Breech at 30 weeks
read: [card-breech-at-30-weeks, card-most-breech-babies-turn]
---
If you've heard the baby is breech, head up and bottom down, it's common at this stage. Most babies turn head-down before the last month, and even at 35 weeks most will gradually turn during the last month. With an uncomplicated pregnancy, no extra care is needed for a breech baby until about 37 weeks.

Your doctor or midwife will feel your abdomen at around 36 weeks to check the baby's position. Near the due date only about 3 in every 100 babies are still breech, and if a baby is still breech then, the chance of turning without help is low. That's when the options, such as ECV, are talked through: if the baby is still breech at 36 weeks, your obstetrician or midwife should discuss it with you.
