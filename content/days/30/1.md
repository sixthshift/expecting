title: Why they take your blood pressure every time

In pregnancy, blood pressure is high at 140 over 90 or more. It may be a one-off, or a sign of pre-eclampsia, a serious condition that usually comes on suddenly after 20 weeks, can affect your kidneys, liver, brain and placenta, and can get worse very quickly. Most people with it notice no symptoms, which is why your blood pressure is checked at every visit.

The signs of severe pre-eclampsia are sudden swelling of your hands, face or feet, a headache simple pain relief doesn't fix, flashing lights or spots, severe pain below the ribs, heartburn antacids don't fix, and feeling very unwell; for any of them, see your midwife or doctor straight away. A severe headache that painkillers don't help, blurry vision or flashes of light, nausea, sudden swelling, or pain under your ribs: seek medical attention urgently or go to emergency. A sudden, severe headache, a headache with vision changes, vomiting or confusion, or a seizure: call 000.
