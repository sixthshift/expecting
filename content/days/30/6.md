---
title: Massage, done safely
read: [massage]
---
With backache common now, massage is worth knowing about. Pregnancy massage is designed to relieve common pregnancy problems and may reduce stress, back and leg pain and anxiety, and improve mood and sleep. It's generally considered safe, as long as the therapist is specially trained in pregnancy massage; it's worth checking with your doctor or midwife first that it's safe for you.

A trained therapist won't have you lie on your stomach or your back: flat on your back in the second half of pregnancy can press on the vein from your legs to your heart, and make you dizzy or short of breath. They'll avoid your abdomen, be very gentle on your legs, as the chance of a blood clot is higher in pregnancy, and use only oils that are safe in pregnancy.

The partner can give a gentle massage too, knowing how to do it safely. And there's good evidence massage in labour helps manage pain.
