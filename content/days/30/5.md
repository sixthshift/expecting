---
title: What helps in labour
read: [pain-relief]
---
How labour feels, and what helps, is very individual, and there's no right answer, only what suits you. You can choose one method or a few.

Without medicines: moving around and changing positions; relaxation and breathing; warm or cold packs, a shower or a bath; touch and massage; continuous support from a partner, family member or midwife; a TENS machine; and sterile water injections for lower back pain. With medicines: gas (nitrous oxide), which takes the edge off and wears off in about a minute; an opioid injection such as morphine, which reduces the pain but doesn't take it away completely; and an epidural, very effective, with trade-offs of its own.

They don't all mix: after an epidural you need to stay still, so moving around and water are off the table. Not every birth unit offers every method, and changing your mind during labour is completely normal.
