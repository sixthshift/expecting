---
title: Working into the last six weeks
read: [working-close-to-the-due-date]
---
There's no one right time to stop work: it depends on your pregnancy, your health, your finances and your job. Parental leave usually starts up to 6 weeks before the due date, or earlier if your employer agrees, and if you're fit for work you can keep going.

From 6 weeks out, about three weeks from now, the rules change a little. Your employer can ask for a medical certificate, within 7 days, saying whether you're fit for work and whether it's not recommended you keep doing your current job. Without one, or if it says you're not fit, they can direct you to start parental leave, and that counts towards your total.

If the certificate says you're fit for work but not for your normal job, you're entitled to a safe job, or no-safe-job leave if there isn't one.
