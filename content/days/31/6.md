---
title: The tour, and the trip there
read: [hospital-tour]
---
Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says it's often useful for both of you, the expecting parent and the birth support partner, to tour the maternity hospital or birth centre, so you know what to expect. Every facility is different and has its own policies.

It also suggests a practice run of the trip in the third trimester, because labour often starts unexpectedly. That means knowing where you can park, and the route, including whether it changes with traffic or the time of day. And the pregnant parent doesn't drive themselves when they're in labour.

Your hospital can tell you how its tours run.
