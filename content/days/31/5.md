---
title: Bleeding, from here on
read: [bleeding-after-20-weeks]
---
Bleeding after 20 weeks is usually a problem with the placenta. A low-lying placenta most often shows as painless bleeding after 28 weeks. With placental abruption, where the placenta separates from the uterus before birth, the pain is usually severe but may be mild or absent, and the amount of blood doesn't show how serious it is.

Near the birth, the show (the mucus plug) can be pink, brown or bloodstained. Blood that's bright red, or not mixed with mucus, probably isn't a show: contact your doctor or midwife immediately. For any other bleeding, contact your doctor, midwife or birth unit straight away, and tell them if you know your placenta is low.

Call 000 or go to emergency for heavy bleeding (soaking a pad or more in an hour, clots, or bleeding you can't contain), bleeding with pain or contractions, strong pain in your tummy, pelvis or shoulder, or feeling dizzy, faint, very unwell or short of breath.
