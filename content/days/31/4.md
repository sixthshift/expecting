---
title: A bassinet, and a portable cot
read: [bassinet, portable-cot]
---
Many parents choose a bassinet in the bedroom for the early months. Since 19 January 2026 a new bassinet must meet the same mandatory standard as a cot, with sides at least 250 mm high; a second-hand one isn't covered, so an older one is yours to check.

It's set up like a cot: a clean, firm mattress that fits tightly, no soft bedding, nothing hanging nearby, and the baby on their back with face and head uncovered. When they first show signs of rolling, or reach its weight limit, they move to the cot.

A portable cot is for travel or visits, and might not be as safe as a permanent cot. A safe one meets the standard, is set up exactly to the instructions, has only its own thin mattress, and is for a baby under 15 kg.
