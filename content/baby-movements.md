First movements usually show up somewhere between 16 and 24 weeks — flutters, swishes, tiny kicks. By about 28 weeks they become regular, and babies keep moving every day right up to birth.

What surprises people:

- **Don't count kicks.** There's no set number. Kick charts are no longer recommended; instead, get to know *this* baby's own pattern.
- **A change matters.** If movements slow, weaken or stop, the advice is to contact the midwife or birth unit straight away — not tomorrow. You are not wasting their time.
- **Home dopplers and apps can't tell you the baby is well**, and eating or drinking something to get the baby moving doesn't work.

Save the birth suite number in both phones now. Ask your midwife or OB.
