The morphology scan is a detailed ultrasound, usually at 18 to 20 weeks. "Morphology" means body structure: the sonographer looks at how the baby is growing and developing, checks the organs, and notes where the placenta is.

Partners can come along. Depending on the baby's position, it may be when you can find out the sex, if you want to; tell the sonographer either way so there are no accidental reveals.

Medicare covers part of the cost; some clinics charge a gap, so ask when booking. If anything needs a closer look, there will be a follow-up plan. Ask your midwife or OB.
