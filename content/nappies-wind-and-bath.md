Ask the midwives to show you a nappy change and a bath before you go home. Nobody minds, and it's easier with someone beside you the first time.

- **Nappies:** keep nappies, wipes, barrier cream and a spare outfit within reach of wherever you change the baby.
- **Poo:** the first poos (meconium) are black or dark green and sticky, changing over a few days. After that, mustard yellow is usual for a breastfed baby; formula-fed babies' poo tends to be darker and firmer. Green or brown can be normal too. **White, grey or very pale poo**, or black poo after the first few days, needs checking.
- **Wind:** some babies need burping and some don't. Try partway through a feed and at the end.
- **Baths:** a newborn only needs a bath two or three times a week. Keep the water shallow and about body temperature (37–38 °C, checked with your wrist or a thermometer), keep it short, and never leave the baby alone in the bath, even for a moment.

If something doesn't seem right, ask your midwife, child health nurse or GP.
