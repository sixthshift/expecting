## The baby

Gaining weight quickly, with the body now growing faster than the head. The bones in the ears are developing, so the baby is starting to hear for the first time: your heartbeat, your digestion, your voice. A fine, soft hair called lanugo covers the body about now, usually shed before birth.

## Your body

More blood is flowing around your body, which can give you a glow and also changes in blood pressure, bleeding gums or nose, and headaches. Nipples may darken, and hair and nails may grow faster. The nausea should have cleared by now.

If you did not have screening in the first trimester, a blood test called maternal serum screening can be offered from 15 weeks, and amniocentesis is possible from now where it is recommended. Still feeling sick or vomiting is worth mentioning.

## For the partner

Start talking to the bump. It feels silly for about a day, and the baby can hear you. If you skipped first-trimester screening and want the second-trimester option, maternal serum screening can be offered from this week, so ask about it. The morphology scan is booked from next week, so find out how your hospital or clinic arranges it.
