## The baby

The brain and nervous system have almost finished developing, and muscles and nerves are starting to work together: the baby is probably making little movements you could see on a scan, though not feel. The head is now about the same length as the body, bones are starting to harden, and there are individual fingers and toes with nail beds.

## Your body

For many people morning sickness starts to settle about now, though for some it carries on. Smaller, more frequent meals and a plain cracker on waking are what the source suggests. More vaginal discharge than usual is normal in pregnancy: clear or white, without a strong smell.

If nausea or vomiting is causing you significant discomfort, see your doctor. If discharge has a funny smell or colour, or comes with itching or pain, see your doctor or midwife. Ask your midwife or OB.

## For the partner

If you have decided on screening, the 12-week scan is next week or so: make sure it is booked, and that you can be there. If you have not decided yet, do it now: the combined test's blood test is between 9 and 13 weeks and its scan between 11 weeks and 13 weeks 6 days. The sickness easing is not guaranteed, so keep doing what has been working.
