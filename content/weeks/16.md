## The baby

On the edge of a growth spurt: roughly doubling in size over the next month. The eyes have moved to the front of the head and the ears are in place. All the joints work, the fingers and toes wriggle, and the baby can make faces, though it cannot control them yet. The umbilical cord is fully formed, with two arteries and one vein.

## Your body

Some people feel the baby move for the first time about now, especially in a second pregnancy; many will not for a few more weeks. Varicose veins and leg cramps can start as blood volume rises, and backache gets more common as hormones loosen the ligaments.

If you have not felt the baby move by 24 weeks, that is a call.

## For the partner

Book the morphology scan this week if it is not already in: it happens between 18 and 22 weeks and is the one you both want to be at. Libido can go either way in pregnancy, so talk honestly about it; sex is fine unless a doctor has said otherwise.
