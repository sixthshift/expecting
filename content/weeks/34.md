## The baby

The immune system is developing and the bones are getting harder, except in the skull, where the plates will not fuse until long after birth. The two fontanelles, the soft spots where the plates meet, let the skull bones slide over each other during birth to protect the brain and fit through the birth canal.

## Your body

Much like the beginning: you may be quite tired and emotional, with aches and pains and poor sleep. Pains at the top of the legs are common as the ligaments stretch. Your weight may be checked at visits; for a BMI in the normal range (18.5 to 24.9), a total gain of 11.5 to 16 kg across the pregnancy is expected, less if you were overweight and more if underweight.

If your blood group is RhD negative, a second anti-D injection may be offered at 34 weeks. If the leg pains are very painful, tell your doctor or midwife. Ask your midwife or OB.

## For the partner

The last stretch of admin starts here. Confirm your leave dates with your employer, which must be at least four weeks before leave starts. Pack the hospital bag together over the next couple of weeks, and save the support numbers, the birth unit and the feeding helpline, in both phones.
