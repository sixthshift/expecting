## The baby

Growing very quickly, and the tiny muscles can now move. The skin is transparent, and the arms and legs are bent so it looks as though the baby is hugging itself. The head is still large and the face is forming: closed eyes, a mouth and a tongue with tiny taste buds. The inner ear is forming, though hearing is still a long way off, at about 24 weeks.

## Your body

No bump yet, though you may have put on a little weight; you do not need extra calories at this stage. Pregnancy hormones can make skin oilier, or drier, and headaches are common about now. Nausea may still be strong but usually eases in a few weeks.

Needing to wee more often is normal, but burning or stinging can be a sign of an infection, which is more common in pregnancy. Ask your midwife or OB.

## For the partner

Still the hard, secret stretch. Six to eight glasses of water a day is the source's advice for the skin, so keep a bottle in reach. If you are wondering about sex, the source is clear that unless a doctor has said otherwise it is fine and will not harm the baby, and that wanting more or less of it is both normal. Talk about it rather than guessing.
