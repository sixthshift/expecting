## The baby

Curled like a tadpole. Small buds that will become arms and legs are starting to show, the neural tube (the start of the brain and spinal cord) is closing, and the heart has started beating.

## Your body

Many people have only just found out. Tiredness, sore breasts and needing to wee more often are common now, and morning sickness often starts around this week. It can come at any time of day.

If you can't keep fluids down, call your GP or midwife today rather than waiting it out. Ask your midwife or OB.

## For the partner

You can't see anything yet, but this is a hard stretch: tired, queasy, and often a secret. Take over the cooking smells, keep easy snacks within reach, and go to the first appointment together if you can. Who to tell, and when, is a choice to make together; many people wait until after the 12-week scan.
