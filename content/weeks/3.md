## The baby

Around now it begins: the egg is fertilised by sperm. Over the next 4 or 5 days the fertilised egg keeps dividing as it travels down the fallopian tube towards the uterus.

## Your body

As soon as you conceive, the hormone levels in your body start to change, but you probably will not notice any signs that you are pregnant just yet.

## For the partner

Nothing is known yet, by either of you, and there is nothing to do this week except carry on. The first sign for many people is a missed period at the end of week 4, and a pregnancy test can be done the day after, so there is nothing to book until then.
