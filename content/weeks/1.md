## The baby

There is no baby yet. Pregnancy and its due date are counted from the first day of your last period, which comes before conception has even happened. Nothing has started, but the count has.

## Your body

You probably will not notice any signs of pregnancy yet, because by this count there is nothing to notice.

If you are trying, the source suggests taking 400 micrograms of folate every day to prevent neural tube defects such as spina bifida. Ask your GP.

## For the partner

Nothing to book yet. If the baby is planned, the source suggests seeing your doctor before you fall pregnant, to talk about genetic screening or to be tested for sexually transmitted infections. Not drinking alcohol and giving up smoking are also best for a healthy pregnancy and baby, so it helps to do them together.
