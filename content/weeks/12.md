## The baby

The organs, muscles, limbs and bones are all in place; from here it is mostly growing. The kidneys are working, the baby swallows the fluid around it, and its chest makes practice breathing movements. Twenty tiny tooth buds sit in the gums.

## Your body

This is the end of the first trimester. For many people the sickness and tiredness start to ease over the next few weeks, though not for everyone. A bump usually isn't showing yet, especially in a first pregnancy.

The 12-week scan and the screening tests happen around now, if you choose to have them. Ask your midwife or OB.

## For the partner

Come to the scan if you can: it is often the first time you'll see the baby move. After it, many couples start telling people. Talk about who hears first, so neither of you is caught out.
