## The baby

The arms and legs have grown and look more in proportion with the body, and there is soft hair on the head. If the baby is a girl, her uterus has formed; if a boy, his testicles have. Most people feel movement for the first time between 16 and 24 weeks, as a flutter, a swish, a roll or a tiny kick.

## Your body

The growing uterus can press on your stomach, so heartburn and indigestion are common; smaller meals and a raised head at night help. Bloating, gas and a changed appetite are common too.

Heartburn that is severe and not helped by the usual things can occasionally be a sign of something more serious, such as pre-eclampsia. Ask your midwife or OB.

## For the partner

If you want a trip away before the baby, the source says the second trimester is a good time to travel: flying is generally fine in a healthy pregnancy up to 28 weeks, with the airline and travel insurer checked first, and compression stockings and a walk about the cabin on long flights. In the car the lap belt goes under the bump. Antenatal classes should be booked by next week if they are not already.
