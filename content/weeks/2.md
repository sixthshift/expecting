## The baby

Still nothing to describe. By the way pregnancy is counted, from the first day of your last period, this is before conception has even happened. Your pregnancy begins when an egg is fertilised by sperm.

## Your body

Nothing to notice yet. The hormone levels in your body start to change as soon as you conceive, and you probably will not notice any signs of pregnancy at first.

## For the partner

There is no admin this week. The source says your baby will have the best possible start in life if you are healthy when you conceive: a healthy diet, regular exercise, no alcohol and no smoking. If you are trying, those are worth doing together.
