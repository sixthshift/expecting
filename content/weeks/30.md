## The baby

Laying down fat, which smooths out the wrinkles and makes the baby look plumper. The lanugo hair is disappearing and more hair grows on the head; the toenails have formed. Reflexes are developing, and the baby may suck its thumb or fingers, sometimes caught on a scan.

## Your body

Ligaments are stretching, so some sitting positions and long standing get uncomfortable, and backache is common in the third trimester: good posture, gentle exercise, flat shoes, a cushion behind the back and care with lifting can help. You now have twice your usual blood volume, and your blood pressure is checked at every visit; about one in ten people develop high blood pressure in pregnancy, often without symptoms, which is one reason the routine visits matter.

Keep an eye on the baby's movements: if you are concerned at any stage, contact your midwife or doctor immediately. Ask your midwife or OB.

## For the partner

Two deadlines this week. The pre-birth Parental Leave Pay claim goes in through myGov, and the employer's written notice of leave is needed at least 10 weeks before leave starts, which is about now if leave starts at the due date. The RSV vaccine can be booked from 28 weeks. The car seat can be bought from now; it needs a professional fitting, which takes time to book, so start looking.
