## The baby

Gaining weight quickly from here. Eyelashes are growing, a creamy coating called vernix protects the skin, and the lungs are maturing, though they aren't ready yet. Movements should feel regular and strong.

## Your body

The third trimester starts. Tiredness often comes back, along with backache, heartburn, breathlessness and trouble getting comfortable at night.

Get to know your baby's usual pattern of movements. If they slow down, change or stop, call your birth unit straight away. Several things fall around now too: the glucose test, the RSV and whooping cough vaccines, and anti-D if your blood group is RhD negative. Ask your midwife or OB.

## For the partner

The paperwork starts here: Parental Leave Pay can be claimed before the birth, and your employer needs notice of your leave. Save the birth unit's number in your phone now, and ask how the baby's movements usually feel, so you can both notice a change.
