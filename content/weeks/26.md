## The baby

From now the baby puts on a lot of fat and muscle, which fills it out. It can suck, see, hear and taste, and may move in response to a hand run firmly over the bump. In boys the testicles are usually fully descended now. The lungs are still developing; the chance of surviving if born now is about 80 per cent with expert care in a neonatal unit.

## Your body

Your centre of gravity is shifting, so you may walk differently and feel a little off balance. Backache is caused by the growing weight of the pregnancy and by hormones loosening your ligaments. From now into the third trimester you may start having vivid dreams, as sleep gets more disturbed.

If your blood group is RhD negative, anti-D may be offered at 28 and 34 weeks. Back pain that is severe or lasts more than two weeks is worth raising. Ask your midwife or OB.

## For the partner

Try the firm hand on the bump: the baby may move in response. Two bits of paperwork open this week. Your own Parental Leave Pay has days reserved for you to use, so look at how they work and start planning when you will take them. And the written notice to the employer is needed at least 10 weeks before leave starts, so get it drafted if you have not.
