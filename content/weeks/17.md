## The baby

Might be bigger than the placenta now, and starting to lay down a layer of fat that will fill out the features. The external sex organs are fully formed. The taste buds work, telling sweet from bitter, and tiny lines on the fingertips will become fingerprints.

## Your body

As the uterus moves up, the pressure on your bladder eases and you may not need the toilet as often, and any nausea is hopefully behind you. A dark line, the linea nigra, may be forming down from the belly button. A stuffy nose and snoring are common hormonal side effects, and more discharge is normal.

Discharge that changes colour or smells could be an infection: see your doctor or midwife.

## For the partner

Time to think about antenatal classes. Hospitals, community health centres and private providers all run them, some free and some paid, and there are online options. Ask your doctor or midwife which ones they recommend, and book by about week 22 before they fill. They are for both of you.
