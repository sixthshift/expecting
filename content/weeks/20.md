## The baby

Fingerprints have formed, and permanent teeth are in the gums underneath the baby teeth. Cartilage throughout the body is turning into bone, the bone marrow is starting to make blood cells, and the baby is building up brown fat to help keep warm after birth. The ears have not finished developing, but the baby can hear sounds from outside the uterus.

## Your body

Halfway: you are now half-way through the pregnancy. The top of your uterus is usually roughly level with your belly button, and your doctor or midwife will measure your belly at each check-up. As the uterus takes up more room you may feel breathless from time to time, especially lying on your back; lying on your side with a pillow helps. You will start to feel your baby's movements between 16 and 24 weeks of pregnancy, and every baby is different.

Round ligament pain, from aching to a sharp, stabbing feeling, is common, but if you are worried about pain, talk to your doctor or midwife. Ask your midwife or OB.

## For the partner

The morphology scan can be done any time between about 18 and 22 weeks: if it is not booked yet, book it now, and go along. Antenatal classes are the other booking to make together, before they fill up. You do not have to spend a lot on maternity clothes either; loose tops from the wardrobe, or borrowed ones, do the job.
