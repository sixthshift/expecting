## The baby

The heart beats 120 to 160 times a minute, fingerprints have formed, and the baby is moving a lot. Their ears work now: they can hear muffled sounds from outside, including your voices.

## Your body

Halfway. Many people feel their best in this middle stretch, but backache, hip ache and the odd moment of breathlessness are common as the bump grows. If this is your first baby, the first flutters of movement often arrive around now; if not, you may have felt them for a few weeks.

The morphology scan is due about now. Ask your midwife or OB.

## For the partner

Talk and sing to the bump: the baby can hear you. Kicks you can feel from the outside usually come a few weeks after the first flutters inside, so keep a hand handy. It's a good time to book antenatal classes together, before they fill up.
