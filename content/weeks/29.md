## The baby

All the organs are developed and most are working; the lungs are maturing and the baby practises breathing in a steadier rhythm. Red blood cells are now made in the bone marrow. The eyelids can open and the eyelashes have grown, though sight has a way to go.

## Your body

The uterus is pushing against your diaphragm, stomach, liver and intestines, so you may feel quite uncomfortable and breathless from time to time, and the extra weight can make you very tired. Sleep gets harder. From 28 weeks, start every sleep on your side, which improves blood flow to the uterus; waking on your back is fine, just roll over.

Feeling very out of breath is worth raising, and if you have asthma, symptoms can get worse in pregnancy, so talk to your doctor about your medications.

## For the partner

This is a good week for a birth plan: where, who is there, what aids, what pain relief, what position. Sit down together and write one, simple or detailed, and know that it is a guide, not a promise. On the admin: the RSV vaccine can be booked from 28 weeks.
