## The baby

The tadpole tail has gone. There are arms, legs, tiny fingers and toes, and the ankles, wrists, knees and elbows are still forming. The jaw already holds the baby teeth. The brain is active with brain waves, and the heart has four chambers and beats at about 150 to 180 times a minute, three times faster than an adult's.

## Your body

The uterus is about the size of an orange. Clothes may be tighter and your stomach may stick out a little, sometimes from a slower gut as much as the pregnancy: progesterone slows digestion, so constipation is common.

Feeling up and down is normal. Feeling consistently sad or worried for longer than two weeks is something to raise early. Ask your midwife or OB.

## For the partner

A decision falls to both of you this week: whether to have first-trimester screening. There are two kinds, the NIPT blood test and the combined screening with the 12-week scan, both optional, and the windows are short: the combined test's blood test is between 9 and 13 weeks and its scan between 11 weeks and 13 weeks 6 days. Read up together and decide, so that both can be booked in time.
