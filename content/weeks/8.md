## The baby

Still a bit like a tadpole, but the tail is getting shorter and will disappear. The eyes and nose are visible, the inner ear and tongue are developing, and there are hands at the end of the arm buds with fingers starting to grow; the arms and legs are still cartilage. For now the baby draws its nutrients from the yolk sac, while the placenta builds and takes over from about 10 weeks.

## Your body

The uterus has grown to the size of a tennis ball and presses on the bladder, so you may be in the toilet more often. This is the week morning sickness is often at its worst. Some people are glowing; others are tired and emotional, and mood swings are normal. Gums may bleed more easily, and a lot of vomiting is hard on teeth.

If you are feeling very anxious or down, that is worth saying out loud.

## For the partner

The worst week of the sickness, for many. Keep the cooking smells away, keep bland food within reach, and do not take the moods personally. If the GP visit still is not done, this is the week to make it happen. The flu vaccine is free at any stage of pregnancy, so it is worth asking about at that visit, and a dentist appointment is a good one to book now.
