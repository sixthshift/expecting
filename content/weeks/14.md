## The baby

The organs have formed and the face is more recognisable, with a longer neck and fully developed eyes and eyelids, kept shut for now. Reflexes are working: touch the hands or feet and they would curl. Tiny nails are growing, and the spleen has started making red blood cells, a job the bone marrow takes over by the third trimester.

## Your body

Many people start to feel more energetic now, and any morning sickness is hopefully on its way out. The bump may be more noticeable, breasts may be getting larger and may already be making colostrum, a thick, sticky early milk. New moles or skin tags are common and nothing to worry about.

Sleep gets harder as the bump grows; side-lying with a pillow between the knees and under the bump is what the source suggests. Ask your midwife or OB.

## For the partner

Energy often comes back about now, so this is a good stretch for the two of you. The source says any physical activity is better than none, and that exercise is good for mood, so a walk together is an easy one. For sleep it suggests a pillow between the knees and another under the bump.
