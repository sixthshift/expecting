## The baby

Burrowed into the wall of the uterus and now called an embryo, with the foundations for all the major organs in place. The brain and spinal column are starting to form, as an open groove called the neural tube, and the head is much larger than the rest of the body because the brain and face are growing so fast. Blood vessels are forming, blood is circulating, and the heart usually starts beating around now.

## Your body

This is the week most people start to wonder. Your period is missed but may feel as though it is about to start. Larger, sore breasts, tiredness, some nausea and needing the toilet more often are all common.

A pregnancy test works from the day after a missed period. A GP can confirm it with a blood test and talk through an early dating scan and the models of care. Ask your GP.

## For the partner

Finding out, planned or not, can be exciting and frightening at the same time, for both of you. The admin starts now, and it is small: a GP appointment to confirm the pregnancy and to hear about the models of maternity care available, and to ask about an early dating scan. Book it, and go together if you can.
