Child and family health nurses — called maternal and child health nurses in some states — are registered nurses with extra training in babies and young children. The service is free, and they check weight, growth and development from birth to school age.

Usually the hospital or midwife tells your local child health service about the baby, and a nurse contacts you to book the first visit, **1 to 4 weeks after the birth**, often at home. If no one has called, ring the service or ask the hospital.

You'll also get the baby's **health record book**. Its colour depends on where you live: blue in NSW, the ACT, SA and Tasmania; red in Queensland; green in Victoria; purple in WA; yellow in the NT. Take it to every check-up and vaccination.
