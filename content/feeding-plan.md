Breastfeeding, formula and a mix of the two (mixed or combination feeding) are all ways to feed a baby well. Feeding doesn't always go to plan, so it's worth working out a backup before the birth, while you have time to think.

- **Who to call** for feeding help once you're home: the hospital's lactation service, your child health nurse, or the Australian Breastfeeding Association's helpline, **1800 686 268** (free, any time).
- **A breast pump:** whether you're likely to need one, and whether to buy or hire.
- **Bottles:** one or two to try first. Babies can be fussy about teats, so don't stock up on one brand — or one formula — until you know what yours will take.
- **Formula:** where you'd get it at short notice, if you need it.
- **Sterilising:** how you'll do it. Bottles and teats are sterilised before each use until the baby is 12 months old.

What matters most is a fed baby and a supported parent. Ask your midwife or OB.
