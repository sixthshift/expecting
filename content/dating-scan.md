A dating scan is an early ultrasound that measures the baby to work out how far along the pregnancy is and estimate the due date. Early on, babies of the same age are all about the same size, so it's the most accurate time to date a pregnancy.

It's often done between 6 and 8 weeks, though timing varies. It can also confirm whether there's more than one baby, check the pregnancy is in the uterus, and look at the placenta. If you're also having a nuchal translucency scan (see first-trimester screening), the two can sometimes be combined at 11 to 13 weeks.

Medicare covers part of the cost; some clinics charge a gap. Partners can usually come along. Ask your midwife or OB.
