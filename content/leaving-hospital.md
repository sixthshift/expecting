The stay can be shorter than you expect — public hospitals often send you home within a day or two, with a midwife visiting at home in the first days. So it pays to know what you want to ask before the midwife's next round, and to write it down.

Some of the baby's checks may happen after you leave, depending on when you're discharged, so find out which are done and which still need booking. Before you go, make sure you have:

- the baby's **health record book**
- the **discharge paperwork**, and the **Newborn Child Declaration** from the Parent Pack
- the phone numbers for the **ward and your midwife**
- the car seat fitted, and an easy going-home outfit for the baby
