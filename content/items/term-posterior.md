A posterior baby is headfirst with the back of their head towards your back. The ideal position for labour is anterior, back of the head at the front of your belly.

The position often changes as labour progresses. If it doesn't, you can sometimes still give birth without help, or your doctor may help turn the baby's head or assist with a vacuum or forceps. A head position that isn't ideal can lead to a longer labour and potential complications.
