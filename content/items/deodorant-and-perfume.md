No need to change. MotherSafe says most cosmetics and skincare products are considered safe to use in pregnancy. Neither source we checked names deodorant, antiperspirant or perfume, so there is no specific guidance on them.

## Why

MotherSafe says cosmetics and over-the-counter skin products generally contain ingredients that are unlikely to be harmful in pregnancy. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby agrees that most products you apply to your skin are not likely to be harmful, though some contain chemicals that can pass through the skin.

## What to do

- **Use the general rule.** Pregnancy, Birth and Baby recommends avoiding products that aren't essential or haven't been proven safe in pregnancy or breastfeeding. Ask yourself whether you really need the product.
- **Check with your doctor or pharmacist** if you're concerned about a particular product.
