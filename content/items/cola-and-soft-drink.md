Yes, in limited amounts. Cola counts toward the 200 mg of caffeine a day recommended as the most in pregnancy.

## Why

A 375 mL can of cola has up to about 49 mg of caffeine. Cola also often contains a lot of sugar, and sugar-sweetened drinks are on its list of foods to limit.

## What to do

Count cola with your coffee and tea, and check the label.
