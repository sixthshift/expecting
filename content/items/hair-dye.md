Yes. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says you can safely colour your hair during pregnancy, and that the idea it harms your baby is a myth. The chemicals in hair dye aren't easily absorbed through your scalp, so harm to your baby is unlikely.

## Why

MotherSafe says the exposure to an unborn baby is minimal because dyes are applied directly to the hair and scalp rather than swallowed, that treating hair is common in pregnancy and no harmful outcomes have been noted. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby's cosmetics page adds that hair dye contains chemicals that can be harmful, but your baby would have very little exposure, and there is no clear evidence of harm.

## What to do

- **If you'd feel better waiting,** you can delay until after the first trimester (12 weeks), as some people do. That is a choice, not a rule.
- **To lower your exposure,** use semi-permanent vegetable dye, leave the dye on for the minimum recommended time, and go to a hairdresser rather than colouring at home.
- **At home,** open windows, wear gloves and wash your hands before eating.
- **If you're a hairdresser,** there is no research suggesting that working with hair dye harms your baby. Wear gloves and work in a well-ventilated salon. Masks may not fully protect you from breathing in chemicals.
