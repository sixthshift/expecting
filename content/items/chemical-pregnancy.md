A chemical pregnancy is a very early miscarriage, one that normally ends before 5 weeks. More often than not there's a positive home pregnancy test, and then bleeding starts within a few days. This early, the bleeding can be mistaken for a period.

## It is a miscarriage

Even though it happens so early, a chemical pregnancy can still bring great sadness, for both of you. There's no right or wrong way to feel after a miscarriage, and you and your partner may grieve differently, each at your own pace. Most of the time a miscarriage happens because of a problem with the baby's development, not because of anything you did or didn't do.

Talking to someone can help. The services that understand early loss are in [Who to call after a loss](loss-support-services), and what comes next is in [After a miscarriage](after-a-miscarriage).

## When to get help

Call triple zero (000) or go to your nearest hospital emergency department immediately if you have heavy vaginal bleeding (soaking 1 or more pads in an hour, or bleeding you can't catch or contain), severe pain in your abdomen, pain in the tip of your shoulder, smelly discharge from the vagina, a fever, or you feel faint, dizzy or unwell.

If you're bleeding and none of those apply, what to do is in [I think I'm having a miscarriage](miscarriage-signs-what-to-do).
