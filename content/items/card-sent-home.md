If you're in early labour and have been checked at the hospital, you may be advised to go home for a while. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says to try not to be disappointed, and to accept that this is a common practice in the early stages of labour, which can often be long and tiring. It's worth knowing before the day so it doesn't catch you off guard.
