Yes. Hairdressers and nail salon workers are exposed to chemicals for longer than clients, but most studies show no significant increase in the risk of birth defects or other pregnancy complications.

## Why

It still makes sense to keep your exposure as low as you can, with ordinary workplace health and safety measures.

## What to do

- **Ventilate**: open windows, and work in a well-ventilated salon (Pregnancy, Birth and Baby).
- **Wear gloves.**
- **Wash your hands before eating.**
- **Store and dispose of chemicals safely.**
- **Don't count on a mask.** Most masks don't stop you breathing in chemicals, though they may stop dust; ventilation is what helps.
- **Spray tans**: they use higher concentrations of the tanning chemical, so if you give them for work, use protection such as nose plugs or a mask so you don't breathe it in.
- **Avoid long periods on your feet and long hours** (MotherSafe).
