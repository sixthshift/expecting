The Apgar score checks your baby's health soon after birth, at 1 minute and 5 minutes and, if needed, 10 minutes. It scores heart rate, reflexes and responsiveness, muscle tone, breathing and skin colour, each from 0 to 2, for a total out of 10. A score of 7 or more at 5 minutes shows your baby is coping well. Below 7 is a low score, and your baby will most likely need help with breathing and closer monitoring.
