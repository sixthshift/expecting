Fine, with breaks. The Royal Women's Hospital advises avoiding prolonged standing in pregnancy, and MotherSafe advises hairdressers and salon workers to avoid long periods on their feet and long hours.

## Why

Standing for long periods, especially in hot weather, lets fluid collect in your ankles and feet, and swelling is more noticeable by the end of the day. You're also more likely to feel faint if you stand for too long, and the Royal Women's suggests avoiding prolonged standing to ease backache and haemorrhoids.

## What to do

- **Sit down and raise your feet** if you've been standing, and stretch if you've been sitting (SafeWork NSW).
- **Take regular breaks** and rest often with your feet up.
- **Wear comfortable, loose shoes.**
- **At the first sign of faintness**, sit or lie down.
- **Talk to your employer** about your shifts: physically exhausting work is one of the risks they must reassess in pregnancy.
- **Tell your doctor or midwife** if your ankles, feet or fingers are very swollen, the swelling isn't relieved by rest, or you notice it elsewhere: swelling can be a sign of high blood pressure or pre-eclampsia. Contact them too if you're often dizzy or fainting.
