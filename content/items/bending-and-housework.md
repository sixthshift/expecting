Bending and squatting aren't singled out as a problem. Backache is, and most people get it at some stage of pregnancy; the advice for it includes easing off the housework.

## Why

Backache is common in pregnancy. The Royal Women's Hospital says posture is affected by the growing baby, hormones loosen your ligaments, and you hold more water in your tissues. It's often worse at night, which can make sleeping harder, especially in the later stages of pregnancy.

## What to do

If your back aches:

- **Avoid heavy lifting and housework** if you can, and avoid long periods of standing.
- **Take breaks** during the day.
- **Rest with your legs up**, or elevated, often.
