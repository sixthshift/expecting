Yes, if you eat it hot. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says hot takeaway chicken is safe to eat if you eat it immediately. The NSW Food Authority puts it under "eat with caution": buy it freshly cooked and eat it while it's hot.

## Why

With a shop-bought chook you usually don't know how long it's been cooked or at what temperature it has been kept, and handling can bring in contamination. Keeping the time it's stored short lowers the chance of bacteria growing. Cold cooked chicken, the kind sliced into sandwiches, is a different matter: it's on the "do not eat" list because of listeria.

## What to do

- **Buy it freshly cooked**, and eat it while it's hot.
- **Leftovers** go in the fridge and get used within a day of cooking. NSW says reheat them to at least 60 °C.
- **Stuffing from inside a chicken** is out unless it's cooked separately and eaten hot: undercooked poultry stuffing can carry salmonella, E. coli, campylobacter and listeria.
- **Cooking at home?** Cook chicken thoroughly, to at least 75 °C, and eat it hot.
