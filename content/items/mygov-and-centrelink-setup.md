Most people can claim Parental Leave Pay and Family Tax Benefit as early as 3 months before the due date, online. Before you can, you need the accounts in place, and if you've never dealt with Centrelink, that takes a few steps.

## What you need

- **A myGov account.** You need your own email address to create one; it can't be shared with another myGov account.
- **A Centrelink Customer Reference Number (CRN).** If you're a new Centrelink customer, you need one before you can claim online.
- **Centrelink linked to myGov.** If you've claimed before, you link it with your CRN. If you haven't, or don't know your CRN, you prove your identity online in myGov: choose **View and link services**, then **Centrelink**, and enter details from 2 acceptable identity documents and your Medicare card, plus some other personal details. Once you've proved who you are, Centrelink is linked and you get a CRN.

## Proving your identity

You can prove who you are online, over the phone, or in person at a service centre. Online, a strong myID Digital Identity lets you link Centrelink and prove your identity in one go. If you get a CRN by entering your document details instead, you can start a claim, but **before you can submit it you'll need to visit a service centre** with an acceptable photo identity document. If you can't prove your identity online, call the Centrelink families line.

## Once it's set up

You claim in your Centrelink online account under **Apply for Family Assistance (including Paid Parental Leave)**. You can manage things through the Express Plus mobile apps, online accounts through myGov, or phone self service, and track a claim in the Express Plus Centrelink app.
