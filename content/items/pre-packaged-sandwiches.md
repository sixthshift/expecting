Best not. The NSW Food Authority puts pre-prepared or pre-packaged sandwiches and wraps under "do not eat (high risk)" in pregnancy.

## Why

Processing and storage increase the risk of listeria, which can grow even in the fridge. In pregnancy listeria can cause miscarriage, premature birth or stillbirth.

The fillings add to it. Cold meats from deli counters and sandwich bars, cold cooked chicken, soft cheeses, pre-prepared salad and chilled seafood such as smoked salmon and cooked prawns are each on the higher-risk list on their own. Store-bought sushi from the fridge is too.

## What to do

- **Choose something freshly made and hot**, like a hot meal. FSANZ says it's safest to eat only freshly prepared food.
- **Make your own** when you can, with safer fillings: hard cheese, egg cooked until firm, canned tuna or salmon, or freshly cooked meat, and washed salad.
- **Already eaten one?** Listeria symptoms can be mild, like the flu: fever, headache, tiredness, aches. FSANZ says to seek medical attention immediately if you have them while pregnant.
