Multiple Birth Allowance is for people who get Family Tax Benefit (FTB) Part A and are expecting or have had **triplets, quadruplets or more**. It's not for twins.

## How much

$203.70 a fortnight for triplets, or $271.32 a fortnight for quadruplets or more.

## How to get it

There's no separate claim: Services Australia assesses you when you claim FTB, which most people can do up to 3 months before the due date. It's paid the same way you choose to get your FTB, so you can take all, part or none of it fortnightly, with the rest paid when your payments are balanced after the financial year ends.

## Twins and other multiples

Twins don't get Multiple Birth Allowance, but other help is counted per child:

- **Parental Leave Pay** can only be paid for one child in each multiple birth. If you're sharing it, you and the other parent need to claim it for the same child.
- **Newborn Upfront Payment and Newborn Supplement** can be paid for the other child or children, if you're eligible.
