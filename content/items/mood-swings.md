## What's usual

Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says it's normal for you, or your partner if you have one, to experience ups and downs, and that mixed emotions are a normal and necessary part of getting ready to become a parent.

**If you're the partner:** your partner may have mood swings during pregnancy. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says to try not to take it personally.

## Call 000 or go to emergency

If your life is in danger, call triple zero (000).

If you're worried you might hurt yourself or your baby, you can call Perinatal Anxiety and Depression Australia (PANDA) on 1300 726 306, Lifeline on 13 11 14, or Beyond Blue on 1300 22 4636.

## See your doctor or midwife

Pregnancy, Birth and Baby lists these as possible signs of anxiety or depression: panic attacks, persistent worry (such as fears for the baby's wellbeing), obsessive or compulsive behaviours, abrupt mood swings, feeling constantly sad or low or crying for no obvious reason, and being nervous, on edge or panicky. If symptoms last for more than 2 weeks, or if you're worried, talk to your GP or midwife.
