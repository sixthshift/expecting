Parental Leave Pay is a flexible payment that can be shared between parents. If you have a partner, some of the days are reserved for them; the rest you decide between you.

## How sharing works

- **The birth mother approves.** The partner must claim Parental Leave Pay for themselves to get any days shared with them. When they claim, Services Australia sends a task to the birth mother's Centrelink online account through myGov, and she completes it to approve how many days she's sharing. The partner can only claim the days she's approved.
- **Claim only your own days.** When you claim, include only the days you're claiming for yourself, so the two of you don't go over the maximum. For example, if you're eligible for 130 days and want to share 40 with the other parent, you can't claim more than 90 for yourself.
- **Both of you need to qualify.** To share, the birth mother must meet the work test for the partner to be eligible, even if she claims no days herself. To get the full number of days, you both need to be eligible and both claim. If only one of you is eligible, the reserved days limit still applies.

## How you can take the days

You can take your days as a single block, several smaller blocks, single days, or a mix. You can't use any days before the birth. Days you haven't planned yet are held in a balance, to use later or share. They must be used within 2 years of the birth, and any left then are forfeited.

You can take up to **20 days at the same time** as each other. There are some exemptions to this limit.

## Changing your plans

After you've claimed, you can change your nominated days, give or remove approval to share, and claim days, all in myGov. You can't change days that have already passed. If your employer is paying your Parental Leave Pay, changing your days may affect that. If your claim hasn't been assessed yet, call the Centrelink families line on 136 150.
