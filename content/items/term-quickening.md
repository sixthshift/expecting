Quickening is another name for the first movements you feel from your baby. They are often described as fluttering (like butterflies in your tummy), bubbling, swishing, rolling, tumbling or tiny kicks. You will usually start to feel your baby moving at around 20 weeks, and the movements become more noticeable as the pregnancy goes on.
