Around week 14 you might notice new moles or skin tags developing on your body. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says these are nothing to worry about, but chat to your doctor or midwife if they're bothering you.

Moles can get larger and darker in pregnancy, but they are not more likely to become malignant. It is important to check your moles for changes in shape, size and colour: see your doctor if you notice any changes.
