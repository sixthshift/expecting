Research suggests more than 80% of expectant and new parents have intrusive thoughts from time to time: unwanted, upsetting images or impulses, often violent or disturbing. Having one doesn't mean you'll act on it. They're usually a sign of overwhelm and anxiety, not usually an indicator of psychosis, and the distress they cause is a strong indicator they're driven by anxiety and nothing more serious.

It can help to label it ("that's an intrusive thought"), let it pass rather than push it away, and remind yourself it isn't who you are. In more severe cases, intrusive thoughts may be a sign of perinatal depression and anxiety, and persistent, obsessive thoughts could be obsessive-compulsive disorder; then it's recommended to get professional support.
