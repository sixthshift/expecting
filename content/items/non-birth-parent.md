Expecting never assumes a dad: in this app you're the partner, and every partner entry is written for you too. Gidget writes for dads and non-birthing partners together, and the experience is shared: joy alongside feeling helpless when your partner is uncomfortable, worry about bonding, the pull to be "the strong one", and finding it hard to ask for help. Non-birthing partners can have perinatal depression and anxiety too, before or after the birth.

## The particular things

- **Care that doesn't get you.** Rainbow families often meet care providers with limited knowledge of their experience. You deserve care that recognises your family without assumptions and uses the terms you prefer.
- **Explaining yourself.** Comments, questions and having to explain your family can be tiring. You don't need to explain your family to everyone; it's okay to set boundaries.
- **Your role.** Keep talking with your partner about feeding, leave, night wakes, money and appointments, and how each of you wants your role recognised.
- **Bonding.** It doesn't always happen instantly, especially if you're not genetically related to the baby. It builds through everyday care: feeding, cuddles, skin-to-skin, settling, talking and singing.

## The birth certificate

Who is recorded is set by your state or territory's Births, Deaths and Marriages registry. In Victoria, for example, the birth mother's female partner can be added when the birth is registered, and if you both consented to donor treatment you're generally both recorded. Check your own state's registry.

## If there's a loss

Your grief is real and valid whatever your physical involvement in the pregnancy. Miscarriage Australia has a section for LGBTIQA+ partners, including what to do if family, friends or work aren't supportive.

## Support

If worry, low mood or overwhelm lasts more than two weeks or makes daily life harder, talk to your GP. QLife offers free, anonymous LGBTIQ+ peer support and referral by phone and webchat, and the Gidget Foundation offers perinatal psychological therapy.
