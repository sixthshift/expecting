Vitamin D helps you absorb calcium and helps your baby's bones develop. A blood test checks your level. Services differ on who gets one: some list it among the tests at your first visit, others offer it if you're likely to be low (little time outdoors, darker skin, or skin mostly covered). Ask your midwife whether you need one.

If your level is low, speak to your midwife or doctor. They may suggest a vitamin D tablet, and you may be advised to keep taking it after the pregnancy.
