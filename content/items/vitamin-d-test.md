Vitamin D helps you absorb calcium and helps your baby's bones develop. A blood test checks your level, and it's listed among the routine antenatal tests at your first antenatal visit.

If your level is low, speak to your midwife or doctor. They may suggest a vitamin D tablet, and you may be advised to keep taking it after the pregnancy.
