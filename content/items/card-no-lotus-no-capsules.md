There is no evidence that lotus birth (keeping the cord attached so your baby stays connected to the placenta) has any health benefits, and it may increase your chance of complications. You may still choose it for spiritual or cultural reasons.

There are no proven health benefits of eating your placenta or having it encapsulated, though you may choose to for cultural, spiritual or personal reasons. It's your choice what you do with your placenta, and in most cases it's safe to take it home if you follow basic health and safety rules. If you bury it, bury it deeply enough that animals won't dig it up.
