Meconium is your baby's first few poos: sticky, thick and dark green, in the first few days after birth.

Sometimes it's passed before birth, such as during labour, and turns the amniotic fluid green. It may be a sign your baby is in distress, so they'll be monitored more closely. If your waters break and the fluid is green, brown or blood coloured, call your doctor, midwife or birthing unit immediately for advice, or go to hospital.
