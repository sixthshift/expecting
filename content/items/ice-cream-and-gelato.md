Yes, for ice cream from a tub or packet and for gelato: you can eat frozen ice cream and gelato. The NSW Food Authority says packaged frozen ice cream is okay to eat, kept and eaten frozen.

## Why

Soft-serve ice cream is stored at a temperature that allows listeria to grow. The NSW Food Authority lists it as "do not eat" (high risk), because machines may be inadequately cleaned. It also lists fried ice cream as "do not eat" (high risk): the batter may contain egg and carry Salmonella, and frying may not reach adequate internal temperatures.

## What to do

Choose packaged ice cream and gelato, and keep it frozen until you eat it. Skip soft serve from a machine, and fried ice cream.
