In most pregnancies the waters break during the first stage of labour, but for some they break before labour starts. The fluid may gush or trickle, it usually isn't painful, and you may not feel anything at all. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says that if they break before labour, you should contact your midwife, doctor or birth centre. If they break before 37 weeks, contact your midwife or doctor and go to the hospital. If the fluid is green, brown or blood coloured, it says to call your doctor, midwife or birthing unit immediately for advice, or go to hospital.

If your waters break and you've been told the baby isn't head-first, call triple zero (000): there's a higher risk of cord prolapse.
