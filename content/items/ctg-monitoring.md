A **CTG** (cardiotocograph) records the baby's heart rate and your contractions continuously, through 2 round sensors held on your tummy by soft belts. It's sometimes used to check the baby's health in pregnancy: before turning a breech baby (ECV), for example, for 20 to 40 minutes. Your doctor or midwife looks at whether the heart rate is too fast, too slow, or responding as expected. Your consent is always needed.
