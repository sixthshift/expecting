## What's usual

Nobody expects a shoulder to be a pregnancy sign, but pain in the very tip of one shoulder is one of the signs of an ectopic pregnancy: a pregnancy growing outside the uterus, usually in a fallopian tube. If the tube tears or bursts, it can cause serious internal bleeding.

It can come on its own, or with other signs of an ectopic pregnancy: spotting or bleeding, tummy or back pain, a fast heart rate, or dizziness. These signs usually come between weeks 4 and 12.

## Call 000 or go to emergency

If you have shoulder-tip pain while pregnant, on its own or with any of those other signs, go to the nearest emergency department or call triple zero (000) for an ambulance.
