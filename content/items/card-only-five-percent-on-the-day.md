Your due date is only an estimate, and the exact timing of your baby's birth is unpredictable. Only about 5 out of every 100 babies are born on their actual due date.

Pregnancy generally lasts about 40 weeks, and babies are considered full term if they are born at any time from 37 weeks of pregnancy, with most born between 37 and 42 weeks. An accurate due date still matters: it helps your health team monitor your pregnancy and your baby's growth.
