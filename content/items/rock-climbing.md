Ask first. None of the sources listed here mentions climbing or bouldering by name, so this answer comes from their general rules rather than a specific verdict.

## Why

The general rules all point at the risks climbing carries:

- **Falls.** Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says to avoid sports that could cause you to fall, and lists activities with a risk of tripping or falling among those that aren't safe in pregnancy. The national guidelines say to avoid any activity with a high risk of falling.
- **Balance.** As the bump grows your centre of gravity moves, which makes a fall more likely.
- **Looser joints.** Pregnancy hormones loosen your ligaments, so joint injuries are more likely.


## What to do

- **Talk to your doctor or midwife** before you climb, and tell them exactly what kind of climbing you do.
- **Stop if you feel unsteady** or uncomfortable.
- The national guidelines' rule: speak with your doctor about the type of activity that best suits you.
