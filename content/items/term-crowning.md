Crowning is when the baby's head is visible from the external vaginal opening. In the second stage you're likely to feel stretching and burning in your vagina.

At the end of the second stage your midwife or doctor will help ease the baby out. With your consent they may apply gentle pressure to the baby's head, so it emerges slowly, and offer a warm compress on the perineum, to reduce the chance of tearing.
