A cardiotocograph (CTG) records your baby's heart rate and your contractions continuously. Two round discs containing sensors are held on your tummy with a soft belt. It's sometimes used to help check your baby's health during pregnancy, and in labour. Some machines use wires that may limit your movement; you can ask if a wireless CTG is available.
