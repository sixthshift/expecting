The Apgar score rates your baby's skin colour, heart rate, reflexes, muscle tone and breathing at 1 and 5 minutes after birth, and sometimes at 10 minutes. Each is scored 0 to 2, out of a total of 10. Blue hands and feet are normal for a newborn, which is why the highest score is usually 9. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says 7 or more at 5 minutes shows your baby is coping well, and a lower score means extra help, most likely with breathing. It reflects your baby's condition at birth and isn't intended to predict how their nervous system and brain will develop.
