Yes. Honey and lemon is a recommended sore-throat remedy in pregnancy.

## Why

Pregnancy, Birth and Baby lists "lemon and honey products" among the things you can use for a sore throat in pregnancy, and MotherSafe says sore throats or coughs may be soothed by warm drinks with honey and lemon.

## What to do

Try a warm honey and lemon drink for a sore throat or cough, alongside gargling warm salty water or sucking lozenges.
