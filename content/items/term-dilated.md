To dilate is for the cervix to open during labour. In pregnancy your cervix is closed, and contractions thin and open it so your baby can be born. It's measured in centimetres.

The first stage ends when the cervix is fully open at 10 centimetres, and the second stage, when you push, begins. The active phase of the first stage begins when the cervix is completely thinned (effaced) and open to 4 centimetres. The glossary instead describes the latent phase as the cervix thinning and opening to around 4 to 6 centimetres.
