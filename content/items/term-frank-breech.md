In a breech presentation your baby's feet or bottom are closest to your birth canal. In a frank (extended) breech, your baby's legs are straight up in front of their body, with their feet near their face. In a complete (flexed) breech, your baby is in a sitting position with their legs crossed in front of their body and their feet near their bottom.
