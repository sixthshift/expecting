## Why

Different policies give different levels of pregnancy cover. You may only be covered until week 32. Some cover a newborn too, which matters if the baby comes early while you're away. For cover during pregnancy you may need to pay extra, or show medical evidence.

## What to do

Before you pay for the trip, ask the insurer whether the policy covers:

- pregnancy, and until what stage
- twins or more, a high risk of complications, and an IVF pregnancy or fertility treatment
- complications or a premature birth while you're away, and the baby if you go into labour
- medical evacuation, if you're going somewhere remote
- cancelling the trip because of the pregnancy or the birth

**Travelling in Australia**, your Medicare card or private health insurance covers your care, but travel insurance is still worth having for a cancelled trip or lost bags.

**Overseas**, Australia has reciprocal health care agreements with some countries; check Services Australia for what they cover before you go. You should still get travel insurance.
