Haemoglobin is the protein in your red blood cells that carries oxygen around your body. You need enough iron, vitamin B12 and folate to make it, and anaemia means your blood doesn't have enough healthy red blood cells or haemoglobin. Your haemoglobin level is tested at your first antenatal visit, and may be tested again around your third trimester.
