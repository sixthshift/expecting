Yes. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says many cleaning products are safe to use in pregnancy, but you should always check the label for safety warnings and follow the directions on it.

## Why

Most chemicals you come across in daily life won't harm your baby, usually because the amount is so small that you don't need to worry. If you breathe in or swallow chemicals, though, they can enter your bloodstream and pass to your baby through the placenta. Large amounts can affect your baby and your pregnancy, and generally it's only long-term exposure to large amounts that is harmful. Being exposed once is very unlikely to cause harm.

## What to do

- **Protect yourself:** wear gloves and a face mask, and avoid breathing in fumes. Make sure you have plenty of fresh air, and keep your home well ventilated.
- **Pass it on.** If a task involves chemicals, ask someone else to do it.
- **Read and follow** the instructions on the packet, store chemicals safely with their safety caps on, and wash your hands regularly.
- **If you're worried** you've been in contact with a chemical, call the Poisons Information Hotline on 13 11 26.
