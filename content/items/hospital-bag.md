Check with the hospital or birth centre what you need to bring; they may have their own list.

- **Paperwork:** Medicare card, health fund details (if you have them), antenatal card, birth plan (if you have one), hospital paperwork.
- **For the birth parent:** an old T-shirt for labour, going-home clothes, maternity bras, maternity pads, sleepwear, a warm layer, toiletries, slippers or thongs, and your own pillow if you'd like one.
- **For the baby:** nappies (newborns go through 8 to 10 a day), wipes, a blanket, baby clothes, a onesie, a hat, booties or socks.
- **For the support partner:** a change of clothes, toiletries, a pillow. Snacks and drinks, phone chargers and a little cash suit everyone.

Leave anything precious, like jewellery or money, at home; a small amount of cash could be useful. Make sure the car seat is properly fitted. Have a plan for who'll look after any other children, and someone on standby for any pets.
