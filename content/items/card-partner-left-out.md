MensLine says that if you feel your partner has a greater role in caring for your child and you're on the outside, there are ways to get more involved. Ask your partner what help they need, and help with housework, cooking and shopping.

During the pregnancy, Pregnancy, Birth and Baby suggests going to antenatal appointments, looking at baby equipment together, and talking about how you're both feeling. Later you can attend antenatal classes and tour the place where the baby will be born.
