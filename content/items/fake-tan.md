Creams and lotions are fine. It's best to avoid spray tans.

## Why

Self-tanning creams and gels contain dihydroxyacetone. MotherSafe says absorption through the skin is thought to be very low, so self-tanning is considered safe in pregnancy. Spray tans use the same ingredient at higher concentrations (Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says they have higher levels of chemicals than self-tanning lotions), and breathing it in is the concern.

## What to do

- **Lotions and creams:** Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says you can use them while pregnant.
- **Spray tans:** best avoided. If you give spray tans for work, use protective measures, such as nose plugs or a mask, to avoid breathing it in.
