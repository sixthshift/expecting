Pregnancy, Birth and Baby writes gestational age as weeks plus days, with the example 38+1, meaning 38 weeks and 1 day. The Australian Institute of Health and Welfare defines the duration of pregnancy in completed weeks, counted from the first day of the last menstrual period, or by ultrasound or clinical assessment. So "28 weeks" on a record means 28 whole weeks have finished, and it stays that way until the next week is complete.
