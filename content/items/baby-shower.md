Most showers are held between about 28 and 34 weeks: late enough that the pregnancy is well along, early enough that the birth parent is comfortable and the baby isn't likely to arrive first.

**Who hosts.** Traditionally a close friend or family member, but there's no rule. Some couples host their own, or have a joint shower for both families' friends.

**The registry.** It saves everyone guessing and stops you getting five of the same thing. Put the big things on it (the pram, the car seat, the cot) for people to go in on together, alongside small things. Nappies in a few sizes are always welcome.

**Invitations.** Send them about four weeks ahead, with the registry. Note any food you're avoiding so the host can plan around it.

**A second baby?** Some families have a smaller "sprinkle" instead, for the things you'll need again: nappies, clothes for the other sex, a second car seat.

Afterwards, keep a list of who gave what, for the thank-you notes.
