Yes. Vitamin B12 comes mainly from animal foods and supports the development of your baby's nervous system, so a vegetarian or vegan pregnancy usually needs a supplement.

## Why

The sources word it differently. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says your midwife or doctor may suggest B12 if you follow a vegetarian or vegan diet. MotherSafe says vegetarian and vegan mothers should take B12 while pregnant and breastfeeding. The Royal Women's Hospital says that if you don't eat animal foods you will need to take it.

A lack of B12, like a lack of folate, can also cause anaemia in pregnancy.

## What to do

- **Ask your midwife or doctor** about a B12 supplement, and whether a dietitian referral would help.
- **Swap in plant proteins.** Lentils, beans, tofu, eggs and soy milk can replace animal foods.
- **Watch your iron.** Legumes, nuts, wholegrains and leafy greens contain it; eat vitamin C-rich foods such as fruit, tomatoes or capsicum at the same meal to help you absorb it.
- **Calcium:** if you drink soy or other plant milks, choose one with as much calcium as cow's milk.
- **Iodine and folic acid** are recommended for everyone in pregnancy.
- **Omega-3:** oil from algae is a plant source of DHA and EPA.
