Dad and Partner Pay was a payment of up to 2 weeks for eligible fathers and partners caring for a child born or adopted before 1 July 2023. Services Australia says it was combined with Parental Leave Pay into one payment for children born or adopted from 1 July 2023, and claims closed on 30 June 2024. Older articles, and friends who had a baby a few years ago, may still describe it.

For a child born from 1 July 2026, if you have a partner on the day you claim, 20 of the 130 Parental Leave Pay days are reserved for them to use.
