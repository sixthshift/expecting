Your pelvic floor is a group of muscles that support your bladder, uterus and bowel. They form a "sling" that attaches to your pubic bone at the front and your tailbone at the back, and your urethra, vagina and anus pass through it.

It has an extra job during pregnancy, to support your growing baby. During labour and birth, the muscles need to relax and stretch to allow your baby to be born.
