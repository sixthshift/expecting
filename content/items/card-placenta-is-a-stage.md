The third stage of labour starts after your baby is born, when the placenta separates from the wall of your uterus and is delivered. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says an actively managed third stage usually takes up to 30 minutes, and a physiological (natural) one can take up to an hour; longer than that is considered prolonged. Your midwife or doctor will then check the placenta to make sure it's complete and nothing was left behind, and will need your permission to do a vaginal examination. People and families have different practices around the placenta, and your choices should be respected.
