A fetal Doppler is a handheld device your doctor or midwife may use at antenatal visits to listen to your baby's heart rate through your tummy. Home fetal heart rate monitors (sometimes called dopplers) are not approved for sale in Australia and are not recommended for use at home: they can falsely reassure you and delay urgent care. If you're worried about your baby's movements, contact your midwife, doctor or maternity service straight away; don't wait. See [The baby is moving less](baby-moving-less).
