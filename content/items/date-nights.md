Once the baby arrives, a night out takes a babysitter, a feed schedule and a lot of planning. Before then it takes a booking. A few nights as two are worth putting in the calendar while it's that simple.

**When.** The middle months are often the easiest. Later on, long evenings and late finishes can be harder going, so plan the bigger nights earlier and keep the later ones close to home.

**Ideas:**

- The restaurant you keep meaning to try, booked for an early sitting.
- A film, a gig, a show or a comedy night; pick seats on the aisle.
- A picnic, a walk somewhere new, or a night at the beach.
- A class together: cooking, pottery, dancing.
- A night in, properly done: phones away, food you both love.
- A night away, if a babymoon isn't on the cards.

**Make it easy:**

- Book ahead and choose places with good seating and a toilet nearby.
- Check the menu has plenty she'll enjoy.
- Take turns planning, and keep at least one a surprise.
- Talk about something other than the baby, at least for a while.

Plan one for after the birth too, with the grandparents or a trusted friend lined up to babysit, so there's something on the calendar to look forward to.
