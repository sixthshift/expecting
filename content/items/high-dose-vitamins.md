Avoid them. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says it's best to check with your midwife, doctor or dietitian before taking any vitamin, mineral or other supplement.

## Why

Your body only needs a small amount of each vitamin and mineral, and more isn't always better: taking more than you need can sometimes cause harm. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says high doses of vitamins A, C and E can be dangerous, and it's best not to take them as supplements in pregnancy.

Very high doses of vitamin C can cause stomach cramps and diarrhoea.

## What to do

- **Get vitamin C from food.** It's in a wide range of fruit and vegetables, and a balanced diet gives you all you need. It may also help you absorb iron from your food.
- **Check with your doctor** before taking any supplement, just as you would a medicine. Your pharmacist, midwife or dietitian can help too.
- **The supplements recommended for everyone** in pregnancy are folic acid and iodine; some people also need vitamin D or iron.
