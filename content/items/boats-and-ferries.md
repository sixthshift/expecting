Yes for short trips. MotherSafe says travelling by boat or ferry for short distances is generally safe in pregnancy.

## Why

Sea travel can sometimes trigger motion sickness, or nausea and vomiting. MotherSafe says that, combined with the increased risk of falls on a moving vessel, this leads many pregnant people to postpone sea travel until after the baby is born.

## What to do

- **Motion sickness medicines exist.** MotherSafe names hyoscine hydrobromide (Travacalm, Kwells), doxylamine (Restavit) and other antihistamines, but says to consult your doctor, pharmacist or MotherSafe first for the best choice for you.
- **For a cruise,** check with the cruise line. MotherSafe says most restrict travel beyond 28 weeks of pregnancy and have other requirements for pregnant people. Infection, respiratory and food-borne, is also a risk of cruising.
