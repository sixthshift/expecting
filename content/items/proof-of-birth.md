After the birth, your hospital or midwife gives you a **Parent Pack**. Inside is a **Newborn Child Declaration**. It's a small piece of paper that does a lot of work, so look after it.

**What to do with it:**

- Make sure your doctor or midwife completes the **Proof of Birth declaration on the back page**.
- If you have a Centrelink Customer Reference Number and a Medicare card, write those numbers on the back page too.

**What it's for.** Services Australia needs proof of birth before it can finalise a claim for Parental Leave Pay or Family Tax Benefit. The back page is that proof:

- to finalise a **pre-birth claim**. If you claimed before the birth, you need to tell Services Australia within 56 days of the baby coming into your care, with the baby's details and proof of birth.
- for a **new claim** for Family Tax Benefit (including the Newborn Upfront Payment and Newborn Supplement) or Parental Leave Pay.
- to **enrol your baby in Medicare**, if you're not claiming a family payment.

**What it isn't.** It isn't a claim form for payments, and it isn't the birth registration: you still must register the birth with your state or territory's registry.

**If you lose it.** It can't be reissued. You can still prove the birth with a birth certificate or certified extract, another Australian Government document showing the baby's name and date of birth, or a letter from the doctor or midwife who attended the birth (home births included). The letter must include the parents' details; the baby's name, date and place of birth; and the doctor or midwife's signature, qualifications and registration number.
