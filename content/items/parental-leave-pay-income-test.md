To get [Parental Leave Pay](parental-leave-pay), you must meet an income test, based on your adjusted taxable income.

## Which year counts

Services Australia looks at your income for the financial year before either your baby's birth or the date you lodge your claim, whichever is earlier.

## The limits

**Individual test:** an adjusted taxable income of **$186,487 or less** in 2025–26.

**Family test,** if you don't meet the individual test: you and your partner's combined adjusted taxable income of **$386,525 or less** in 2025–26. Your partner is the person you're in a relationship with on the day you claim.

If you're single, your own income is tested against the family limit.

## Each parent separately

Each parent who claims is tested in their own right, so one of you can qualify when the other doesn't. In Services Australia's own example, one partner earned $170,000 and qualified; the other earned $230,000, and with a combined $400,000 they were over the family limit, so only the first could get it.

## What to do

Check each of your adjusted taxable incomes for the year that will be tested.
