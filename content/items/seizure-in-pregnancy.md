## What's usual

A seizure (a fit) is never part of a usual pregnancy. In pregnancy it can be eclampsia: seizures caused by pre-eclampsia, a condition that raises blood pressure from 20 weeks and in the first days after the birth. It can also be epilepsy or another cause. Whatever the cause, being pregnant is one of the reasons to call an ambulance for any seizure.

## Call 000 or go to emergency

Call triple zero (000) and ask for an ambulance. Say they're pregnant, and how many weeks.

While you wait:

- stay with them until it's over, and notice how long it lasts
- move anything sharp or hard out of the way, and put something soft under their head and shoulders
- if it's safe to, roll them onto their side (the recovery position)
- don't hold them down or try to stop the jerking, and don't put anything in their mouth
- afterwards, keep them on their side, talk to them calmly, and watch their breathing; give them nothing to eat or drink
