Your doctor or midwife does the same routine health check at every visit, and adds tests and scans at set points in the pregnancy.

## The routine check

- **Blood pressure.**
- **Measuring your tummy** to check your baby's growth, from week 20 onwards. This is called fundal height.
- **Your general health and wellbeing.**
- **Your baby's heartbeat,** possibly from week 12 onwards.
- **A conversation about your baby's movements.**

Your doctor, midwife or obstetrician may suggest more tests, based on your risk factors and your situation.

## What else you can bring up

Antenatal appointments are a chance to ask questions about your pregnancy: physical symptoms, what's happening in each trimester, the birth, and caring for your baby after it. You can also get support with mental health, dietary advice, quitting smoking and avoiding alcohol, and you can talk about any problems at home.

## Urine tests

A urine test is offered if you have raised blood pressure, at the checks listed from 28 weeks (28, 29 to 34, 35 to 37, 38 to 40 and 41 weeks).

## Tests and scans at set points

Scans and extra tests come at particular weeks rather than every visit, for example the ultrasound at 18 to 22 weeks and the glucose tolerance test at 24 to 28 weeks. See how many appointments, and when.

## Your first visit is different

It's longer. As well as the routine measurements, your doctor or midwife will ask about your medical and family history, measure your height and weight, and may arrange blood and urine tests.
