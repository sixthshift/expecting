No. MotherSafe doesn't recommend herbal medicines, St John's wort included, for depression or anxiety while you're pregnant or breastfeeding.

## Why

MotherSafe's herbal medicines sheet says several small studies suggest St John's wort may be safe in pregnancy and breastfeeding, but further research is needed to confirm it. Meanwhile:

- the depression and anxiety sheet says it may have dangerous interactions with prescribed medicines; the herbal medicines sheet says it can interact with many other medicines, including some antidepressants, so caution is recommended
- most herbal medicines haven't been studied enough to know whether they're safe in pregnancy
- herbal products aren't strictly regulated, so there are no standards for ingredients or dose, and the label may not match what's inside

Depression and anxiety in pregnancy are worth treating properly. Left untreated, they carry significant risk for you and your baby, and have been linked with miscarriage, low birth weight and premature birth.

## What to do

- **See your GP or a mental health professional.** They can help coordinate treatment, which may include counselling or cognitive behavioural therapy.
- **Already on an antidepressant?** Don't stop it suddenly. Many have been studied in pregnancy without an increased risk of birth defects, and staying on a medicine that keeps you well is often advisable.
- **Already taking St John's wort?** Tell your GP, or call MotherSafe on 9382 6539 (Sydney) or 1800 647 848 (rest of NSW).
- **Support lines:** PANDA 1300 726 306, Beyond Blue 1300 22 4636.
