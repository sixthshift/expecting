A Pinard horn, also called a Pinard stethoscope or ear trumpet, is what your doctor or midwife may use at antenatal visits to listen to your baby's heart rate through your tummy. In labour they may place a Pinard or a Doppler on your abdomen and listen to the heartbeat that way.
