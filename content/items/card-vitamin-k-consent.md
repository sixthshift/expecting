It's recommended that all babies are given a vitamin K injection within a few hours of birth, and it will only be given with your permission. So someone will ask you, in those first hours.

Vitamin K helps blood clot. Newborns often don't have enough of it for their first 8 days, which puts them at risk of a rare disorder called vitamin K deficiency bleeding. It can cause a bleed in the brain and can lead to death.
