Red Nose Grief and Loss answers **1300 308 307**, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and it's free.

It's for people affected by pregnancy loss, stillbirth, or the death of a baby or child. It isn't only for the parent who carried the baby: Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says Red Nose also runs support groups for non-birthing parents.
