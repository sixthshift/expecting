How you'll feed your baby is one of the most important choices of new parenthood: breast milk, formula, or both (mixed feeding). It's worth working out the plan, and who to ask for help, before the birth, while you have time to think.

- **Who to call** for feeding help once you're home: the Australian Breastfeeding Association's helpline, **1800 686 268** (free, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week), or a Pregnancy, Birth and Baby maternal child health nurse on 1800 882 436.
- **A breast pump:** whether you might need one to express milk, and whether to buy or hire. Electric pumps can be either.
- **Bottles:** whether you'll use one, for expressed breast milk or formula.
- **Formula:** whether you'd use it, and how to choose between types.
- **Sterilising:** how you'll do it. Until the baby is 12 months old, sterilise bottles before you use them.

Ask your midwife or OB.
