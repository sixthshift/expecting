After 16 weeks, it's best to avoid exercises lying flat on your back.

## Why

The weight of the baby can press on the major blood vessels returning blood to your heart. That can make you feel faint, light-headed or sick, and can reduce blood flow to your baby.

It's the same reason you're advised to sleep on your side in the second half of pregnancy.

## What to do

- **Ask for another position.** An instructor or physiotherapist can suggest a version that doesn't have you on your back.
- **In a class,** tell the instructor how many weeks you are so they can modify the moves.
