BMI is body mass index, calculated from your height and weight. Your pre-pregnancy BMI is used to work out how much weight you should gain in pregnancy, together with a talk with your doctor or midwife. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby gives a healthy BMI as between 18.5 and 24.9, and says BMI does not necessarily indicate your health, or how fat is spread around your body.
