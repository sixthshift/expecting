During pregnancy and again after the birth, most people in Australia are screened for depression. A commonly used tool is the Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale (EPDS), a short questionnaire with a standard scoring guide.

It's asked because it's common: about 15% of pregnant people have depression or anxiety during pregnancy, and more after the birth.
