If in doubt, don't. SafeWork NSW's rule is simple: if something seems too heavy to lift, or you feel dizzy or faint, ask for help or find out if someone else can do it. None of these sources gives a weight limit.

## Why

Pregnancy hormones loosen your ligaments, which makes injuries more likely. Lifting heavy weights is on the list of things to avoid in pregnancy, and the Royal Women's Hospital suggests avoiding heavy lifting and housework if you have backache.

## What to do

- **At home**, ask for help with heavy loads and furniture.
- **At work**, heavy lifting is one of the risks your employer must reassess once you're pregnant. Changes to your duties are more likely if your job is physically demanding, and you can ask for help with posture and moving at work.
- **If your usual job isn't safe**, you're entitled to move to a safe job (see working while pregnant).
- **If you lift weights for exercise**, light weight training is fine; avoid lifting heavy weights.
