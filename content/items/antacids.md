Yes. Antacids can relieve heartburn quickly, and they are safe in pregnancy if you don't take more than the recommended dose. There are many types, so ask your pharmacist which suits you. KEMH names liquid antacids such as Gaviscon and Gastrogel as the ones to try at the recommended dose when eating differently hasn't been enough.

## Why

Heartburn is very common in pregnancy: Pregnancy, Birth and Baby gives it an 8 in 10 chance, because of hormonal changes and the uterus pressing against the stomach.

## What to do

- **Change how you eat first.** Eat smaller meals more often, eat slowly, avoid eating for 2 or 3 hours before exercising or going to bed, don't lie down right after a meal, and stay away from foods and drinks that set it off.
- **Prop yourself up.** Raise the head of your bed or sleep on at least 2 pillows. Sleeping on your left side may also help.
- **If antacids don't help,** your doctor can prescribe other medicines that are safe in pregnancy.
- **See your doctor** if heartburn is severe, persistent, becoming worse, or doesn't go away with medicine. Pre-eclampsia, a serious pregnancy condition, can cause pain under your ribs and a feeling of heartburn.
- **See your doctor immediately** if you are vomiting up blood, losing weight, have painful or difficult swallowing, feel short of breath or have chest pain.
