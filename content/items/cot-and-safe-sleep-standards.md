Your baby will spend more time in the cot than anywhere else, so it's worth getting right. You don't need the latest or most expensive model, but whatever cot you choose has to be safe: Pregnancy, Birth and Baby warns that an unsafe cot can cause injury or even death.

## The standard

Since 19 January 2026, every cot sold new in Australia must meet the mandatory safety standard for infant sleep products. A household cot meets it by meeting one of three standards: the Australian AS/NZS 2172:2013, or its US or European equivalent. The cot must also carry warnings and come with safety information (ACCC Product Safety).

Second-hand cots aren't covered by the mandatory standard, unless they're imported. Red Nose's advice is to check that a second-hand cot meets the standard anyway: it's noted in the instructions, and often on a label. If you're in any doubt about a cot's safety, don't use it.

## Checking the cot

Take a tape measure. Make sure:

- the spaces between the bars are between 5 cm and 9.5 cm
- there are no gaps between 3 cm and 5 cm, or over 9.5 cm, that could trap a limb, finger or head
- no parts stick out more than 5 cm
- there are no toeholds or footholds above the mattress that could be used for climbing
- the depth, from the base of the mattress to the top of the lowest side, is at least 60 cm for a fixed-height cot or the lower setting, and at least 40 cm at the upper setting

## The mattress and bedding

Use a single firm, flat mattress that fits within 2 cm (20 mm) of the cot's sides and ends, with no more than 4 cm of gap when it's pushed to one side. Remove all plastic wrapping from a new mattress.

Keep the cot clear: no bumpers, pillows, doonas, loose bedding, lambswool or sheepskin, or soft toys.

## Where it goes

Sleep your baby in a safe cot in the same room as you for the first 6 to 12 months, always on their back, with their face and head uncovered. Place the cot away from curtains, blind cords and other cords, with mobiles and toys out of reach, and not under pictures or mirrors that could fall.
