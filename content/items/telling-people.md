Many people wait until about 12 weeks, after the first-trimester screening, because the chance of a miscarriage falls after the first trimester. That's a convention, not a rule.

A way to decide: if something went wrong, would you want to get through it privately, or with people around you? Telling a few close people early means support if you need it.

Some things worth agreeing between you:

- **Whose family hears first,** and whether you tell them together.
- **The friends you each want to tell yourselves,** before it's on social media.
- **Anyone who might find it hard,** like a friend who is trying to get pregnant or has lost a baby. Tell them gently, privately and before the wider announcement.
- **How you'll share it more widely:** in person, a call, a photo of the scan, a post. Agree before anyone posts.

Telling work is its own decision, with its own timing for parental leave.
