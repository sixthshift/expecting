Wait. Teeth whitening is not recommended if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.

## Why

Whitening products use hydrogen peroxide or carbamide peroxide, usually as a gel, sometimes with a light. Neither page explains why pregnancy is on the list. Gums are also often swollen, inflamed and prone to bleeding in pregnancy (Pregnancy, Birth and Baby), and healthdirect also lists gum disease and sensitive teeth as reasons not to whiten.

## What to do

- **Put it off** until after the birth, or check with your dentist.
- **Don't delay urgent care.** If you have a toothache or are worried about changes in your mouth, see your dentist as soon as possible. Most dental treatments are safe to have in pregnancy.
