## What's usual

Restless legs syndrome is an urge to move your legs to relieve unpleasant sensations: burning, tingling, aching, itching, pins and needles, or a feeling like insects crawling under your skin. It can twitch or jerk at night too. Symptoms are usually worse in the evening and at night, especially when you're trying to fall asleep, and often start when you're resting, lying down or sitting for a long time.

If you're pregnant you're more likely to have it, especially in the third trimester. It's common in late pregnancy and most of the time goes away after the baby is born. Low iron is one of the possible causes, though usually no cause is found.

For short-term relief, try moving around in bed, stretching or rubbing your legs, or getting up and walking around. Cutting down on caffeine might help.

## See your doctor or midwife

healthdirect says to see your doctor if you have symptoms of restless legs syndrome. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says to see your doctor or midwife if restless legs are disturbing your sleep.
