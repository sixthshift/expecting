"Antenatal" just means before birth. The first antenatal visit is best before 10 weeks, ideally at about 6 to 8 weeks.

Most people start with their GP, who will confirm the pregnancy, can organise routine blood tests, explain your options and give you a referral to a maternity service or obstetrician. The first appointment may be with a midwife or your doctor. Your main carer could be:

- **A public hospital maternity team.**
- **A midwife or midwife group.**
- **Shared care** between different healthcare professionals.
- **An obstetrician (OB).**

Your midwife or doctor will tell you about the tests and how much they will cost.

You can also make an appointment directly with a midwifery service in your area, and you may not need a referral if the midwife is part of a group practice or private practice. Ask your GP what's available locally.
