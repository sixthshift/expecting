Yes. Generally the safest foods are those freshly cooked or prepared, and FSANZ says that if you're vulnerable, as you are in pregnancy, it's safest to only eat freshly prepared food.

## Why

The risky foods are the same whether you're at home or out. At warm temperatures most food-poisoning bacteria grow very quickly, and some make toxins, so food should be kept either cold or hot, not lukewarm. Listeria can grow even in the fridge.

## What to do

- **Order something cooked and hot.** Meat cooked through with no pink in pork, sausages or mince; ham or salami only if it's cooked to at least 75 °C and eaten straight away; fish and seafood cooked and eaten straight away.
- **Skip the higher-risk foods:** cold deli meats and cold cooked chicken, soft cheeses such as brie, feta and ricotta unless cooked, store-bought sushi and raw seafood, pre-made salads, raw or lightly cooked sprouts, raw or lightly cooked enoki, and soft serve.
- **Check the sauces.** House-made mayonnaise and aioli are raw-egg foods; commercial ones are fine. Hummus and tahini are on the avoid list too.
- **Hot chicken** is fine if you eat it straight away.
- **Keep food either cold or hot, not lukewarm** (FSANZ). Anything you save for later is a leftovers question: fridge it straight away, eat within a day, reheat until steaming hot.
