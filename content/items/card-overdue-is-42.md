Pregnancy normally lasts about 40 weeks, and Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says your pregnancy is considered full term at 37 weeks and that if labour hasn't started by 42 weeks you're overdue. Better Health Channel says a baby is considered full term if born between 37 and 42 weeks, and that around 5 out of every 100 babies will be overdue. Every baby is different. If you do go overdue, your doctor or midwife will recommend regular checks and explain your options, which include monitoring, a membrane sweep and induction, and it's your choice whether to have treatment.
