Gravida, the G, is the number of times you've been pregnant: primigravida means a first pregnancy, multigravida more than one (Queensland Health's Pregnancy Health Record).

Parity, the P, is the number of previous pregnancies resulting in live births or stillbirths, not counting the current pregnancy (Australian Institute of Health and Welfare).

So G2P1 means a second pregnancy, after one birth.
