Avoid unpasteurised fruit juice while you're pregnant. Small amounts of other juice are fine.

## Why

Unpasteurised fruit juice is on Food Standards Australia New Zealand's list of foods to avoid if you're pregnant, because of listeria. Listeria is a bacteria that can grow in food even in the fridge, and even a small amount can cause illness. The illness it causes, listeriosis, is rare, but in pregnancy it can be serious: it may cause a miscarriage, or the baby can be born prematurely or stillborn.

## What to do

- **Skip unpasteurised juice,** along with the other ready-to-eat foods on the same list, such as pre-prepared or pre-packaged fruit salads, including those from buffets and salad bars.
- **Other juice, in small amounts,** is fine. The safest drinks in pregnancy are water and milk.
- **Know the signs.** Listeriosis usually shows up about 3 weeks after eating contaminated food, but can take anywhere from 3 to 70 days. It can look like a mild flu: fever, headache, tiredness and muscle aches, and less often nausea, cramps or diarrhoea. In pregnancy the symptoms may seem mild, but it's important to seek medical attention immediately.
