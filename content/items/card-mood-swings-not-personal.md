Pregnancy, Birth and Baby tells partners that your partner may have mood swings during pregnancy, and to try not to take them personally. Pregnancy and childbirth can be exhausting, physically and emotionally, and it's a time of adjustment for both of you.

Talk about how you're each feeling. If your partner is very anxious, or seems depressed about the pregnancy, encourage them to ask for help.
