## What's usual

Sore or swollen breasts are an early sign of pregnancy. In the first trimester your breasts become tender, larger and heavier. They're getting ready to make milk, so you may also notice more visible veins, and your nipples and areola may darken and get bigger. Small bumps on the areola (Montgomery glands) may become more noticeable; they make an oily substance that helps protect your nipples.

A supportive bra may help with discomfort. The Royal Women's Hospital suggests getting new bras properly fitted so they're comfortable and not too tight, and avoiding hard underwire.

Later in pregnancy you may notice a yellowish fluid, colostrum, leaking from your breasts.

## See your doctor or midwife

If you have significant pain, redness or a lump, speak to your midwife or doctor.
