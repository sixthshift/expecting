If you notice any of these, contact your midwife, doctor or hospital straight away. If you need urgent medical help, call 000. If you're not sure, it's best to get it checked.

## Straight away

- **Your baby is moving less than usual, or has stopped moving.** Go to your hospital now. Don't wait until tomorrow.
- **Bleeding from your vagina.**
- **Severe pain in your abdomen.** Early in pregnancy, also pain or cramping low on one side, or pain in the tip of one shoulder.
- **Clear fluid leaking from your vagina.**
- **A bad headache that doesn't go away,** even with simple pain relief.
- **Changes to your vision,** such as blurring or flashing lights.
- **Sudden swelling of your hands, feet or face.**
- **Extreme itchiness,** especially of your hands and feet.
- **Fever or chills.**
- **Shortness of breath or a racing heartbeat.**
- **Pain or swelling in one leg.**
- **Pain under your ribs.**
- **Severe nausea or vomiting.**
- **Dizziness or fainting.**
- **A hit or blow to your stomach,** even if you feel fine.

## Soon

See your doctor or midwife as soon as you can about:

- low mood, worry or other problems with how you feel that last longer than 2 weeks
- an unusual discharge from your vagina
- losing weight
- pain, burning or urgency when you wee
- swelling in your legs that doesn't go down overnight
