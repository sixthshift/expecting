A **screening test**, such as combined first trimester screening or NIPT, estimates the chance your baby has a condition. A high-chance result isn't a diagnosis, and a screen can miss some babies who have it.

A **diagnostic test**, such as CVS or amniocentesis, can tell you for certain. They're generally safe but carry a small risk of miscarriage, and most people have a screening test first. A doctor may recommend one after a screening result suggesting a higher chance, or if you already have a child with a genetic or chromosomal condition, or if you or your partner carry a genetic condition.
