If you have lower back pain that persists throughout labour, midwives can use sterile water injections. The Royal Women's Hospital says they're usually given in four places in your lower back, just beneath the skin. They cause a strong stinging sensation, like a bee sting, for up to 30 seconds before it disappears along with the back pain. They can bring up to two hours of relief to your lower back, though you'll still feel the contractions. It says there are no side effects for you or your baby, but not enough research yet to show they reduce the need for other pain relief.
