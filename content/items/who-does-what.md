The first weeks bring a lot of new jobs at once, on little sleep. Working out who does what before the baby arrives means fewer of those conversations happen in the middle of the night.

**How it works.** Open Who does what in the Drawer, each on your own phone. For each job, say who you think will do it: you, the other one, or both of you together. Neither of you sees the other's answer until you've both given one.

**Then the results.** A job you both answered the same way is settled. One where you differ is one to talk about: both saying "me" for the night wakes is as worth a conversation as both saying "you" for the admin.

**It's a starting point.** Plans change once you meet the baby, and the split that suits the first fortnight won't suit the first year. Change an answer whenever you like.
