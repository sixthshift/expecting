Placenta praevia is when the placenta attaches very close to your cervix, or covers it entirely. It can block your baby's way out. Most people with placenta praevia need a caesarean.

The most common sign is painless vaginal bleeding after 28 weeks. Contact your midwife or doctor immediately if you have any bleeding. If the bleeding is heavy, or comes with pain or contractions, call triple zero (000) and ask for an ambulance or go to your nearest hospital emergency department.
