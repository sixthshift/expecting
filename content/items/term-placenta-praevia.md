Placenta praevia is when the placenta attaches very close to your cervix, or covers it entirely. It can block your baby's way out. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says that in some cases it will be safest to birth your baby by caesarean; the Royal Women's Hospital says that in most cases you will need a caesarean birth.

The most common sign is painless vaginal bleeding after 28 weeks. Contact your midwife or doctor immediately if you have any bleeding. If the bleeding or pain is severe, call triple zero (000) and ask for an ambulance. If the bleeding is heavy and you have pain or contractions with it, call triple zero (000) and ask for an ambulance or go to your nearest hospital emergency department.
