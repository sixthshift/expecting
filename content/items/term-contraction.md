A contraction is the tightening of your uterus. In active labour the uterine muscles contract to open your cervix and help your baby move down into the birth canal. Labour contractions start like a wave from the top of the uterus down to the cervix and get stronger and stronger; between them the pain eases off. Better Health Channel says Braxton Hicks contractions are not painful and do not get stronger and closer together like true contractions.
