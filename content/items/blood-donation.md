Not right now.

## Why

Lifeblood's answer to "I am pregnant. Can I donate?" is "Not right now, no." This is to protect your health and avoid causing stress to you and your baby's circulation.

## What to do

- **After the birth,** you'll need to wait another nine months from the delivery date to allow your body enough time to replenish its iron.
- **While breastfeeding,** there are also donation restrictions.
- **Donate breast milk instead.** Lifeblood says that even if you can't donate blood for now, you may be able to donate breast milk.
- **If you're a donor trying to become pregnant,** Lifeblood recommends taking a break from donation to help build and maintain healthy iron levels, to support the increased iron requirements of a pregnancy.
