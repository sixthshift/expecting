After-birth pains can feel like period pain. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says they're due to your uterus contracting back to its pre-pregnancy size, you're more likely to get them if this isn't your first baby, and you may notice them when you breastfeed. A warm pack on your tummy may help, and you can take paracetamol 30 minutes before breastfeeding.
