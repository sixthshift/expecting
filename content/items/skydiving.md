No. Both belong on the list for after the birth.

## Why

**Skydiving.** Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says to avoid activities that involve large changes in pressure and altitude, and names skydiving alongside scuba diving. It also says to avoid exercising more than 2,000 metres above sea level until you have acclimatised.

**Bungee.** MotherSafe says to avoid high-impact sport or sports with a risk of falling or injury, and gives bungee jumping as its first example.

Pregnancy, Birth and Baby's sport advice points the same way: avoid sports that could cause you to fall, could hit your baby, or involve high altitudes. From the second trimester the baby is no longer protected by your pelvis.

## What to do

- **Postpone** the jump or the tandem until after the baby's born.
- **Stay active another way,** such as walking, swimming or a pregnancy exercise class.
