**Pre-eclampsia** is a pregnancy complication that can develop after 20 weeks of pregnancy. Symptoms include high blood pressure and protein in the urine.

If you're worried about the warning signs, see [The signs of pre-eclampsia](signs-of-pre-eclampsia).
