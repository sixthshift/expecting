Yes.

## Why

Decaffeinated coffee has 2 to 4 mg of caffeine a cup, a tiny share of the 200 mg a day recommended maximum in pregnancy.

## What to do

If you want to keep the coffee ritual, decaf is an easy swap. The amount of caffeine is usually marked on the packet.
