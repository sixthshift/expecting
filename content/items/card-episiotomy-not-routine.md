An episiotomy is a cut in the perineum to widen the vaginal opening, and Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says it's not recommended for every vaginal birth. It may be recommended to prevent a severe tear during an assisted birth with forceps or vacuum, because your baby is distressed and needs to be born quickly, or because a severe tear is likely. It's only done with your consent, and the page suggests talking it over with your doctor or midwife before labour so you can make informed decisions.
