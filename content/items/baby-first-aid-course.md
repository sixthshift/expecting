A baby and child first aid course teaches what to do in an emergency with a baby, before you're the one holding one. It's worth doing together.

The Australian Red Cross runs **First Aid for Babies and Children** for parents, expecting parents, grandparents and anyone who cares for children. It covers performing cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) and breathing emergencies, including choking and asthma. Sessions are listed as half days, such as 9:30am to 1:30pm. It's a short, non-accredited course, so not every topic is covered in depth, and you get a certificate of completion.

Ask your hospital or birth centre whether it runs a baby CPR or first aid course for expecting parents, and look for one in your area with a first aid provider. Grandparents and anyone else who'll look after the baby can come too.
