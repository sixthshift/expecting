Yes. The NSW Food Authority says canned foods (tinned fruit, vegetables, fish and so on) are shelf-stable and safe to eat, best eaten immediately after opening.

## Why

Heat processing kills harmful organisms in canned foods. Once opened, non-acidic canned foods (fish, meat, some fruits and vegetables) are potentially hazardous: they can allow Listeria to grow if contaminated after opening.

## What to do

Eat it soon after opening. Store unused portions in the fridge in a clean, sealed container (not the open can) and use them within a day.
