Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says nitrous oxide has no known harmful effects on your baby, doesn't affect your labour or your breast milk, and clears from your body within a few minutes. The side effects are yours: some people get dizziness, light-headedness, nausea or vomiting. You hold the mask or mouthpiece yourself, so you can stop straight away if you feel any side effects or drowsy.
