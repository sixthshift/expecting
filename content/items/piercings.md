Ask your doctor or midwife first. The page we checked doesn't mention pregnancy, so there is no pregnancy-specific guidance on new piercings or on what to do with a belly button ring.

## Why

healthdirect says most piercings done by a skilled body artist heal well, but risks include scarring, excessive bleeding and infection. If a piercing is done with contaminated equipment you may have a higher chance of a blood-borne infection such as hepatitis B or hepatitis C.

## What to do

- **Choose a reputable shop** that uses sterile procedures and equipment. healthdirect suggests asking whether the business is registered, how they sterilise equipment and whether they reuse needles.
- **See your doctor** if a piercing becomes painful or weeps pus, as it may be infected. You may need antibiotics.
- **Ask your doctor or the piercer** about the risks of the specific piercing, including a navel piercing, before going ahead.
