You'll be offering snacks, drinks and ice to the person giving birth, but Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says being a birth support partner can be exhausting, so look after yourself too. Take rest breaks when you can, and bring snacks and drinks for yourself, plus swimwear if you plan to go into a birthing pool or shower.
