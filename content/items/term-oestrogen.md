Oestrogen is one of the main female sex hormones, needed for puberty, the menstrual cycle, pregnancy and bone strength, among other things. The main type produced during pregnancy is oestriol, mostly by the placenta. Higher levels of oestrogen and progesterone in pregnancy also cause more vaginal discharge than usual.
