Ask your doctor or pharmacist first. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says there is currently no reliable evidence that homeopathy is effective for any health condition.

## Why

Homeopathy is based on the idea that a tiny dose of a substance thought to cause symptoms in a healthy person can treat those symptoms in someone who is unwell. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby lists homeopathy products among complementary medicines. It says these are often described as natural, but that doesn't mean they are safe, especially in pregnancy, and we often don't know much about their safety.

## What to do

- **Talk to your doctor or pharmacist** before you use a complementary medicine in pregnancy. They can advise which are safe to use.
- **Check the cost.** Homeopathy is among the complementary therapies that are no longer claimable on private health insurance, so you would pay the full fee.
