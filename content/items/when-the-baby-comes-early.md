A baby born before 37 weeks is premature, or preterm. In Australia about 8 in every 100 babies are born early, and often no one knows why. Twins or more make it more likely.

## If you have signs of labour before 37 weeks

Contact your midwife, doctor or nearest birthing centre immediately. Sometimes labour can be slowed or stopped, and each day the baby stays inside is likely to be better for their health. The signs, and the injections you may be offered to protect the baby's lungs, are on the labour pages.

## What it means for the baby

It depends a lot on how early.

- **Between 32 and 37 weeks:** babies may need care in a special care nursery (SCN). Most of them don't have any serious long-term problems.
- **Before 32 weeks:** babies have a higher risk of health and developmental problems. Many may need help breathing, and they are usually cared for in a neonatal intensive care unit (NICU) at first.

Most premature babies thrive as they grow, though they will need regular health and development checks.

## Where the birth happens

If labour starts early, you may need to go to a hospital with a NICU or special care nursery. A team from the newborn unit will be at the birth and care for your baby straight away; once the baby is stable, they may move to the NICU or SCN. If the hospital where your baby is born doesn't have a NICU, your baby may be transferred to another hospital.

How soon you can hold your baby depends on how they are. Holding them on your chest (kangaroo care) is an important part of their health, and staff will help you do it as soon as you can.

## How it can feel

A birth you didn't plan for, and a baby in intensive care, can be overwhelming. You may feel distant from your baby, find it hard to bond, and worry about their health now and later. Talking to family and friends, to people who've been through it, writing down your feelings and getting professional help can all help.

## Support, even before the birth

If you've been told your baby may need neonatal care, or may come before 37 weeks, Miracle Babies can support you now and all the way through. Their Nurture Line, 1300 622 243, is free and open 24 hours, answered by parents who have had a premature or sick baby themselves. Life's Little Treasures Foundation is on 1300 697 736 and the Preterm Infants Parents Association on 1300 773 672.
