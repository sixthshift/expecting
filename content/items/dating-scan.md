A dating scan is an early ultrasound that measures the baby to work out how far along the pregnancy is and estimate the due date. Early on, most babies of the same age are about the same size, so a dating scan can give an accurate estimated due date.

It's most often done between 6 and 8 weeks. It can also confirm whether there's more than one baby, check the baby is growing as expected and that the pregnancy is not ectopic, and check where the placenta is. If you're also having a nuchal translucency scan (see first-trimester screening), you may be able to have both at one appointment, between 11 and 13 weeks.

Medicare covers the cost of a dating scan, but there may be out-of-pocket costs depending on where you go, so ask when you book. You can bring your partner. Ask your midwife or OB.
