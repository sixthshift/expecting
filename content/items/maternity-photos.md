A photo of the bump is something many people are glad to have later, whether it's a professional shoot or a friend with a good phone on a nice evening.

**When.** Most photographers suggest a shoot somewhere around 28 to 34 weeks, when the bump is clearly showing but moving about is still comfortable. Ask the photographer what they recommend, and leave room in case the baby comes early.

**Booking.** Popular photographers book out weeks or months ahead, so book around the middle of the pregnancy. When comparing:

- What's included: how long the shoot is, how many edited images, and whether prints or albums cost extra.
- Where: a studio, a park or beach, or at home. Outdoors depends on the weather and the light, so ask about rescheduling.
- Wardrobe: many maternity photographers lend gowns.
- The partner, other children or pets: whether they're in it, and whether it costs more.

**A newborn shoot.** Some photographers sell maternity and newborn sessions as a package. Newborn shoots are usually done in the first couple of weeks after the birth, so they're booked against the due date and moved when the baby arrives. If you want one, book it now, and ask how they handle the date changing.

**Doing it yourselves.** A monthly photo against the same wall, in the same clothes, costs nothing and makes a good sequence.
