If your baby was stillborn, there are payments to support you. For these payments, a stillborn baby is one who had not breathed since delivery and had no heartbeat after birth, and was born at 20 weeks or more, or weighed at least 400 grams.

## Choosing a payment

Your family can get **either** the Stillborn Baby Payment **or** Parental Leave Pay, not both.

- **Stillborn Baby Payment** is a single payment of $4,482.12 for a baby born on or after 1 July 2026. It isn't taxable and there's no work test. Your family's adjusted taxable income must be no more than $77,177 in the 6 months after the delivery. If it's more, you may still be eligible if you get, or become eligible for, FTB Part A for another child within 52 weeks.
- **Parental Leave Pay** is taxable, and you need to meet its work test and income test. For a baby born on or after 1 July 2026 it's weekly payments for up to 26 weeks, to a total of up to $26,122.20.

You can claim either online through your Centrelink online account. If you made a pre-birth claim for Parental Leave Pay, sign in and select **Add Newborn**: you'll be asked whether your child died or was stillborn, and for proof of birth.

## Your leave from work

If your baby is stillborn, you can take up to 12 months' unpaid parental leave, even if you hadn't given notice; tell your employer as soon as you can. Your employer **can't call you back to work or cancel your unpaid parental leave**. You can choose to cancel it yourself, with written notice before it starts or at least 4 weeks' written notice once it has.

Employer-funded paid parental leave can't be refused or cancelled because of a stillbirth either, for a baby stillborn on or after 7 November 2025, unless an exception applies under your employment terms.

Stillbirth Foundation Australia offers practical resources and emotional help for parents.
