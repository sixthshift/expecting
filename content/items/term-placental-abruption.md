Placental abruption is when part or all of the placenta separates from the wall of your uterus before your baby is born. It's uncommon (fewer than 1 in 100 pregnancies) but a medical emergency.

It usually brings vaginal bleeding and abdominal pain, but the bleeding can stay hidden behind the placenta, and the pain can be mild or absent. Contact your midwife or doctor immediately for any bleeding, and as soon as possible for sudden belly pain even without bleeding. For heavy bleeding or severe pain, call triple zero (000) and ask for an ambulance.
