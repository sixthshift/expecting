## What's usual

Colostrum is your first milk. Your breasts make it from about 16 weeks of pregnancy, and you may find it leaks from your nipples, especially later in pregnancy. It's not always obvious that your breasts are making it, so you may notice nothing at all. It's usually a thick, sticky, yellowish liquid, but can also be thinner, clearer or white.

**Don't express it early.** You should not express colostrum before 36 weeks. It isn't advised if you're at risk of a premature birth, because stimulating your breasts may trigger premature labour. Check with your doctor or midwife whether expressing in late pregnancy is a good idea for you.
