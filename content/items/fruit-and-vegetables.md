Yes, wash them. Fruit and vegetables are a big part of eating well in pregnancy; the care is in the washing.

## Why

**Toxoplasma** can sometimes be found in soil. Eating unwashed vegetables is one way to catch toxoplasmosis, which can cause a birth difference in your baby if you're infected during pregnancy.

**Listeria** is found in soil and water, and raw vegetables are among the foods at higher risk of it. It can grow even in the fridge.

## What to do

- **Wash them well** under running water, and scrub off dirt. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says to wash or peel raw fruit and vegetables, and to wash herbs too.
- **Wash just before** eating raw, or before cooking.
- **Home-made salads:** wash the ingredients just before making and eating, and keep leftovers in the fridge for no more than a day.
- **Frozen vegetables:** cook them before eating, don't eat them uncooked. Blanched frozen vegetables can still carry organisms from where they were processed.
- **Skip pre-cut and pre-packaged** fruit and vegetable salads, and rockmelon. Whole fruit washed at home is fine.
- **Gardening?** Wash your hands afterwards.
