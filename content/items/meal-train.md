A meal train is a roster of friends and family who each bring a meal on a given day in the first weeks home. People want to help, and a meal is one of the most useful things they can do.

**Ask someone else to run it.** A friend or family member sets it up, shares the roster and handles the messages, so you don't have to. Ask them before the birth, then a text from either of you when the baby arrives starts it.

**Give the organiser the details now:**

- **Dietary needs:** allergies, anything you don't eat, how many people (including children).
- **How many days:** two or three weeks of dinners, or every second day, is plenty. Leftovers fill the gaps.
- **Drop-off:** a time window, and whether people should knock or leave it at the door. An esky by the door means nobody has to stay and chat.
- **Containers:** ask for ones that don't need returning.
- **Your address and parking.**

**A shared roster** keeps it simple: a shared document, a group chat or one of the free online meal-train sign-up sites. People pick a day, and nobody brings the third lasagne.

**Food that works:** meals that reheat well, freeze, or can be eaten one-handed, labelled with what it is and the date.

Keep a note of who brought what, for the thank-yous.
