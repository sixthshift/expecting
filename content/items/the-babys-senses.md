Your baby's senses arrive gradually through the pregnancy; the weeks are approximate.

**Taste.** By week 9 the face has a mouth and tongue with tiny taste buds. By week 17 the taste buds are working and your baby can tell sweet from bitter. By about week 24 they're becoming more sensitive.

**Hearing.** Hearing develops over weeks 15 to 24: first your heartbeat, digestive system and voice, and by week 24 sounds from outside the uterus. By about week 25 it's fully functional, and your baby may react to noises and be soothed by your voice. Some research suggests very early language development may start before birth.

**Sight.** From about week 24 your baby's eyes respond to light, and they keep developing after birth.

**Touch.** At 26 weeks your baby can suck, see, hear and taste, and might move in response to you running your hand firmly over your tummy. From about week 24 your baby may be able to feel pain.

## Talking and singing

From around week 15 is a great time to start talking to your baby; you might feel silly at first but will get used to it. You can talk and sing to your baby, play calming music, and gently touch or rub your belly, and that partners and other people close to you can do the same. Bonding in pregnancy can help your baby's development after they're born.
