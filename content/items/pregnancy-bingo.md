Some things happen to nearly everyone who's expecting: someone asks if it's twins, a stranger reaches for the bump, a relative tells you to sleep while you can. Pregnancy bingo turns them into a game.

**A card each.** Each of you is dealt your own card in the Drawer: 24 squares around a free centre, drawn from the same pool, so your cards differ. If you don't like yours, deal again, until you've marked something.

**Mark it when it happens.** Tap a square, say when, and add a few words if you want ("the lady at the bakery"). If it's on the other's card too, Expecting says so.

**Bingo.** Five in a row, a column or a diagonal. The centre is free. The first of you to a line wins, and it goes in the Journal. Then keep going for a full house.

You can see each other's cards, and how close each of you is. Only your own is yours to mark.
