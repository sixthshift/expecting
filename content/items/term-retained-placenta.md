A retained placenta is when all or part of the placenta doesn't come out after your baby is born; "retained products" is the term for placental tissue still in your uterus.

Signs include fever, heavy bleeding, bad-smelling discharge and many large clots. If you're bleeding heavily after the birth (soaking a pad every hour or two, or passing large clots), go to your hospital's emergency department straight away. Call triple zero (000) and ask for an ambulance if you gave birth in the last 24 hours, or you feel unwell, dizzy or clammy, your heart is racing or you're short of breath. Contact your midwife or doctor immediately about pain or bleeding you're unsure of.
