The cervix is the muscular channel at the lower part of your uterus, connecting it to your vagina. In pregnancy it is closed and a mucus plug seals it, protecting your baby from the start of pregnancy until just before you give birth. In labour it softens and thins, then dilates (opens) to 10 cm so your baby can be born.
