## What's usual

Sepsis is when your immune system reacts strongly to an infection. It can start from any infection, including ones linked to pregnancy, such as a urinary tract infection or a chest infection. Even though it's rare, it's one of the biggest causes of severe illness and death during pregnancy and after birth, which is why it needs treating quickly.

Signs can include:

- a fever (38°C or higher), or a temperature under 36°C
- feeling confused or drowsy
- tummy pain, or pain in your uterus
- a fast heart rate or fast breathing
- coughing or feeling breathless, vomiting or diarrhoea, or pain when you wee
- your baby's heart rate being faster than normal, or their movements changing

Everyone's symptoms can be different.

## Call 000 or go to emergency

If you have signs of sepsis, such as an infection, a fever or difficulty breathing, see your doctor urgently, or go to your nearest emergency department, or call triple zero (000) and ask for an ambulance.

## Contact your hospital now

Contact your doctor, midwife or hospital immediately if you have a fever or chills, or shortness of breath or a racing heartbeat.

## See your doctor or midwife

See your doctor or midwife as soon as possible if you have symptoms of an infection, or you're feeling unwell during your pregnancy.

Even if you're not sure, and something just doesn't feel right with you or your baby, it's best to get it checked.
