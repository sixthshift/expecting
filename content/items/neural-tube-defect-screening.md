Neural tube defects, such as spina bifida, are usually found in the second trimester. The ultrasound at around 18 to 20 weeks can diagnose most cases; the maternal serum screening blood test (14 to 20 weeks) estimates the chance but can't diagnose.

A finding means referral to a specialist team for more tests, information and counselling. Folic acid supplements can reduce the risk by 70%. If you're planning a pregnancy, and for the first 3 months of it, an extra 0.5 mg daily is recommended; if you're at higher risk of a neural tube defect, a higher dose (5 mg) is recommended.
