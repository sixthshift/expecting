Avoid them. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says to avoid hot tubs and heated spas during pregnancy, and lists hydrotherapy pools alongside spas as an unsafe place to exercise.

## Why

Spas can raise your core body temperature, which could lead to overheating, dehydration or fainting, and a significant rise in your temperature may affect your unborn baby. Pregnancy already makes you feel warmer, and your body may be less effective at cooling down.

## What to do

- **Stay out of the spa, hot tub and heated pools** until after the birth.
- **Swim instead.** Swimming and aqua-natal classes are encouraged in pregnancy, as they support your weight; just avoid heated pools and spas.
- **A warm bath is fine.** It's perfectly safe to have a warm bath when pregnant, as long as you don't get too hot.
- **Already been in?** MotherSafe says a short soak is unlikely to raise your temperature enough to cause problems. If you're worried, ask your midwife or doctor, or call MotherSafe: Sydney metro (02) 9382 6539, NSW non-metro 1800 647 848.
