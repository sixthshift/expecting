Each week brings one question for the two of you: about how you were raised, what you're looking forward to, what you'll miss, what you hope for them.

**How it works.** This week's question is on This week. Each of you answers on your own phone, in a line or a paragraph. Neither sees the other's answer until you've both given one; then both are kept in the Journal, under the week, and in the book at the end.

**Missed one?** An earlier week's question can still be answered whenever you like, from This week. Nothing counts them or chases you.

**It's for you two.** Who's holding a phone is a choice, not a login, so it's kept on trust: a game between the two of you, never a secret.
