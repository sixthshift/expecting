## Why

Pre-prepared and pre-packaged salads, fruit salads included, carry an increased risk of listeria, a bacterium that keeps growing in the fridge. That takes in the cut fruit cups, the bagged salads and the salad kits, and the salads at buffets and salad bars. The NSW Food Authority rates them "do not eat (high risk)", and also lists pre-cut fruit among the foods to avoid.

## What to do

- Make it at home. Homemade salads are okay: wash the ingredients well just before you make and eat them.
- Whole fresh fruit is fine; wash it well before eating. Skip rockmelon, which is on the avoid list in its own right.
- Keep any leftover salad in the fridge and use it within a day.
