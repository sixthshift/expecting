Yes, go ahead. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby answers this one directly: reaching up above your head and hanging washing on the line is safe, and it is unlikely to affect your baby's umbilical cord in any way.

## Why

Pregnancy, Birth and Baby lists this among its common myths about pregnancy, under the question "Is it unsafe to hang washing on the line?". Its answer is that reaching up above your head and hanging washing on the line is safe.

## What to do

Carry on with it. Your midwife or doctor will advise you if there are any activities that are unsafe for you in pregnancy, so it's worth asking them if you're unsure about something specific.

The same page does name activities to avoid: exercises that put you at risk of a fall, such as horse riding, skiing or cycling.
