Yes. Wear gloves and wash your hands well afterwards.

## Why

Toxoplasmosis is caused by a parasite that lives in cat faeces, and also in soil and water. It's usually harmless, but catching it for the first time while pregnant carries a small risk of miscarriage, stillbirth and birth differences, including blindness and brain damage. Pregnant people in Australia aren't normally screened for it, so prevention matters.

## What to do

- **Wear gloves** in the garden, and wash your hands thoroughly with soap after touching soil.
- **Cover sandpits** when they're not in use.
- **Potting mix** can carry Legionella bacteria. Follow the warnings on the bag, wet the mix to keep the dust down, wear gloves and wash your hands after.
- **Weedkillers and insect sprays**: reduce your use as much as you can (see pesticides).
- **See your doctor or midwife** if you've had direct contact with cat faeces or contaminated soil, or have symptoms of toxoplasmosis.
