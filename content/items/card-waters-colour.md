When your waters break, the fluid is usually clear, but it can be yellow, straw-coloured or pink. Look at it.

If it's any shade of green or brown, your baby may have passed meconium (their first poo), which may be a sign the baby is distressed. If it's blood-coloured, it could be a sign of placental abruption, which is very rare. Either way, call your doctor, midwife or birthing unit immediately for advice, or go to hospital. They'll guide you on what to do next.
