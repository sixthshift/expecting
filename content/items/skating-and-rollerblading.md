Best not. Better Health lists skating among the activities to avoid in pregnancy because of the risk of falling, next to downhill skiing and horse riding.

## Why

A fall can hurt you and your baby. Pregnancy makes one more likely: as the bump grows your centre of gravity moves forward, which changes your balance and coordination. From the second trimester your baby has grown out of the protection of your pelvis. Your ligaments also loosen, so sprains and other joint injuries come more easily.

Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says to avoid sports that could cause you to fall, or that involve sudden movements or changes in direction.

## What to do

- **Sit this one out** and stay active with something steadier, such as walking or swimming.
- **If you feel unsteady** doing any activity, stop.
- **If you have any injury to your tummy,** such as from a fall, contact your midwife or doctor immediately.
