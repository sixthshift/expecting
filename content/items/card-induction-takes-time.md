Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says that once an induction has started, it can take a few hours or more than 24 hours until your baby is born, depending on how your body responds. It may start with a gel (which may be given in several doses, usually every 6 hours) or a pessary that slowly releases the hormone over 12 to 24 hours, or with a balloon catheter, before the waters are broken and an oxytocin drip is started. RANZCOG says the process varies for everyone and may need one method or a combination.
