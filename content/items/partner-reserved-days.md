For couples, some of the family's Parental Leave Pay is set aside for the partner. For a child born **from 1 July 2026**, that's **20 days** of the 130, reserved for them to use. You decide together how to use the rest. To get the full number of days, you both need to be eligible and both need to make a claim. If only one of you is eligible, the reserved days limit still applies.

Days can be taken any time within 2 years of the birth, and both parents can be on Parental Leave Pay at the same time for up to 20 days. Many partners use some days in the first weeks home, when the help matters most.

The partner gets their own Fair Work notice deadlines (10 weeks' written notice, confirmed 4 weeks out), so plan the dates with your employer around the same time as the main claim.
