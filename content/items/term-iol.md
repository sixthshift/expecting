IOL is induction of labour, also called being induced: when a healthcare professional tries to artificially start your labour.

Breaking your waters (artificial rupture of membranes, ARM) is a form of labour induction. A balloon catheter is another type: a thin tube with a balloon on the end is put into your cervix and inflated with a small amount of water, to press on the cervix and encourage it to open.
