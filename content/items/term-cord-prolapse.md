Cord prolapse is when the umbilical cord moves in front of your baby. It can only happen once your waters have broken, and is rare: about 1 in 600 births. It is an emergency because the cord can be squashed, which can reduce the flow of oxygen to your baby.

If your waters have broken and you feel the cord in your vagina, call triple zero (000) and ask for an ambulance. While you're waiting for the ambulance, you may be asked to bring your knees to your chest with your head facing down. If your baby isn't head down and your waters break, call triple zero (000) and ask for an ambulance; if labour starts, go to hospital straight away.
