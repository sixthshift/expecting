Strength training is recommended in pregnancy, at least 2 days a week: light resistance training or bodyweight exercises (Australian Government guidelines). Avoid heavy lifting.

## Why

Pregnancy, Birth and Baby and the Royal Women's both list weightlifting among activities to avoid, and the national guidelines say to avoid anything that needs heavy lifting. Your ligaments also loosen in pregnancy, so heavy weights carry more chance of injury.

## What to do

- **Go light.** Resistance training with light weights is fine (Pregnancy, Birth and Baby); the Royal Women's lists a 'Pump' class with light weights among good group choices.
- **Get off your back.** After 16 weeks, avoid exercises lying on your back (Royal Women's); ask the instructor for another position.
- **Stop if anything hurts.** The national guidelines say to avoid any activity that gives you pain or discomfort.
- **Tell the instructor** you're pregnant and how many weeks, and ask for modifications. Some gyms run pregnancy classes.
