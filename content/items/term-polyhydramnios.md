Polyhydramnios is the medical term for having too much amniotic fluid, or more than expected. Healthy amounts range widely, and fluid is often measured on routine ultrasounds. A high level can be due to a pregnancy complication and may sometimes be a sign of a problem. What it means depends on the cause (if known), how severe it is and how far along you are. Your doctor or midwife will explain what it means for you.
