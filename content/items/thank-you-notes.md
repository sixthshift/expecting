Gifts start arriving well before the baby: at the shower, in the post, dropped at the door. Thanking people is lovely; trying to remember months later who gave the tiny knitted hat is not.

**One list, from now on.** Keep it somewhere you can both add to from your phones:

- **Who** it came from.
- **What** it was (and if it was a group gift, who was in on it).
- **Thanked yet?** A tick when it's done.

Add things as they arrive, before the wrapping paper goes out. After a shower, have someone else write the list while you open.

**Get ready before the birth:**

- **Cards and stamps,** enough for the shower and the weeks after.
- **Addresses** in one place.
- **A baby photo** can go in later, if you like.

**Keep it short.** Two or three lines is plenty: thank them for the gift by name, say how you'll use it, sign off. A text or a message with a photo of the baby using it is fine for many people. A handwritten card suits the ones who'd value it most, often the older relatives.

**Share it out.** Each of you thanks your own family and friends. Either can write them.

**No deadline.** People expect a wait after a baby. A few weeks, or a few months, is fine. Meals and practical help deserve a thank-you too.
