It's your choice. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says most people can start perineal massage around week 34 or 35, once or twice a week, and it may reduce the risk of tears, especially in a first birth. Don't do it if you're less than 34 weeks pregnant, or have a low-lying placenta, a short cervix, bleeding from the vagina, an infection such as thrush or herpes, or severe blood pressure problems. Check with your midwife or doctor before you start.
