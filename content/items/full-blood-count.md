A routine blood test at your first antenatal visit and again at about 26 to 28 weeks. It checks your haemoglobin for anaemia, and your platelets. If your iron is low, you may be offered a supplement or an infusion.

About 1 in 10 people have low platelets in pregnancy. Most often it's caused by the normal changes of pregnancy, is mild, doesn't cause problems, and goes away after the birth. You may have more tests to find the cause.
