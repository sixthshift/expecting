The third stage starts after your baby is born and ends when the placenta and membranes have come away, which can take 5 to 60 minutes. The cord is clamped and cut in this stage.

There are two ways to manage it. **Active management** uses an oxytocin injection and controlled cord traction, and usually takes up to 30 minutes. **Physiological management** (natural) means you pass the placenta without intervention, and can take up to an hour.
