Listeria infection is rare, but in pregnancy it can cause miscarriage, stillbirth or premature birth. Unlike most food bugs, listeria keeps growing in the fridge, so the risky foods are the chilled, ready-to-eat ones: soft cheeses, deli meats, cold cooked chicken, chilled seafood, and pre-cut fruit and salads.

The one rule that covers most of it: freshly cooked and eaten hot is safe. Cooking or reheating until steaming hot (75°C) kills it. Keep the fridge at 5°C or colder, and eat leftovers within a day.

If you have a fever or flu-like symptoms after eating something risky, contact your doctor or midwife straight away: symptoms in pregnancy may seem mild, but it's important to get checked immediately.
