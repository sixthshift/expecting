Parenting Payment is the main income support payment while you're a young child's main carer. It's **separate from Family Tax Benefit and Parental Leave Pay**.

## Who can get it

All of these must apply:

- you're under the income and asset test limits
- you meet the principal carer rules for a child under 14 if you're single, or under 6 if you have a partner
- you meet residence rules
- your partner isn't currently getting Parenting Payment.

How much you can get depends on your and your partner's circumstances.

## When to claim

You can only submit a claim for Parenting Payment **after your baby is born**. You'll generally get your first payment within two weeks of your claim outcome, if it's approved, though waiting periods sometimes apply.

To do your business with Centrelink online, you need a myGov account linked to Centrelink.
