Yes. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says you can safely use hair removal creams, lotions, powders, gels, roll-ons, sprays and wax while pregnant, and that laser hair removal is also safe. Waxing can also be done safely while breastfeeding.

## Why

Hair removal creams usually contain thioglycolic acid. There isn't specific data on how much is absorbed, but the concentration is low and it is only on your skin briefly, so it is not expected to be harmful (MotherSafe). Laser light heats only the surface of the skin and doesn't go deeper, so it poses no known risk to your baby. Hair bleaching agents such as hydrogen peroxide are used briefly at low concentrations and aren't a significant exposure.

## What to do

- **Avoid products with hydroquinone** (MotherSafe).
- **Laser may work less well.** Because of hormone and body changes, laser hair removal may be less effective and long-term results less predictable. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby recommends speaking to your doctor before light-based cosmetic treatments, and lists waxing, shaving, threading and topical products as alternatives.
