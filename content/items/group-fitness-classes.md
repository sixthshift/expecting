Yes, with changes. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby lists dancing and fitness classes among aerobic exercises in pregnancy, and says your gym may offer classes designed especially for pregnancy. If you go to ordinary classes, make sure the teacher is properly qualified and knows you're pregnant and how many weeks along you are.

## Why

Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says to avoid sports and activities that make you work very hard or become too hot, could cause you to fall, could hit your baby, or involve lots of jumping or bouncing, sudden movements or changes in direction. Low-impact aerobics is one of the moderate activities it calls great for minimal stress on your joints.

## What to do

- **Skip the jumping and bouncing,** and anything with sudden changes of direction.
- **Keep to a level where you can still hold a conversation,** and drink plenty of water. Avoid strenuous exercise in hot or humid weather.
- **For a good abdominal workout in a group,** the Royal Women's suggests clinical Pilates, a fitball class, boxercise, aquarobics or a Pump class with light weights.
- **Tell your maternity team** about any sport you play.
