Not raw. Raw oysters are on the avoid list in pregnancy. Shellfish that's been cooked thoroughly and eaten hot is fine.

## Why

**Listeria.** FSANZ names raw oysters among the chilled seafood to avoid when pregnant. Listeria can grow even in the fridge, and in pregnancy it can cause miscarriage, premature birth or stillbirth.

**Other bacteria and viruses.** The NSW Food Authority puts all raw seafood under "do not eat (high risk)": raw or undercooked seafood can contain harmful viruses and bacteria, such as vibrio.

Mercury isn't the worry here: the NSW Food Authority says molluscs, oysters included, generally contain lower levels of mercury.

## What to do

- **Cook shellfish thoroughly**, to at least 63 °C, and eat it hot. That goes for other cooked fish and seafood too.
- **Leftovers** go in the fridge and get eaten within a day, reheated to steaming hot.
- **Already had raw oysters?** Listeria symptoms can be mild, like the flu: fever, headache, tiredness, aches. FSANZ says to seek medical attention immediately if you have them while pregnant.
