Despite the name, morning sickness — nausea, with or without vomiting — can happen at any time of day. It's most common between about 6 and 14 weeks, and for many people it settles by the second trimester, though it can last to 14 to 20 weeks.

It ranges from mild queasiness to something much harder. When it's very severe it's called **hyperemesis gravidarum**: it becomes hard to keep enough food and fluids down, and it can lead to dehydration and weight loss. That needs medical help, not toughing out.

For the partner: small, frequent snacks on hand, taking over cooking smells, and fewer questions about dinner go a long way. If fluids aren't staying down, that's worth a prompt call. Ask your midwife or OB.
