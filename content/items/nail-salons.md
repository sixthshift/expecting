Yes.

## Why

Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says it's generally considered safe to have nail treatments during pregnancy. The chemicals are generally in low concentrations and aren't easily absorbed by the skin. MotherSafe says that because exposure is brief, it isn't anticipated that nail work would cause problems.

## What to do

- **Choose good ventilation** and limit contact with chemicals, as you would with hair dye.
- **Skip the treatment** if you have cuts around your nails, and make sure the technician uses sterilised tools to prevent infection.
- **If you're unsure about the polish,** ask the salon to avoid products containing dibutyl phthalate, toluene and formaldehyde.
- **If you work in a nail salon,** MotherSafe says most studies show no significant increase in the risk of birth defects or other pregnancy complications. It still advises maximising ventilation, using gloves, washing your hands before eating, and storing and disposing of chemicals safely. It also advises avoiding long periods on your feet and long working hours. Most masks don't prevent you breathing in chemicals, though they may stop dust.
