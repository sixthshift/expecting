Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says it's often useful for expecting parents and birth support partners to have a tour of the maternity hospital or birth centre, to know what to expect. Every health facility is different and has its own policies.

## What to do

- **Ask.** "Can we tour the maternity facilities before the birth?" is one of the questions Pregnancy, Birth and Baby suggests asking when you're choosing a hospital or birthing centre.
- **Work out the trip.** Labour often starts unexpectedly, so it helps to know where you're going. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby suggests a practice run in your third trimester, being clear where you can park, and talking over the route with your partner, including whether it changes with traffic or the time of day. It also says not to drive yourself when you're in labour.

Ask your hospital how its tours run and when to book.
