It sounds absurd to think about childcare before the baby has arrived, but many centres have long waiting lists. If either of you plans to return to work within the first year or two, the time to get on lists is well before you need a place.

- StartingBlocks.gov.au lists centres near you along with their national quality ratings.
- Join the lists of the centres you'd be happy with — not just your first choice.

Later, the Child Care Subsidy is claimed through Services Australia. Claim before care starts: otherwise you may pay full fees until the claim is finalised, and it can only be backdated 28 days.
