It sounds absurd to think about childcare before the baby has arrived, but many centres have waiting lists, and it's best to start well before you think you may need care, so it's available when you need it. In NSW, the government says you can put your child on the wait list at more than one service at once, and suggests doing so if your child needs to be enrolled by a particular age or time. Most services charge a wait list fee, so know the terms, including whether it's refundable if you don't get a place or find one elsewhere.

- StartingBlocks.gov.au, the Australian Government's search for child care services, lists approved services with their fees, vacancies and quality ratings.

Later, the Child Care Subsidy is claimed through Services Australia. A claim can be backdated to the first Monday of a CCS fortnight, no more than 28 days before it was submitted.
