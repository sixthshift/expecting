Yes, if it's done by a qualified professional. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says acupuncture is generally safe during pregnancy, and that only a trained person should perform it. Tell your acupuncturist you are pregnant, as some acupuncture points are not safe to use during pregnancy.

## Why

Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says there is some evidence that acupuncture during pregnancy and birth might help reduce nausea, back and pelvic pain, and labour pain. If it is used appropriately, very few side effects are associated with it, and mild pain or bruising from the needles is the most common.

## What to do

- **Speak to your doctor first.** healthdirect says it's important to speak to your doctor before getting acupuncture if you are pregnant, and that there may be a chance of miscarriage in the first trimester.
- **Chinese herbal medicine** is a different question. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says there is limited research on its safety and benefits in pregnancy, and to ask your doctor or midwife before using Chinese medicine practices.
