Yes, go. It's a good idea for the birth support partner to come to appointments, especially when the birth plan is being discussed and how the pregnant parent wants you to support them during the birth.

Antenatal appointments are a chance to ask questions about tests, symptoms, the birth and caring for the baby. The Library has a ready-made list of questions to ask about tests and scans.
