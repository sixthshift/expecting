Yes, but avoid very large amounts of it.

## Why

Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says peppermint tea is one of the most used herbal medicines in pregnancy, and that studies have shown it doesn't harm you or your baby. It can help ease nausea and wind.

## What to do

Herbal teas aren't all alike and are often not well studied, so check with your doctor or pharmacist before drinking them, especially if you take prescription medicine. Limit yourself to 2 cups of herbal tea a day, and vary the types you drink to limit the amount of any one herb.
