Yes. The National Health and Medical Research Council (NHMRC) recommends that everyone who is pregnant, breastfeeding or considering pregnancy takes an iodine supplement of 150 micrograms (µg) each day.

## Why

The thyroid uses iodine to make hormones that are vital for the normal development of the baby's brain and nervous system, before birth and in the first years of life. Even mild to moderate iodine deficiency can cause learning difficulties and affect physical development and hearing.

Since 2009, bread in Australia (except organic) has been made with iodised salt. That's enough for most people, but pregnancy and breastfeeding bring the greatest need, which is why a supplement is recommended on top.

## What to do

- **Take 150 µg a day** from when you're thinking about pregnancy, right through pregnancy and breastfeeding.
- **Have a thyroid condition?** Get advice from your doctor before you take a supplement.
- **Not kelp or seaweed tablets.** They aren't recommended, because the amount of iodine in them varies.
- **For advice on your own diet,** ask your doctor, midwife or an Accredited Practising Dietitian.
