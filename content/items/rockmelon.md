No. Rockmelon (cantaloupe) is on the avoid list from FSANZ, Pregnancy, Birth and Baby and the NSW Food Authority alike. The NSW Food Authority calls it "do not eat (high risk)".

## Why

The rough rind can trap bacteria, and cutting the melon can carry them into the flesh. Rockmelon may contain listeria or salmonella. Listeria can grow even in the fridge, and in pregnancy it can cause miscarriage, premature birth or stillbirth.

## What to do

- **Choose other fruit.** FSANZ suggests tree fruits such as mango, orange and apple, or tinned fruit, instead.
- **Wash whole fruit well** before eating it, and keep any cut leftovers in the fridge and eat them within a day.
- **Skip pre-cut fruit from a shop**, including fruit salads: pre-prepared or pre-packaged fruit salads are on the avoid list too.
- **Already eaten some?** Listeria symptoms can be mild, like the flu: fever, headache, tiredness, aches. FSANZ says to seek medical attention immediately if you have them while pregnant. If you're worried about something you've eaten, call your maternity care provider or doctor.
