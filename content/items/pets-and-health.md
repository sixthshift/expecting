Yes, keep them. The page we checked doesn't suggest giving up a pet. Its pregnancy advice is about cats, and about getting your pets ready for the baby. It doesn't cover birds or reptiles in pregnancy.

## Why

Toxoplasmosis is an infection that can cause serious problems during pregnancy, and it is caused by a common parasite. For cats, Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says to avoid handling kitty litter if possible, disinfect the litter tray at least daily, and practise good kitchen hygiene. If you have to change the litter yourself, wash your hands thoroughly afterwards.

## What to do

- **Get your partner to take over all pet care,** if you can, and get your pets used to being home alone.
- **Plan for the baby now.** Think about where your pet sleeps, where it's allowed in your home and how it travels with you, and make any changes before your baby is born.
- **Dogs:** make sure yours can walk on a lead, exercise it regularly, teach it which rooms will be off-limits, and consider training it not to jump or bark.
- **Getting a new pet:** the page says it should never be an impulsive decision. Ask whether you can afford one, have the time and know how to look after it.
