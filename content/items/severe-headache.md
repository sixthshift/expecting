## What's usual

Ordinary headaches are common in pregnancy, and paracetamol is fine to take for one. What isn't usual, after 20 weeks, is a headache that won't go away with simple pain relief. That can be a sign of pre-eclampsia, a serious condition that raises your blood pressure and can affect your kidneys, liver and brain. Pre-eclampsia can get worse very quickly.

Watch for it with the other signs: blurred vision or flashing lights, sudden swelling of your face, hands or feet, and pain under your ribs.

## Call 000 or go to emergency

Call triple zero (000) and ask for an ambulance, or go to your nearest emergency department, if you have a sudden, severe headache, or a headache with vomiting, confusion, a stiff neck, fever, loss of balance, a seizure or changes in your vision.

## Contact your hospital now

After 20 weeks, contact your doctor, midwife or hospital immediately if you have a headache that doesn't get better with simple pain-relieving medicine.

If it comes with sudden swelling of your hands, feet or face, or pain under your ribcage, seek medical attention urgently or go to your local emergency department.

A severe headache that isn't usual for you: seek medical help immediately.
