Up to 1 in 4 pregnancies end in miscarriage before 14 weeks. Most of the time it happens because of a problem with the baby's development, not because of anything you did or didn't do.

Around 50 to 80 per cent of miscarriages come from random chromosomal abnormalities. In most cases no one can say exactly why it happened, but it is unlikely that you did something to cause it. And people who miscarry for this reason have a high chance of a healthy pregnancy later.
