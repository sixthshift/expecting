Yes. ARPANSA, Australia's radiation protection agency, says body scanners used at Australian airports are safe and don't pose a health risk. Its page doesn't mention pregnancy, so this is its general advice to passengers.

## Why

ARPANSA says body scanners pose no known health risks to passengers or operators, and the energy released by the scanners used in Australia is much lower than that emitted from mobile phones. Australian law requires body scanners used for security screening to use non-ionising technology, which means they don't emit ionising radiation such as X-rays. The scanners use millimetre waves, a form of radiofrequency radiation similar to that emitted by mobile phones.

X-ray backscatter scanners, which apply very low amounts of ionising radiation, are used at some overseas airports, but have never been used for passenger screening in Australia.
