Sleep gets harder as the bump grows: the toilet, heartburn, cramps and simply finding a comfortable position.

**Side sleeping from 28 weeks.** Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says doctors recommend sleeping on your side from 28 weeks until the baby is born, at night and for daytime naps. It's part of the advice on lowering the risk of stillbirth. Either side is fine. Waking up on your back is nothing to worry about: just roll onto your side and go back to sleep.

**What helps, according to the same advice:**

- A pregnancy pillow. They come in many different shapes and sizes.
- Knees bent, with a pillow between them.
- A pillow under the bump for support.

**The bed itself.** A few practical things that are worth sorting before 28 weeks:

- Room for the pillow: a long pregnancy pillow takes up a lot of a queen bed, so decide who sleeps where.
- An easy way to the toilet in the night, with a night light.
- A spare pillow or two for the partner, who may lose theirs.

See your doctor if trouble sleeping worries you for your or your baby's health, or if you've started snoring in pregnancy, which could be a sign of other medical problems.
