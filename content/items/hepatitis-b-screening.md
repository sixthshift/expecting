Hepatitis B is a liver virus that can pass to your baby during pregnancy or birth, and some people have it without symptoms. Everyone is recommended a test for hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg), which shows a current infection.

If it's positive, your doctor will monitor the virus and may prescribe antiviral medicine. Your baby is offered hepatitis B immunoglobulin and the vaccine, and you can breastfeed as long as your baby has their vaccines. Untreated, the baby has a very high chance of infection. If you're not immune and at increased risk, you should have the hepatitis B vaccine.
