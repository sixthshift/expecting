## Why

Cold cured and sliced meats can carry listeria, a bacterium that keeps growing in the fridge. Their long shelf life gives it time. Listeriosis is rare, but in pregnancy it can cause miscarriage, premature birth or stillbirth.

That covers ham, salami, luncheon meat and the like, whether from a deli counter, a sandwich bar, or a packet of sliced ready-to-eat meat from the supermarket.

## What to do

- Skip them cold: on a platter, in a roll, in a wrap.
- Cooked is fine. Thoroughly cooked to at least 75°C and eaten soon afterwards, these meats are safe: ham on a hot pizza, or a toasted ham sandwich.

If you've already eaten some, listeriosis is rare. Its symptoms can be mild and flu-like (fever, headache, tiredness, aches), and in pregnancy it's important to seek medical attention immediately if you get them.
