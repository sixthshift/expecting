Yes, but it counts toward your caffeine. The limit in pregnancy is 200 mg a day in total.

## Why

A 100 g bar of chocolate has about 20 mg of caffeine, though it depends on the strength of the chocolate. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says 200 mg is about 3 to 5 cups of tea, hot chocolate or cola drinks, and another of its pages says 4 cups of hot chocolate.

## What to do

Count it alongside coffee, tea and cola, and check labels for exact amounts. Some people notice they're more sensitive to caffeine in pregnancy.
