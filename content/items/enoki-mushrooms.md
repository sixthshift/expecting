Only thoroughly cooked. FSANZ advises everyone not to eat enoki mushrooms raw, and the NSW Food Authority calls raw or lightly cooked enoki "do not eat (high risk)" in pregnancy.

## Why

Listeria has been found in some imported fresh enoki, and those products were recalled. Six cases of listeriosis in Australia, notified between 2017 and 2020, were linked by genetic testing to an overseas outbreak strain linked to enoki. Pregnant people and their unborn babies are among those most at risk: in pregnancy listeriosis can cause miscarriage, premature birth or stillbirth.

## What to do

- **Cook them until steaming hot.** FSANZ recommends boiling them for at least 2 minutes.
- **Don't use raw enoki** in salads or as a garnish.
- **Store them in the fridge**, at 5 °C or colder, away from ready-to-eat food.
- **Check for recalls.** Don't eat recalled enoki: return it for a refund or throw it out, and clean anything it touched.
- **Worried about your health** after eating some? Seek medical advice.
