## What's usual

Some swelling of the feet and ankles is a normal part of pregnancy, especially later on, after standing a long time or in hot weather. It builds up gradually through the day and usually goes down overnight while you sleep.

Swelling that comes on suddenly, or is severe, in your face, hands or feet is different. It can be a sign of pre-eclampsia, a life-threatening complication of pregnancy.

## Call 000 or go to emergency

Call triple zero (000) and ask for an ambulance if you have a headache together with changes in your vision.

## Contact your hospital now

Contact your doctor, midwife or maternity hospital immediately if you have sudden swelling of your face, hands or feet, especially with any of these:

- a very severe or dull headache that doesn't go away
- changes to your vision: blurriness, flashing lights or dots
- severe pain just below your ribs
- feeling sick or vomiting
- high blood pressure
- heartburn that doesn't go away with antacids

After 20 weeks, healthdirect also says to contact your doctor, midwife or hospital immediately for swelling in your legs that doesn't improve overnight.

If one arm or leg is more swollen than the other, with pain, redness or warmth, contact your doctor, midwife or maternity hospital straightaway: it can be a sign of a blood clot.

## See your doctor or midwife

If you notice swelling early in the day, or in other parts of your body such as your fingers, hands and face, speak to your midwife or doctor. Tell them too if the swelling is more than slight or isn't relieved by rest.
