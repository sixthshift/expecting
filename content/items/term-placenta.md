The placenta is a temporary organ that develops during pregnancy. It attaches to the wall of your uterus and, through the umbilical cord, delivers oxygen and nutrients to your growing baby. It's birthed after your baby, vaginally or by caesarean. This is the third stage of labour, and the placenta is sometimes called the afterbirth.
