## Why

Food Standards Australia New Zealand says people who are vulnerable, including anyone pregnant, should not eat raw fish because of risks such as listeria. That covers sashimi, sushi, ceviche, gravlax and cold-smoked salmon.

Freezing raw fish for at least 7 days kills parasites, but it doesn't kill harmful bacteria. And ceviche, cured in citrus, is still raw fish. Raw oysters sit in the same group of chilled, ready-to-eat or raw seafood to avoid.

## What to do

- Have the fish cooked. Freshly cooked fish, or fish thoroughly reheated until steaming hot, is the safe option.
- If you're not sure whether fish is meant to be eaten raw, cook it.
- Leftover cooked fish: into the fridge, and eat it within a day.
