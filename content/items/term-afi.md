The amniotic fluid index (AFI) is how the amount of fluid around your baby is described after an ultrasound. Your healthcare professional looks for pockets of amniotic fluid and checks how much there is; the amount is usually described in centimetres. There is a wide range of healthy levels. Too much or too little fluid can sometimes be a sign of pregnancy complications.
