None of these are needed, and it's easy to buy more kits than you'll use. Pick one or two you'll actually do.

**A belly cast.** A plaster cast of the bump, made with plaster bandage strips from a kit, then smoothed and painted or left plain. Most people make it in the last couple of months, when the bump is biggest. Allow an afternoon, an old sheet and a helper. Some photographers and artists will do it for you.

**Hand and foot prints.** Ink or clay prints are easiest in the first days, while the baby sleeps a lot and the hands and feet are at their smallest. Inkless wipe kits are less messy than ink pads. Some hospitals give you a footprint card or the hospital name band, so keep those too.

**A letter to the baby.** Each of you writes one before the birth, about what you're hoping for and what life is like right now, sealed for a birthday years away.

**A time capsule.** A box for the things of the moment: a scan photo, the hospital band, a newspaper from the day they're born, a list of what things cost, a note from each grandparent. Decide when it opens, often at 18 or 21.

**A baby book or journal.** Fill it in as you go rather than trying to remember later. Notes on a phone count.

Keep them somewhere dry and out of the sun. Ink and photos fade.
