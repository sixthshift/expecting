A caesarean section (also called a C-section) is a surgical procedure in which a baby is delivered through a cut in the abdomen and uterus.

Healthdirect describes the usual cut as a low horizontal one on your "bikini" line: the obstetrician separates the muscles of the abdominal wall, opens the uterus and delivers the baby through the cut.
