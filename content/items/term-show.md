A show is when the thick plug of mucus that seals your cervix through pregnancy comes away. It's a pink or bloodstained mucus you might see when you wipe, and means your cervix may be starting to soften and open, sometimes before contractions start.

Bleeding from your vagina is different: call your midwife or doctor straight away. For heavy bleeding, call triple zero (000) and ask for an ambulance. If you notice early signs of labour, such as a show, before 37 weeks, contact your midwife or doctor and let them know. Call them straight away if your contractions start or you have other signs of labour before 37 weeks.
