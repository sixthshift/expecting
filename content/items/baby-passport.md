If you're planning to travel overseas in the baby's first months, the passport is a chain of steps, and the first link is the [birth certificate](birth-certificate). A passport for a child aged 0 to 15 is valid for up to 5 years.

## What you'll need

- your baby's **full, original birth certificate**, showing the parents' names and the baby's full name at birth
- documents proving your baby's **Australian citizenship**
- **2 passport photos** that meet the photo guidelines
- documents for any change of name (the baby's, yours or the other parent's), and any court orders about the baby
- your own photo ID showing your photo, signature and current address

You bring the originals when you lodge.

## The photos

No one else can be visible in the photo. Children under 3 may have their mouth open. Anyone over 3 must face the camera straight on, with eyes open and a neutral expression.

## A guarantor

A guarantor confirms who the baby is. For a baby under one, it's an adult Australian citizen who has **known the baby since birth**, with a current Australian passport with at least 2 years' validity, or on the electoral roll at their current address for at least 12 months. They can't be related to you or the baby by birth or marriage, have parental responsibility, be the partner of someone who does, or live at the baby's address. They sign section 11 of the form and the back of one photo, in black pen: "This is a true photo of [the baby's full name]".

## Consent from both parents

Everyone with parental responsibility, usually each parent on the birth certificate, should consent by completing sections 12 to 15 of the form, each signature witnessed by an adult who isn't related or living with you. Without full consent or a relevant Australian court order, you can ask for an assessment under special circumstances, but it can take much longer, there's no faster processing, and the passport may not be approved. The Passport Office recommends not booking travel until it has confirmed the passport.

## Lodging, and how long it takes

Fill in the form online and print it, or pick up the purple paper form at a post office. Lodge it in person at a participating post office and pay the fee there. Allow **at least 6 weeks** from lodging to the passport arriving. Priority and Fast Track processing cost extra and are only for complete applications with full parental consent; with delivery, they take up to 11 and 14 business days.
