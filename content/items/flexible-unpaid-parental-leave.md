Unpaid parental leave doesn't have to be one unbroken block. You can take part of your 12 months as **flexible parental leave**: a single day, or separate periods of a day or more. Flexible days are unpaid.

## How many days

It depends on when your child is born:

- between 1 July 2025 and 30 June 2026: up to 120 days
- on or after 1 July 2026: up to **130 days**.

Flexible days come out of your 12 months: together, your flexible and continuous leave can't be more than 12 months unless you extend it.

## When you can take them

Any time within the first 24 months after the birth. You can take them before and after a continuous block, including once you've gone back to work. If you're pregnant and working in the 6 weeks before your due date, you can start taking flexible days then.

Both working parents can take flexible days, from the same or different employers, and not necessarily at the same time.

## Notice

- **At least 10 weeks before** you start your leave, tell your employer in writing the **total number** of flexible days you intend to take.
- **At least 4 weeks before** each flexible day, give notice of the **specific dates**. You can change a day after giving notice if your employer agrees.

With your employer's agreement, you can give less notice if you're only taking flexible leave, or taking it before a continuous block.

The eligibility rules are the same as for continuous parental leave, including having worked for your employer for at least 12 months.
