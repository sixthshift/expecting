Pregnancy, Birth and Baby suggests practising walking your dog on the lead while you push a pram, and teaching them to walk at the same pace. Never tie the dog's lead to the pram. It also suggests booking your dog into obedience training and practising having them under control, at home and out, with some "recall" training so they come when called. Let your dog see and sniff the bassinet or cot, pram and change table while you supervise.
