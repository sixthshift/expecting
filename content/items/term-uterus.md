Yes: the uterus is also known as the womb. It sits in the middle of your pelvis and is pear-shaped, with four main parts: the fundus (the top), the corpus (the main body), the isthmus and the cervix. It grows through pregnancy, and in labour its muscles contract to open the cervix and help your baby move down into the birth canal.
