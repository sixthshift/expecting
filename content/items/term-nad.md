NAD stands for no abnormality detected (Queensland Health's Pregnancy Health Record).

That record's visit notes have a column for each routine check: blood pressure (BP), weeks of gestation, fundal height in centimetres, presentation, descent or fifths above the brim, fetal heart rate (FHR), fetal movement, liquor (the fluid around the baby), weight in kilograms, and urinalysis (U/A), if required. Under presentation you may see C or Ceph, lying head down, or Br, lying bottom down. A glossary at the back explains the rest, and the record says to ask your GP, midwife or obstetrician if you don't understand any of the terms or words they use.
