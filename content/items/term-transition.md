Transition is the end of the active phase of the first stage, just before the second stage begins, when your cervix is around 8 to 10 centimetres dilated. Contractions become faster and more intense. You might feel irritable, out of control or like you "can't do it anymore", have shaking legs or nausea, and feel pressure in your bottom or an urge to push. Your partner, midwife or doctor can help with calm reassurance and by staying close. The glossary, by contrast, describes transition as the phase when the cervix becomes fully dilated, at 10 centimetres.
