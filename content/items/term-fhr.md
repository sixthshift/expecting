On Queensland Health's Pregnancy Health Record, fetal heart (FH) is your baby's heart and fetal heart rate (FHR) your unborn baby's heart rate; auscultation is listening to it. Fetal movements (FM) are your baby's movements: FMF means fetal movements felt, and FMNF fetal movements not felt. The record's visit notes have a column for the FHR and one for fetal movement, filled in at your antenatal visits.

The same record says to call your GP, midwife, obstetrician or birth suite if your baby is moving less than usual, or the pattern of movement has changed so that you're concerned. Don't wait until the next day.
