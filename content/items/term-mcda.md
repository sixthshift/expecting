MCDA stands for monochorionic diamniotic: your twins share a placenta but are each in their own amniotic sac. It's one of the types of identical twins.

Some complications can only happen in twins who share a placenta, including twin-to-twin transfusion syndrome (TTTS), so you'll need extra health checks. Your health team will closely check both babies, because the health of each twin affects the other.
