Cephalic means your baby is head-down, ready for birth. Most babies present headfirst, and by 37 weeks your baby will usually be in a headfirst position. About 3 in every 100 babies are breech after 37 weeks. In rare cases a headfirst baby is in a face or brow presentation, which may not be suitable for vaginal birth.
