**Caffeine:** the advice in Australia is no more than 200 mg a day from everything: coffee, tea, cola, chocolate and energy drinks. That's roughly one or two espresso-based coffees, or three cups of instant. Café coffee varies a lot in strength, and a double shot counts twice. Too much caffeine is linked to miscarriage and low birth weight.

**Alcohol:** the National Health and Medical Research Council's guideline is that anyone pregnant or planning a pregnancy should not drink. There is no known safe amount, time or type: alcohol crosses the placenta to the baby. If you drank before you knew you were pregnant, mention it to your midwife or doctor; most people in that position go on to have a healthy baby.

A partner cutting down too makes it easier, and helps with the questions at parties when you're not telling people yet.
