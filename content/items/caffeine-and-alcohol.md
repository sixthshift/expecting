**Caffeine:** the advice in Australia is no more than 200 mg a day from everything: coffee, tea, cola, chocolate and energy drinks. That's roughly one or two espresso-based coffees, or two or three cups of instant. Café coffee varies a lot in strength. Too much caffeine may increase the risk of miscarriage, stillbirth and low birth weight.

**Alcohol:** the National Health and Medical Research Council's guideline is that anyone pregnant or planning a pregnancy should not drink. There is no known safe amount, time or type: alcohol crosses the placenta to the baby. If you drank before you knew you were pregnant, the risk from low-level drinking is likely to be low; if you're concerned, speak to your doctor or midwife.

A partner cutting down too makes it easier, and helps with the questions at parties when you're not telling people yet.
