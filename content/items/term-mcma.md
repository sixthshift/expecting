MCMA stands for monochorionic monoamniotic: your twins share a placenta and are in the same amniotic sac. It's a rarer type of twin pregnancy.

With MCMA twins there's a chance the babies' umbilical cords can become tangled together, which can put both babies' lives at risk. You'll need extra health checks, and your health team will closely check both babies.
