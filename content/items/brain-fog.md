Pregnancy can affect brain function and memory, especially in the third trimester. This is sometimes known as "brain fog".

Pregnancy, Birth and Baby lists reduced concentration and alertness among the possible complications of severe or ongoing tiredness. If you're exhausted and more rest doesn't help, see your doctor or midwife.
