Syntocinon is a medication used to induce labour. It is given through a vein, attached to a bag that continuously delivers a small amount of the medication. Oxytocin is the hormone that causes contractions, and this is a synthetic version, given through a drip in your arm.

It can also be used to strengthen contractions that aren't strong or regular enough once labour has started (augmentation). After the birth, with active management of the third stage, an injection of synthetic oxytocin helps your uterus contract, so the placenta comes out more quickly and the risk of heavy bleeding is lower.
