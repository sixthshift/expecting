Your baby's brain starts forming in the first weeks of pregnancy and keeps growing and developing for many years after birth.

## Through the pregnancy

- **The first weeks.** The brain and spinal cord form very early, which is when neural tube defects such as spina bifida and anencephaly can happen. Most can be prevented with enough folate before and during the first 3 months of pregnancy: that's why folic acid is recommended from at least a month before trying.
- **The first trimester.** Nerve connections form that let your baby move around in the uterus.
- **The second trimester.** More connections are made. Around 18 weeks the nervous system is developing and a layer of myelin is growing to cover the nerve cells. By 23 weeks the brain and nervous system are developing rapidly, and your baby can recognise light, sound and pain; by 24 weeks the brain is growing rapidly and the senses keep developing.
- **The third trimester.** The cerebral cortex, the part for thinking and planning, becomes more active, preparing your baby for learning.

By birth your baby can hear (and will know your voice) and see a little.

## What feeds it

- **Folate:** your baby needs it for brain, spine and nerve development. Take a supplement with at least 400 micrograms of folic acid; your doctor may recommend 5 mg if your pregnancy has a higher risk of a neural tube defect.
- **Iodine** is key to your baby's brain growth. The Royal Women's Hospital recommends a pregnancy multivitamin with at least 150 micrograms.
- **Omega-3 fats** are needed for healthy brain, nerve and eye development; they're in fish, especially oily fish, and in walnuts, chia and linseed.
- **Vitamin B12:** if you eat no animal foods you'll need a supplement, as it's needed for your baby's brain development.

Alcohol is the one to avoid: even small amounts can harm a baby's growth and may cause fetal alcohol spectrum disorder (FASD).

## After birth

The brain develops through use. Loving relationships, play, talking, singing, reading, sleep and good food all help it form new connections.
