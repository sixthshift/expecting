Yes, in moderation. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says ginger tea should be safe to drink in moderation while pregnant.

## Why

The sources disagree on whether it works, not on whether it's safe. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says ginger tea can help ease nausea. KEMH (King Edward Memorial Hospital, 2026 booklet) says ginger is no longer suggested as a treatment for hyperemesis (severe pregnancy sickness): studies show it has minimal benefit and may sometimes worsen symptoms or cause unpleasant side effects.

## What to do

Limit herbal tea to 2 cups a day, and check with your doctor or pharmacist first, especially if you take prescription medicine. If your nausea is severe, talk to your doctor or midwife about what else can help. Ginger beer is different: the NSW Food Authority lists it, with kombucha, kvass and kefir, as a fermented drink to avoid (high risk).
