Employees can take up to 12 months of unpaid parental leave, and can ask for up to 12 months more. There are two different steps, with different rules.

## Within the first 12 months

If you planned to take less than 12 months, you can **extend it up to a total of 12 months**. Give your employer notice in writing, **at least 4 weeks before your current leave ends**, with your new end date. Any further extensions within the first 12 months need your employer's agreement.

## Beyond 12 months

Once you've taken 12 months, you can **apply to extend by up to a further 12 months**. Apply in writing, at least 4 weeks before your first 12 months ends. The Fair Work Ombudsman has free template letters.

The total can't be more than **24 months from the birth**. In a couple, each parent can take up to 12 months of unpaid parental leave and apply to extend by up to 12 more.

## How your employer must respond

Your employer has to reply **in writing within 21 days**. They can agree, agree to a different period after discussing it with you, or refuse. They can only refuse if they've discussed it and genuinely tried to reach an agreement, considered the consequences for you, and have **reasonable business grounds**, such as the cost, or a significant loss of productivity or impact on customer service. A refusal must give the reasons, any alternative period they'd agree to, and details of the Fair Work Commission dispute process.

## If you can't agree

Try to resolve it by talking first. If your employer refuses or doesn't reply in writing within 21 days, and you can't sort it out at work, you can take the dispute to the Fair Work Commission.
