## What's usual

The linea nigra ("pregnancy line") is a dark line of skin from your belly button down to your pubic area, and sometimes up towards the top of your abdomen. It often develops in the first trimester and can get darker and wider as pregnancy goes on, which is normal. Pregnancy hormones make your skin produce more melanin, and it's more likely if your skin is naturally darker.

It isn't dangerous, causes no symptoms, won't cause problems for you or your baby and needs no treatment. It will usually fade after the birth, but might not disappear, and you may get it again in later pregnancies. Sun on your belly can make it darker, so protect your abdomen from the sun. Bleaching treatments don't work, and creams containing hydroquinone can be harmful for your baby and should not be used in pregnancy.

## See your doctor or midwife

If you notice any other changes or dark areas of skin on your abdomen, see your doctor: some health conditions and moles can show up as a dark patch of skin. If you're not sure what the line is, you can show it to your doctor or midwife.
