## Why

Pre-cooked chicken that you eat cold, whether bought whole, in portions, sliced or diced, is one of the higher-risk foods for listeria, a bacterium that can grow in the fridge. Processed poultry is more likely to be contaminated, and a long shelf life increases the risk. The NSW Food Authority's advice is plain: cold chicken or turkey, for example from sandwich bars, do not eat.

## What to do

- Cook chicken fresh, cook it through, and eat it straight away.
- Hot takeaway chicken bought freshly cooked and eaten while hot is a "with caution" option.
- Leftovers: cool them, keep them in the fridge, use them within a day of cooking, and reheat them until they're steaming hot.
