Fish is worth eating in pregnancy: protein, iodine and omega-3. Food Standards Australia New Zealand suggests two to three serves a week of fish that isn't on the mercury list, cooked through and eaten hot. Salmon, sardines, mackerel, trout and herring are high in omega-3 and low in mercury.

Mercury builds up in a few large fish, and it can harm the baby's developing nervous system. The guidance:

- **Shark (flake), swordfish, broadbill or marlin:** one 150 g serve a fortnight, and no other fish that fortnight.
- **Orange roughy (sea perch) or catfish:** one 150 g serve a week, and no other fish that week.

Flake is shark, so ask what the fish is at the fish and chip shop. Cooking and canning don't remove mercury.
