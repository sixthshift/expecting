A transverse lie is when your baby is lying sideways across your uterus, so their back, shoulders, arms or legs may be first to enter the birth canal. An oblique lie is when they lie diagonally, with no particular part against the cervix.

Most babies with a malpresentation close to birth are born by caesarean. If your waters break and you've been told your baby isn't head first, seek medical help immediately by calling triple zero (000) and asking for an ambulance: there is a risk of cord prolapse.
