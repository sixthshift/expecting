Yes, hot. Processed meats such as ham, salami and luncheon meat are OK to eat if they're cooked to at least 75 °C and eaten straight away.

## Why

Processed meats are more likely to carry listeria, and a long shelf life makes it worse, because listeria can grow in the fridge. The NSW Food Authority puts cold processed meats in its "do not eat (high risk)" group unless they're thoroughly cooked to at least 75 °C and eaten soon afterwards. Thorough heating is what changes the answer.

Undercooked meat may also carry bacteria such as listeria and salmonella.

## What to do

- **Cook it through and eat it hot.** Ham, salami and luncheon meat are fine when they're cooked to at least 75 °C and eaten straight away.
- **No pink left** in sausages, pork or mince, including burgers.
- **Eat it soon after cooking.** It's the cold, stored version that's the risk.
- **Leftovers** go in the fridge, covered, and get eaten within a day, reheated until steaming hot.
- **Cold ham off the bone, or ham in a cold sandwich**, is a different answer: see deli meats.
