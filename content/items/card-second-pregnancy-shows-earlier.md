Your bump might appear earlier this time, because your stomach muscles have already stretched during your first pregnancy.

You might feel your baby move earlier too, which may be because you recognise the feeling and know what to look out for. If you haven't felt any movement by 24 weeks, it's important to contact your midwife, doctor or local maternity unit.
