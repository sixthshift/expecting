The Australian pages we checked don't say whether sleeping on your stomach is safe or unsafe, so there is no guidance to give you either way. If you're worried, ask your doctor or midwife.

## Why

Pregnancy, Birth and Baby's advice is about the other end of pregnancy: from 28 weeks until your baby is born, doctors recommend that you sleep on your side, at night and when you nap. Lying on your back later in pregnancy puts pressure on your major blood vessels, which can reduce blood flow to your uterus and your baby's oxygen supply, and research suggests this raises the risk of stillbirth.

## What to do

- **From 28 weeks, sleep on your side,** on either or both sides. Don't worry if you wake up on your back: roll over and keep sleeping.
- **To make side-sleeping easier,** bend your knees and put a pillow between them. You can also put a pillow under your belly for support.
- **A pregnancy pillow** can help improve the quality of your sleep. They come in many shapes and sizes.
