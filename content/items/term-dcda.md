DCDA stands for dichorionic diamniotic: each twin has its own placenta and its own amniotic sac. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby describes it as the most common type of twin pregnancy. The other two types, MCDA (a shared placenta, separate sacs) and MCMA (a shared placenta and sac, rarer), are for identical twins.
