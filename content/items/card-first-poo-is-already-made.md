By week 18 your baby's bowel is filling with meconium, which will become their first poos.

Around week 38, Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says meconium can sometimes be released during labour. If this happens, it would turn the amniotic fluid green, and your baby would then need to be monitored more closely since it could be a sign they are in distress.
