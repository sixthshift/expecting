Generally, if you miscarry in the first 20 weeks, the birth and death of your baby are not formally registered, so there is no birth or death certificate. You can ask for an optional certificate recognising early pregnancy loss. Miscarriage Australia says every state and territory has one. It isn't a legal document; it's a way of recognising your baby and your loss. Red Nose Grief and Loss has information on applying through your state or territory's Births, Deaths and Marriages registry.
