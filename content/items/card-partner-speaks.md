Somewhere in labour, your partner may not be able to say what they want. You may need to speak up on their behalf: tell the maternity team their needs and wishes, talk through what the team advises, keep them told what's happening, and help them make decisions.

The Royal Women's Hospital says a birth plan you prepared together leaves you more empowered to advocate for your partner. So write it with them: how much pain relief, which kinds they'd prefer, who they want there, and what if it's an induction or a caesarean. Come to the antenatal classes too.
