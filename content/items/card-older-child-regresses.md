Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says it's common for toddlers to feel left out or to blame the baby for changes to their routine. Sometimes they regress and become more "babyish": forgetting the potty, how to feed themselves, or crying more. This happens because they may be trying to show you that they are still small and still need your attention. Talking about "our baby" during pregnancy helps, and so does arranging for your toddler to visit you and the new baby in hospital. Try not to compare your toddler to others.
