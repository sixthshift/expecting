A baby's corrected age is the age they would be if they had been born on their due date. It's used to check a premature baby's growth and development, which are measured against corrected age rather than the date they were born.

To work it out, subtract the number of weeks they were born early from their actual age. A baby who is 16 weeks old and was born 8 weeks early has a corrected age of 8 weeks. Corrected age is useful until at least 2 to 3 years of age.
