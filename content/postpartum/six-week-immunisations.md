The baby's first round of routine vaccines is listed in the schedule as "2 months", but it **can be given from 6 weeks**. They're free under the National Immunisation Program for children on, or eligible for, a Medicare card.

At this visit:

- a combined vaccine for diphtheria, tetanus, whooping cough, hepatitis B, polio and Hib (*Haemophilus influenzae* type b)
- pneumococcal
- rotavirus

Your GP or a local immunisation clinic can give them; book ahead and bring the baby's health record book. The next rounds are at 4 and 6 months. Ask your GP or child health nurse.
