Red Nose's six recommendations reduce the risk of sudden unexpected death in infancy (SUDI). They apply to every sleep, day and night, from birth to 1 year:

1. Always put the baby **on their back** to sleep.
2. Keep the **head and face uncovered**.
3. Keep the baby in a **smoke-free** environment, before and after birth.
4. A **safe sleep space**, night and day: a cot meeting the Australian Standard, a firm flat mattress, feet at the bottom, light bedding tucked in to chest height — and no doonas, pillows, bumpers, lambswool or soft toys.
5. Their own safe space **in your room for at least the first 6 months**.
6. **Breastfeed** if possible.

Once the baby shows signs of rolling, stop wrapping and move from a bassinet to a cot. Free help to quit smoking: Quitline, 13 78 48.
