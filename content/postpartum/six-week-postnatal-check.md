Amid all the baby appointments, this one is for the person who gave birth. Most people have mostly recovered physically by about 6 weeks, though it depends on how the birth went.

The check is usually with a GP, midwife or OB. It covers physical recovery — bleeding, any stitches, bladder and bowel, sex and contraception, and a cervical screening test if one is due — and how you're feeling emotionally. If there were issues in pregnancy, like high blood pressure or gestational diabetes, it's a chance to check they've settled.

It's easy to let it slide, so book it before week 6 and write down questions beforehand. It's also a good moment to say honestly how things are going. Ask your midwife or OB.
