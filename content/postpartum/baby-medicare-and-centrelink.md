In hospital you'll get a **Parent Pack** with a **Newborn Child Declaration**. A midwife or doctor signs it, and it's your proof of birth — if it's lost, you'll need the birth certificate instead.

If you made a pre-birth claim, finalise it in myGov with the baby's details and proof of birth. For Parental Leave Pay, that has to happen **within 56 days**. Finalising it also enrols the baby in Medicare, with no separate form. Without a pre-birth claim, you can claim after the birth; to get Parental Leave Pay from the birth date, claim within 14 weeks.

If you're not getting Parental Leave Pay, you may be eligible for the **Newborn Upfront Payment and Newborn Supplement**, checked automatically when you claim Family Tax Benefit.

If either parent is still on their own parents' Medicare card, move to your own card first.
