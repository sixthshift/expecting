About 4 in 5 people who give birth get the **baby blues**: teary, up-and-down, overwhelmed, usually starting 3 to 5 days after the birth. It tends to pass within about 10 days.

If those feelings last **2 weeks or more**, or anxiety is taking over, it may be postnatal depression or anxiety. That affects about 1 in 7 women in the first year — and up to 1 in 10 fathers. It's common, it's treatable, and it isn't a failure.

Either of you can call:

- **PANDA** (Perinatal Anxiety & Depression Australia) — 1300 726 306, seven days a week
- **Lifeline** — 13 11 14, any time
- **000** in an emergency

Partners: you're often the first to notice. Ask your midwife or OB, or your GP.
