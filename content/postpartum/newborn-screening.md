In the first few days, every baby in Australia is offered some free checks and protection. You'll be asked for consent, so it's worth reading about them before the birth.

- **Vitamin K injection** — soon after birth, to prevent a rare but serious bleeding disorder.
- **Hepatitis B vaccine** — the first vaccine on the National Immunisation Program, usually given in hospital at birth.
- **Newborn bloodspot screening** (the "heel prick") — a few drops of blood on a card, in the first few days, screening for rare but serious conditions. If the result is normal, you won't hear anything.
- **Hearing screening** — quick and painless, usually within 2 days, and the baby can sleep or feed through it. If it isn't passed, it's repeated 12 to 24 hours later before any referral to a hearing specialist.

Ask your midwife or OB.
