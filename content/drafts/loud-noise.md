Ask. The page we checked doesn't mention noise, concerts or festivals, so there is no pregnancy-specific guidance on them.

## Why

For work, SafeWork NSW says employers are required to protect pregnant workers from any risks or hazards that may affect them in the workplace. The potential risks it lists are chemicals and materials, excessive travel, shift work, heavy lifting, physically exhausting work and mentally fatiguing work. Noise isn't on that list.

## What to do

- **If you're worried about something at work,** SafeWork NSW says to talk to your manager, human resources department or union representative. You might need to tell your employer you are pregnant earlier than you want to so they can make sure your workplace is safe.
- **If you're unsure about a particular event,** ask your doctor or midwife.
