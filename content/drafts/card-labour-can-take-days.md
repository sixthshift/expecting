The first phase of labour, the latent phase, can last for many hours or even days, with contractions that may be regular or irregular. You can usually stay at home during it. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby suggests resting or sleeping, eating light snacks such as fruit or toast, and drinking water, so you have energy for the active phase ahead. You can contact your midwife or doctor at any time if you're unsure or need support.
