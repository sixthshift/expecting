Fetal growth restriction (FGR) is when your baby grows more slowly than expected in pregnancy. Most commonly it's because the placenta hasn't developed well enough to give them the nutrients and oxygen they need (placental insufficiency).

It brings closer monitoring: extra ultrasounds and an umbilical artery Doppler. If there are signs your baby isn't well, your doctor may recommend an induction or caesarean before your due date. If you're concerned about your baby's movements, contact your doctor, hospital birth unit or midwife immediately, or go to your local hospital.
