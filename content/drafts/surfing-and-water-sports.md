**Water skiing: avoid.** Better Health lists it among sporting activities to avoid, because coming off the skis could force water into the vagina. The Royal Women's Hospital also lists water skiing among activities to avoid.

**Surfing, wakeboarding, jet skis, paddle boarding:** none of the sources listed here names these, so ask your doctor or midwife.

## Why

The general rules from Pregnancy, Birth and Baby are to avoid sports that:

- could cause you to fall
- could hit your baby
- involve sudden movements or changes in direction.

From the second trimester your baby is no longer protected by your pelvis, so avoid high-impact sports that could hit the bump. Your centre of gravity also moves, which raises the chance of a fall.

## What to do

- **Talk to your doctor or midwife** about the sport you do before you go out.
- **Swim instead.** Swimming and water aerobics support your weight and are among the exercises Pregnancy, Birth and Baby suggests.
- **Stop if you feel unsteady** or uncomfortable.
