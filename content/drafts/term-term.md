A baby born at term is born from 37 weeks. The AIHW, which keeps Australia's birth statistics, counts a term birth as 37–41 completed weeks. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby and the Better Health Channel say babies are full term anywhere between 37 and 42 weeks.

Before 37 weeks is preterm; 42 weeks or more is post-term. Your due date is only an estimate: about 5 in 100 babies arrive on it.
