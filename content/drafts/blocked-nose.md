## What's usual

Pregnancy rhinitis is inflammation of the nose that affects about 1 in 5 pregnant people. You may have a runny or congested nose, sneezing, itchy or watery eyes, or mucus in your throat. It isn't an infection and isn't contagious. It can happen at any time but is most common in the second trimester, can last at least 6 weeks, and usually disappears within 2 weeks of your baby's birth. It can affect your sleep and concentration.

Salt water (saline) nasal sprays and nasal irrigation are drug-free and safe in pregnancy. Some medicated nasal sprays and oral antihistamines can be used in pregnancy too: always check with your doctor or pharmacist before taking any medicine.

## Call 000

If you have unexplained breathing problems that are very bad and come on quickly, call 000 for an ambulance.

## See your doctor

See them as soon as possible if you have unexplained breathing problems. Also see your doctor if your symptoms are bothering you, you feel breathless, your chest feels very tight, or you're needing more of your usual asthma or lung medicines.
