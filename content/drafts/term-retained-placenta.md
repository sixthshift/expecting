A retained placenta is when all or part of the placenta doesn't come out after your baby is born; "retained products" is the term for placental tissue still in your uterus.

Signs include fever, heavy bleeding, bad-smelling discharge and many large clots. Contact your midwife or doctor immediately if you have any heavy bleeding from your vagina or pain in the days or weeks after the birth; the page's symptoms section says to contact your doctor or medical team as soon as possible. If the bleeding or pain is severe, call triple zero (000) and ask for an ambulance.
