## What's usual

Yes, babies can get hiccups while they're in your uterus. They can feel different from your baby kicking or moving around: many people describe a series of small, jerky, rhythmic movements. It isn't fully understood why babies hiccup; it might be related to the development of their lungs and diaphragm. Hiccups are a reflex, and they're nothing to be concerned about. No treatment is needed.

## Contact your midwife or doctor now

If you notice any change in your baby's usual movements or patterns, contact your midwife or doctor immediately.
