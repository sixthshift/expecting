You claim Family Tax Benefit (FTB) online in your Centrelink account through myGov, under **Apply for Family Assistance Payments (including Paid Parental Leave)**: the same claim as Parental Leave Pay.

## When to claim

There are two ways to be paid:

- **Fortnightly:** claim as early as **3 months before the due date**, and no later than **52 weeks after the birth**.
- **As a lump sum** after the year ends: claim **before 30 June of the next financial year**.

Either way, Services Australia needs proof of your baby's birth before it can finalise the claim.

## Payment choices

If you claim fortnightly, you choose how much to take during the year. Picking the right one reduces the risk of an overpayment. For FTB Part A:

- **All of it fortnightly:** best if you can give an accurate family income estimate.
- **The base rate fortnightly,** the rest after the year ends: may suit you if your income is hard to estimate (say, your pay changes each week) and your family income is likely to be under $123,078.
- **None during the year** (a "zero rate"), all of it after the year ends: may suit you if you can't estimate your income accurately and it's high but likely under the income limit.

FTB Part B has two choices: all fortnightly, or all after the end of the year. Taking it after the year may suit a couple where one of you is temporarily not working to care for the baby, or where the lower earner isn't confident estimating their income.

Services Australia will recommend a choice when you claim, and you can change it at any time in myGov.

## After the year ends

Services Australia balances your payments after the end of each financial year, and pays any FTB still owing then. You have **12 months from the end of the financial year** to confirm your family income, or to claim FTB as a lump sum.

## What to do

Include FTB in your pre-birth claim, and pick a payment choice that matches how sure you are of your income.
