Yes. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says massage therapy is generally considered safe, and it's important that your therapist is specially trained in pregnancy massage. Check with your doctor or midwife first that it's safe for you.

## Why

Pregnancy massage is designed to relieve common pregnancy problems and may reduce stress, back and leg pain, depression and anxiety, and improve mood and sleep. There is some evidence that massage helps in pregnancy, and good evidence that massage in labour can help manage pain and improve your emotional experience of labour.

## What to do

- **Position:** no lying on your stomach or back. Avoid lying flat on your back in the second half of pregnancy, especially the third trimester, because it can put too much pressure on the vein from your legs to your heart, and make you dizzy or short of breath.
- **Your therapist should** avoid massaging your abdomen, avoid certain pressure points, be very gentle on your legs (you have a higher chance of a blood clot in pregnancy), and use only creams or oils that are safe in pregnancy.
- **Your partner** can give you a gentle massage, as long as they know how to do it safely during pregnancy.
