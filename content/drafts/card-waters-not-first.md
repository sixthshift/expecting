In most pregnancies the waters break during the first stage of labour, but for some they break before labour starts. The fluid may gush or trickle, it usually isn't painful, and you may not feel anything at all. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says that if they break before labour, you should contact your midwife, doctor or birth centre. If the fluid is green, brown or blood coloured, it says to call your doctor, midwife or birthing unit immediately for advice, or go to hospital.
