It isn't only the person carrying the baby. Depression affects 1 in 10 fathers, from the first trimester of pregnancy right through the baby's first year. Anxiety is common too: 1 in 6 fathers have it during their partner's pregnancy.

Knowing the signs, in yourself or your partner, can help your family recover. One researcher behind SMS4dads, which sends free text messages to new and expecting dads, puts it plainly: "we need to be screening dads for depression, not just mums."
