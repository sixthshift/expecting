Limit them. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby lists sugar-sweetened drinks, such as soft drinks or cordial, among the foods to limit in pregnancy.

## Why

It says the safest drinks in pregnancy are water and milk. Small amounts of low-sugar soft drink, juice or mineral water are also fine.

## What to do

Make water your default. If you want something fizzy or fruity, keep it to small amounts. Cola and other caffeinated drinks also count toward your caffeine limit.
