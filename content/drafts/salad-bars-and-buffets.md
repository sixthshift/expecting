Mostly no. Pre-prepared fruit and vegetable salads, including those from buffets and salad bars, are on the list of foods to avoid when pregnant. The NSW Food Authority calls salads from salad bars and smorgasbords "do not eat (high risk)".

## Why

Pre-prepared salads have a higher risk of listeria, and listeria can grow even in the fridge. In pregnancy it can cause miscarriage, premature birth or stillbirth.

At warm temperatures most food-poisoning bacteria grow very quickly, and some make toxins. FSANZ's advice is to keep food either cold or hot, not lukewarm, and to limit time in the "danger zone" of 5 °C to 60 °C.

## What to do

- **Pass on the cold end**: pre-made salads, cut fruit, cold meats, cold cooked chicken, soft cheeses, smoked salmon and cooked prawns are all on the higher-risk list.
- **Choose freshly cooked food.** FSANZ says if you're vulnerable, it's safest to eat only freshly prepared food.
- **Make your own salad** instead: freshly prepared salads, washed well, are the safer alternative.
