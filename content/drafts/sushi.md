## Why

The NSW Food Authority rates store-bought sushi "do not eat (high risk)": making sushi increases the risk of listeria, and raw or undercooked fish raises it further. The rating covers store-bought sushi as a whole, not only the raw fish kind.

Listeria can cause serious illness in pregnant people and their babies, and it can grow in the fridge. Food Standards Australia New Zealand adds that anyone pregnant should not eat raw fish, because of risks such as listeria.

## What to do

- Make your own. Homemade sushi is okay as long as you don't use raw meat or seafood, and you eat it straight away.
- The NSW Food Authority's main advice for pregnancy applies here too: eat freshly prepared food, not refrigerated ready-to-eat food that may have been stored a long time.
