Ask first. None of the sources listed here mentions climbing or bouldering by name, so this answer comes from their general rules rather than a specific verdict.

## Why

The general rules all point at the risks climbing carries:

- **Falls.** Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says to avoid sports that could cause you to fall, and lists activities with a risk of tripping or falling among those that aren't safe in pregnancy.
- **Balance.** As the bump grows your centre of gravity moves forward, changing your balance and coordination. Better Health lists activities that need extreme balance, coordination and agility among those to avoid.
- **Looser joints.** Pregnancy hormones loosen your ligaments, so joint injuries are more likely.
- **Heights.** Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says to avoid sports involving high altitudes.

## What to do

- **Talk to your doctor or midwife** before you climb, and tell them exactly what kind of climbing you do.
- **Stop if you feel unsteady** or uncomfortable.
- Better Health's rule for anything you're unsure of: check with your healthcare professional.
