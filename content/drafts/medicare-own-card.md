If you're on the same Medicare card as your parents, **you need to transfer to your own Medicare card before you can enrol your baby**. Anyone 15 or older and enrolled in Medicare can get their own card and number.

## How

**Online.** If your Medicare online account is linked to myGov, sign in, select **My card**, then **Get a new card and number**, and follow the prompts. You can also use the myGov app. You can do this online if you're living in Australia as an Australian or New Zealand citizen or permanent resident, you're only on one Medicare card, it has other people on it, and nobody else acts for you for Medicare.

**By form.** If you can't do it online, complete the *Application to copy or transfer from one Medicare card to another* form and send it with your supporting documents.

The card arrives by post in about **3 to 4 weeks**, and you can use a digital copy straight away. Once you're on your new card, you can invite people to be added to it.

## Worth knowing

- **Two cards at most.** You can only be on 2 different Medicare cards at the same time.
- **You and your partner.** If you have different cards, you can copy or transfer onto one card or stay on separate cards. If your baby needs to be on your partner's card, you can ask to copy or transfer them after they're enrolled.
- **Check your Safety Net.** If you're getting your own card because your family situation has changed, also check your Medicare Safety Net registration.
