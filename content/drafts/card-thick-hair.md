During pregnancy, hormone changes such as oestrogen can cause your hair to grow more and get thicker. Your hair becomes thicker, you shed less and it stays in the growing phase for longer.

Around 2 to 4 months after you give birth, you may start to shed hair. This can be worrying, but you will not go bald. You can continue losing extra hair for up to 6 months, and by the time your baby is about 12 months old your hair should go back to normal. If the loss seems excessive, or your hair hasn't returned to normal after a year, speak to your doctor.
