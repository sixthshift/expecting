You don't have to "eat for 2" when pregnant. During the first 3 months you'll probably find you don't need to eat too many extra kilojoules.

In the second and third trimesters you'll need to increase your kilojoule intake. To be healthy, Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says to eat a bit more vegetables, lean protein and wholegrains every day.
