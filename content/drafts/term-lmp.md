LMP is the first day of your last menstrual period. Your baby is "due" around 40 weeks after it, and your gestational age is measured from it. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says this method is most accurate with a regular cycle, and can be more difficult and unreliable if your cycle is longer, shorter or irregular. An early ultrasound can also be used to work out your due date.
