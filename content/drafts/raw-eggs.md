## Why

Raw and lightly cooked eggs can carry salmonella, which causes food poisoning. If you get it in pregnancy, it can cause miscarriage, though this is rare.

Raw egg turns up in more places than you might think: homemade mayonnaise and aioli, mousse, lightly poached eggs, milkshakes made with egg, homemade custard, and raw cake or biscuit batter.

## What to do

- Cook eggs until both the yolk and the white are firm (about 72°C). Hard-boiled eggs and cooked omelettes are fine.
- Commercially made products with egg, such as mayonnaise, are fine. Commercial custard and dressings like aioli, and pasteurised eggs, are the safer alternatives.
- Buy clean, uncracked eggs and keep them in the fridge. Don't use eggs with a dirty shell, or eggs that were cracked and then stored.
- Bake the batter rather than tasting it, and wash your hands after handling raw egg.
- Keep leftovers in the fridge and eat them within a day.
