## What's usual

When the fertilised egg implants in the lining of the uterus, it can cause some cramping and light bleeding. But pain or bleeding in the first trimester can also be a sign of a problem, such as a miscarriage, an ectopic pregnancy or a molar pregnancy, so get medical help for vaginal bleeding or belly pain during the first trimester.

An ectopic pregnancy is one growing outside the uterus, usually in a fallopian tube. About 1 in 100 pregnancies is ectopic, and it usually shows up 6 to 8 weeks after your last period. If the tube tears, it can cause serious internal bleeding, which can be life-threatening. The signs usually come between weeks 4 and 12:

- vaginal bleeding or spotting
- tummy pain
- back pain
- pain in the tip of your shoulder
- nausea and vomiting
- a fast heart rate
- dizziness or feeling faint

## Call 000 or go to emergency

If you have heavy bleeding, strong pain or feel unwell, call triple zero (000) for an ambulance or go to your nearest emergency department.

If you have any of the signs of an ectopic pregnancy above while pregnant, go to the nearest emergency department or call triple zero (000) for an ambulance.

## Contact your hospital now

Before 20 weeks, contact your doctor, midwife or hospital immediately if you have severe pain or cramping low in your belly, or pain in the tip of one shoulder (especially if you're also bleeding), or if you feel dizzy or faint.
