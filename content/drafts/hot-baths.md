Yes. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says it's perfectly safe to have a warm bath when you're pregnant, as long as you don't become too hot.

## Why

You may feel warmer than usual in pregnancy, because of hormonal changes and extra blood flow to your skin, and your body may be less effective at cooling itself down. A significant rise in your temperature may affect your baby.

That's why saunas, spa baths and hot tubs are on the avoid list: they can raise your core body temperature, which could lead to overheating, dehydration or fainting.

## What to do

- **Warm, not hot.** Keep the water at a temperature that doesn't leave you overheated.
- **Get up slowly.** The Royal Women's Hospital says you're most likely to feel faint when you get up quickly from a chair or a hot bath. Sit or lie down at the first sign of faintness.
- **Skip the spa** and the sauna until after the birth.
- **Drink water** and don't stay in hot environments for long periods.
