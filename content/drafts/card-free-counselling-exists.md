You may not have to pay. Gidget Foundation's perinatal specialists offer expectant and new parents Medicare bulk-billed counselling, at no cost to you: 10 sessions a calendar year (its counselling fact sheet says at least 10, its Gidget House page up to 10), in person at a Gidget House or by video call. Parents who are trying for a family, have a baby up to one, have had a pregnancy or baby loss, or a traumatic birth are eligible.

First, see your GP for a Mental Health Treatment Plan and a referral letter to Gidget; book a longer appointment, as it can take 30 minutes or so. Then call Gidget on 1300 851 758.
