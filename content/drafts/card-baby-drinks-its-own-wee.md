In the early weeks of pregnancy your body produces the amniotic fluid. As your baby grows, they start to produce most of it through their urine and fluid from their lungs.

By around week 12, your baby is swallowing the amniotic fluid around them and passing it back out as urine. The fluid helps their lungs, digestive system and muscles develop, and helps them move around, and it protects them from pressure or injury.
