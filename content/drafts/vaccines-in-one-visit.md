The Department of Health says you can safely have the influenza, whooping cough and RSV vaccines in the same visit, as recommended by your health professional. All three are free through the National Immunisation Program while you're pregnant.

The windows overlap: flu at any stage of pregnancy, whooping cough from 20 to 32 weeks (it can be given up to delivery), and RSV from 28 to 36 weeks. So between 28 and 32 weeks you can have all three. If they're given together, the Handbook says to use different sites.
