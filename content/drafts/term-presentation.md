Presentation is the part of your baby's body facing downwards towards the birth canal. Position is where your baby's occiput, the bottom part of the back of their head, is in relation to your body. For a breech baby, position refers to where their sacrum (lower back) is instead. People, including medical professionals, sometimes mix the words up: "breech position" usually means breech presentation. Presentation is usually checked around 36 weeks.
