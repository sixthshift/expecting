Yes, as long as it's pasteurised. The NSW Food Authority says pasteurised milk, cream and yoghurt are okay to eat: check the best-before or use-by date and follow the storage instructions. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby calls water and milk the safest drinks in pregnancy.

## Why

Dairy foods are a good source of calcium and iodine, as well as protein and several vitamins, and FSANZ recommends moderate amounts of low-fat dairy. Unpasteurised (raw) milk is on the NSW Food Authority's "do not drink or use" list (high risk), because raw milk can carry several germs and cause severe illness.

## What to do

Choose pasteurised dairy, check dates, and keep it in the fridge. Kefir is a fermented drink: the NSW Food Authority lists kefir with kombucha, kvass and ginger beer as "do not drink" (high risk), for contamination risk and possible leftover alcohol.
