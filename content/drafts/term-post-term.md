A post-term birth is one at 42 or more completed weeks of pregnancy. Going past your due date to 41 to 42 weeks is called overdue.

If you go more than 10 to 12 days past your due date, or there is a risk to your or your baby's health, your doctor or midwife may recommend inducing labour to prevent complications that can come with being overdue. You can talk through the risks and benefits and decide with them.
