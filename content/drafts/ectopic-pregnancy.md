An ectopic pregnancy is when a fertilised egg implants outside the uterus, usually in one of the fallopian tubes. Sadly, there is no way for it to survive. In a tube it can make the tube tear or burst, causing serious internal bleeding, and it can be life-threatening. About 1 in 100 pregnancies is ectopic, and many people who have one have no risk factors.

## Call 000 or go to emergency

The signs usually come between week 4 and week 12:

- bleeding or spotting from the vagina
- pain in your tummy, or on one side of it
- back pain
- pain in the tip of your shoulder
- nausea and vomiting
- a fast heart rate
- dizziness or feeling faint

If you have any of these while pregnant, go to the nearest emergency department or call triple zero (000) for an ambulance.

## How it's found and treated

A blood test for the pregnancy hormone (hCG) and an ultrasound, which may be vaginal, show where the pregnancy is. Your doctor recommends treatment for your situation:

- **Watch and wait:** only if you're well or have little pain, the ectopic is small and hasn't ruptured, and your hCG is low and falling. While waiting, go to emergency or call 000 for tummy pain, shoulder-tip pain, heavy bleeding, dizziness or fainting.
- **Medicine:** an injection of methotrexate, if the ectopic is very small and there's no sign of internal bleeding. It takes time to work, and some people need another injection or surgery.
- **Surgery:** usually keyhole, and usually needed if you're bleeding inside or at risk of it. Sometimes it's open surgery, or the tube is removed.

You'll need follow-up tests to make sure the pregnancy has fully ended. Don't drink alcohol while recovering from surgery, or for at least 3 months after methotrexate.

## It's a loss, too

An ectopic pregnancy is a kind of early pregnancy loss, arriving with the fright of an emergency. It is normal to grieve. Your partner may be upset too, after watching you go through the pain and treatment. You're entitled to Medicare-funded counselling with a psychologist after a pregnancy loss; your doctor can arrange it. Red Nose Grief and Loss supports people after pregnancy loss, including ectopic, on 1300 308 307, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

## Next time

An ectopic pregnancy may affect your fertility; ask your doctor about your situation. Your chance of another one is higher. Wait 2 months after surgery, or 3 to 4 months after methotrexate, before getting pregnant again. When you are pregnant again, tell your doctor early: they will likely advise an early ultrasound to check the pregnancy is in the uterus.
