If in doubt, don't. SafeWork NSW's rule is simple: if something seems too heavy to lift, or you feel dizzy or faint, ask for help or find out if someone else can do it. None of these sources gives a weight limit.

## Why

Pregnancy hormones such as relaxin loosen your ligaments, which can raise the risk of joint injuries like sprains. As your weight and shape change, your centre of gravity moves forward, which can affect your balance. Your pelvic floor muscles are also weakened in pregnancy.

Better Health Channel lists heavy lifting among the exercises to avoid while pregnant, and the Royal Women's Hospital suggests avoiding heavy lifting and housework if you have backache.

## What to do

- **At home**, ask for help with heavy loads and furniture.
- **At work**, heavy lifting is one of the risks your employer must reassess once you're pregnant. Changes to your duties are more likely if your job is physically demanding, and you can ask for help with posture and moving at work.
- **If your usual job isn't safe**, you're entitled to move to a safe job (see working while pregnant).
- **If you lift weights for exercise**, Better Health Channel says choose low weights and medium to high repetitions, and avoid lifting heavy weights altogether.
