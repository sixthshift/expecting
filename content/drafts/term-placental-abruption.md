Placental abruption is when part or all of the placenta separates from the wall of your uterus before your baby is born. It's uncommon (fewer than 1 in 100 pregnancies) but a medical emergency.

It usually brings vaginal bleeding and abdominal pain; sometimes there is no bleeding, only sudden, severe pain. If you have bleeding, contact your midwife or doctor immediately. If the bleeding or pain is severe, call triple zero (000) and ask for an ambulance.
