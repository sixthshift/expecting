Ask first. The Australian pages we checked don't say whether particular essential oils, or a diffuser, are safe or unsafe in pregnancy.

## Why

Pregnancy, Birth and Baby lists aromatherapy among complementary medicines. It says these are often described as natural, but that doesn't mean they are safe, especially in pregnancy, and we often don't know much about their safety.

## What to do

- **Talk to your doctor or pharmacist** before using one. They can advise which are safe to use.
- **At a massage,** check with the therapist that they only use creams or oils that are safe in pregnancy.
- **Check the cost.** Aromatherapy is among the complementary therapies that are no longer claimable on private health insurance.
