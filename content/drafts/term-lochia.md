Lochia is the bleeding from your vagina after the birth. It's bright red and heavy, perhaps with clots, for about the first 3 to 4 days (lochia rubra); reddish-brown from about day 4 to 10 (serosa); then white or yellow from about day 10 to 28 (alba). Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says to expect it for up to 6 weeks; RANZCOG says it may continue for about two months.

Use maternity pads or period underwear until your 6-week check: tampons and menstrual cups may increase the chance of infection. If you pass large clots, Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says tell your midwife; RANZCOG says contact your midwife or doctor or present to an emergency facility. Tell your midwife too if you notice a bad smell.
