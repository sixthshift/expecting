## What's usual

Heartburn and indigestion are common in pregnancy, and they can feel like a burning in your chest. But you can't tell from the symptoms alone whether chest pain is your heart, so healthdirect's advice is to treat chest pain as heart-related until it's proven otherwise, and to have any chest pain checked by a doctor right away.

Being pregnant or having recently had a baby makes a blood clot more likely. A clot that travels to the lung (a pulmonary embolism) can cause chest pain that may be worse when you breathe in deeply, sudden or worsening breathlessness, a fast heartbeat, coughing up blood, or fainting.

## Call 000 or go to emergency

Call triple zero (000) immediately and ask for an ambulance if:

- your chest pain is severe, getting worse, or has lasted for 10 minutes
- you have chest pain or trouble breathing
- you have palpitations (a racing or pounding heart) along with chest pain, shortness of breath, dizziness or fainting

## See your doctor or midwife

See your doctor right away if you have chest pain that lasts more than 10 minutes while you're resting.

If you have heartburn, see your doctor immediately if you also have chest pain, feel short of breath, are vomiting up blood, are losing weight, or find swallowing painful or difficult.
