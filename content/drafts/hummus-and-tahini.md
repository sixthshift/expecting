Best avoided. All three Australian sources agree: the NSW Food Authority calls hummus, tahini and other sesame pastes "do not eat (high risk)", whether store-bought or home-made; FSANZ lists hummus and tahini, including dips containing tahini, as higher-risk foods; and Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says it's best to avoid all sesame products, including hummus, tahini and halva.

## Why

Sesame seeds may carry salmonella, and processing raises the risk of listeria and salmonella contamination. In pregnancy listeria can cause miscarriage, premature birth or stillbirth, and salmonella can cause miscarriage, though this is rare.

## What to do

- **Swap the dip.** FSANZ suggests pasteurised dips and spreads without tahini instead.
- **Read the label** on other dips: FSANZ includes any dip that contains tahini.
- **Keep dips in the fridge**, and don't leave them out for long: FSANZ says limit time in the "danger zone" of 5 °C to 60 °C.
- **Already had some?** If you're worried about something you've eaten, call your maternity care provider or doctor.
