A late miscarriage is a loss between 13 and 20 weeks. If it happens, you will usually need to go through labour and birth, which can be very hard emotionally. Your healthcare team will explain your choices and support you. Your doctor may recommend medicine to help start labour; sometimes a surgical procedure is recommended instead.

Being on a labour ward then can be difficult. Let your care team know what you need: they may be able to offer a more private space or extra support. Afterwards you'll usually be offered the chance to see and hold your baby, if you want to. It's always your choice.
