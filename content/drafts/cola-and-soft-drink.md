Yes, in limited amounts. Cola counts toward the 200 mg of caffeine a day recommended as the most in pregnancy.

## Why

Pregnancy, Birth and Baby gives about 35 mg of caffeine per 375 mL can of cola; another of its pages says 49 mg. Cola also often contains a lot of sugar, and sugar-sweetened drinks are on its list of foods to limit.

## What to do

Count cola with your coffee and tea, and check the label.
