It's likely safe, but there's no evidence it helps. Some people drink raspberry leaf tea in their last trimester to prepare for labour and birth. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says drinking it has not been shown to be helpful in any studies, and that it is likely to be safe in pregnancy and when breastfeeding.

## What to do

Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says it's always a good idea to check with your doctor or pharmacist before drinking any herbal tea in pregnancy, and to ask your doctor or midwife for advice on how to do so safely. Its general advice for herbal teas is to limit yourself to 2 cups a day. Large amounts of some herbs may be harmful during pregnancy.
