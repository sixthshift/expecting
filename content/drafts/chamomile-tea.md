A cup of chamomile before bed may help you relax, but Pregnancy, Birth and Baby lists it among the teas where there isn't enough information about safety. It's best to avoid drinking chamomile tea in large quantities.

## Why

There's a suggestion that chamomile could stimulate the uterus or lead to circulation problems for the baby. Higher rates of preterm labour and miscarriage have also been reported.

More broadly, herbal teas are a form of herbal medicine. They're often not studied scientifically, the amount of herb in them can vary, and they may contain ingredients that aren't on the label.

## What to do

- **Keep it small.** Don't drink chamomile in large quantities.
- **Ask first.** It's a good idea to check with your doctor or pharmacist before drinking any herbal tea while pregnant, especially if you take prescription medicine.
- **For any herbal tea,** limit yourself to 2 cups a day and vary the type.
