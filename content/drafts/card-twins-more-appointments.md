Most twin pregnancies go smoothly, but there are more risks, so you'll need more antenatal appointments and ultrasounds to pick up problems early. It's very important to find out whether your twins share a placenta, because that pregnancy needs careful monitoring.

Twins are more likely to be born early, before 37 weeks, so your health team might recommend a hospital with services to care for twins, and may advise against a home birth or birth centre. Premature babies sometimes need time in a neonatal intensive care unit or special care nursery.
