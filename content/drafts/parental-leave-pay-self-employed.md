Running your own business doesn't rule you out of Parental Leave Pay. You may still meet the work test if you're self-employed.

## Your hours count

If you're working for financial reward or gain, you can count your hours in the work test, **even if the business doesn't run at a profit**. That includes:

- providing goods and services for hire or reward
- carrying on a business, including as a partnership or enterprise
- working for a trust operating as a business.

If you get Parental Leave Pay and you're self-employed, Services Australia pays it to you directly.

## Not working on your paid days

Parental Leave Pay is paid for days when you're not working, and if you're self-employed you **must not work on any day you choose to get it**. If you work for more than one hour, Services Australia considers that work.

Parental Leave Pay is part of your taxable income, and it can affect your tax and some other payments and services.

## No employer leave to fall back on

Independent contractors work for themselves and aren't employees, so they don't have the same rights and obligations. Contractors aren't entitled to the same pay and conditions as employees. If you're not sure whether you're a contractor or an employee, the Fair Work Ombudsman suggests considering legal advice.
