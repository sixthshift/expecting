An ultrasound uses sound waves to create images of your baby in the uterus. The scan is done by a technician called a sonographer. In early pregnancy it can be done through your abdomen (tummy) or through the vagina (transvaginal); the sonographer recommends which. Ultrasounds during pregnancy don't harm you or your baby or increase your risk of miscarriage.
