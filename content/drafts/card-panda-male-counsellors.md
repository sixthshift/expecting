PANDA's National Helpline isn't only for the pregnant parent. Almost 1 in 10 callers are men, and PANDA has male telephone counsellors if you'd rather speak to another bloke about what's going on. They'll listen, support you, and help you find support designed for expecting and new dads.

Call **1300 726 306**, 7 days a week. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 000.
