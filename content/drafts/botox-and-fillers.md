Not recommended. Because cosmetic Botox is for beauty reasons and isn't medically necessary, Pregnancy, Birth and Baby and MotherSafe both say it can't be recommended during pregnancy.

## Why

This isn't because of known harm. There is minimal research, but small doses injected into a muscle are unlikely to enter your blood, and Botox does not usually cross the placenta, so it is unlikely to reach your baby. If you had Botox before you knew you were pregnant, it is unlikely to cause any problems, and MotherSafe says inadvertent exposure would not be a cause for concern.

Dermal fillers are different in one way: Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says there isn't enough research to know how they might affect pregnancy or breastfeeding.

## What to do

- **For fillers,** speak to your maternal health provider if you're considering treatment.
- **Botox for a medical reason** (it is also used for some neurological conditions and muscle spasm): talk to your doctor.
