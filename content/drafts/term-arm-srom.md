**ARM** is artificial rupture of membranes, also known as breaking the waters. A small instrument is placed in your vagina and the membranes around your baby are punctured to help bring on or speed up labour. It's a form of labour induction.

**SROM** and **PROM** are spontaneous rupture of membranes and prelabour rupture of membranes: your waters breaking by themselves before labour. **PPROM** is the preterm version, waters breaking early. The glossary describes premature rupture of membranes as waters breaking before the 37th week.
