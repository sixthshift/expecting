No. Put riding on hold until after the birth.

## Why

RANZCOG, Pregnancy, Birth and Baby, MotherSafe and Better Health all list horse riding among activities with a risk of falling that should be avoided in pregnancy.

Better Health spells out the two dangers. The motion of riding carries a risk of placental abruption, where the placenta separates from the uterus. And falling from a horse, or being kicked by one, carries a high risk of trauma to your baby, or even death.

Your balance changes as your centre of gravity moves forward with the bump, and your ligaments loosen, which make a fall more likely and an injury easier.

## What to do

- **Stop riding** for the rest of the pregnancy.
- **Around horses,** remember a kick is one of the risks Better Health names.
- **Stay active another way:** walking, swimming, water aerobics or an exercise bike.
- **If you have any injury to your tummy,** such as from a fall or a kick, contact your midwife or doctor immediately.
