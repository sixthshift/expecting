A premature or preterm baby is born before 37 completed weeks of pregnancy. In Australia, about 8 in every 100 babies are born prematurely.

Babies born between 32 and 37 weeks may need care in a special care nursery, and most have no serious long-term problems. Very premature babies, born before 32 weeks, have a higher risk of health and developmental problems and are usually cared for in a neonatal intensive care unit at first.
