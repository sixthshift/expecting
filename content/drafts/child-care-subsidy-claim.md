The Child Care Subsidy (CCS) helps with the cost of child care. You can only submit a claim **after the birth of your baby**, but the timing still matters, because a claim doesn't reach far back.

## The 28-day limit

A CCS claim can be backdated to the first Monday of a CCS fortnight, **no more than 28 days before your claim was submitted**. A CCS fortnight is two weeks long; for 2026–27 the first one started on Monday 6 July 2026, then every second Monday.

Services Australia can only pay CCS once **your claim has been approved and you've confirmed your child's enrolment**.

## How to claim

The easiest way is online. With your Centrelink online account linked to myGov, choose **Make a claim**, then under Families, **Apply for Family Assistance Payments (including Paid Parental Leave)**. You can track the claim online or in the Express Plus Centrelink app. If you can't claim online, call the Centrelink families line.

You claim as the person who is liable for the fees.

## Confirming the enrolment

Your child care service submits your child's enrolment details to Services Australia, and you confirm them in your Centrelink online account. Before you confirm, check they match your **Complying Written Arrangement**: the agreement your service completes with the details of your care sessions and fees. The same person who claims CCS needs to have that arrangement with the service. If the details don't match, speak to the service first.

## Keep it active

Your child must attend child care **at least once in the first 26 weeks** after your CCS starts. If they don't, your payment may stop and you'll need to submit a new claim.
