Oligohydramnios is the medical term for having low or not enough amniotic fluid. Anhydramnios is when no amniotic fluid is seen on an ultrasound. A low level can be due to a pregnancy complication and may sometimes be a sign of a problem. What it means depends on the cause (if known), how severe it is and how far along you are. Your doctor or midwife will explain what it means for you.
