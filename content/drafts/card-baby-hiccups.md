By week 18 your baby can yawn and hiccup. By week 28, Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says your baby may also get the hiccups, which are caused by their diaphragm contracting, and that it's normal if this happens.

From week 18 you might notice little "flutters" in your abdomen, known as quickening, and their movements become more distinct as the pregnancy goes on.
