## What's usual

When you're pregnant you may have more dreams than you normally would, and they can be unusual, or nightmares. Sometimes it's related to stress, or to inconsistent sleep. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby also notes that disturbing dreams in late pregnancy might be a sign that you feel anxious.

Try sticking to a regular sleep schedule, different sleep positions, or a pregnancy pillow.

## If they're disturbing you

If dreams are disturbing you, it can help to talk to your partner or a friend, or to consider seeing a counsellor. If you feel low, worried or on edge most of the time, see your doctor or midwife. For support, you can call PANDA on 1300 726 306 or Lifeline on 13 11 14.
