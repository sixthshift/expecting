King Edward Memorial Hospital says that if you and your baby are well enough, you can expect to go home from 4 hours after an uncomplicated vaginal birth, and between 24 and 72 hours after a caesarean birth. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says that if you go home early from a public hospital, midwives will usually visit you at home with care for you and your baby, including breastfeeding advice. Stays vary by hospital.
