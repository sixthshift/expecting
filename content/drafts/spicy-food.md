Yes. The page we checked doesn't say to avoid spicy food in pregnancy, so it's your call.

## Why

Heartburn is common in pregnancy, because of hormonal changes and your uterus pressing against your stomach, and it's often triggered by fatty or spicy foods, caffeine, chocolate or citrus fruit juice.

## What to do

If spicy food gives you heartburn, eat small meals more often, eat slowly, and avoid lying down or exercising after meals. If symptoms don't improve, see your doctor: there are medicines to control heartburn.
