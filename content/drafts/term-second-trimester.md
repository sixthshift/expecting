The second trimester is the middle part of pregnancy, from about weeks 13 to 26 according to Pregnancy, Birth and Baby's glossary. The Better Health Channel's glossary says 14 weeks to 26 weeks. Other pages from both give a start of 13 or 14 and an end of 26 or 27.
