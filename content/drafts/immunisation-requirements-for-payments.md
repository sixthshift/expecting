If you get Family Tax Benefit (FTB) Part A or help with child care fees, your child must meet immunisation requirements.

## What counts

Your child meets the requirements if they:

- are up to date with the age-appropriate early childhood vaccination schedule, or
- are on an approved catch-up schedule, or
- have a medical contraindication or natural immunity recorded on the Australian Immunisation Register (AIR).

The AIR is the national register that records vaccines given to all people in Australia. Your vaccination provider updates it.

## How it's checked

When you claim FTB or the Child Care Subsidy, Services Australia asks for your child's Medicare card number and their Individual Reference Number (the number next to their name on the card), and uses them to check the AIR. If your child isn't eligible for a Medicare card, tell them when you claim.

**You don't need to tell them** when your child gets a vaccination, is up to date, or starts a catch-up schedule.

## What happens if they're not up to date

- **FTB Part A** may be reduced by up to $36.54 a fortnight for each child who doesn't meet the requirements. Services Australia sends a letter explaining what to do before your payment is affected. For a lump sum claim, the reduction applies to the whole claim period.
- **Child Care Subsidy:** if your child stops meeting the requirements, you have **63 days** to meet them again. If you don't, the subsidy stops and you'll need to submit a new claim, which can only be approved once your child's status is up to date on the AIR.

If your child misses a vaccination, talk to your vaccination provider, such as a GP or community health clinic, about a catch-up schedule. If your child was vaccinated overseas, take translated evidence to a vaccination provider in Australia to update the AIR.
