The page we checked doesn't say either way, so ask your doctor or midwife if you want to eat hemp seeds.

## Why

The Food Standards Code was amended in 2017 to permit the sale of low-THC hemp seed foods. Hemp has no, or very low levels of, THC, the cannabinoid behind marijuana's psychoactive effects. Hemp seeds contain protein, vitamins, minerals and polyunsaturated fats, particularly omega-3. FSANZ's page doesn't mention pregnancy.

## What to do

If you're keen, raise it at your next antenatal visit.
