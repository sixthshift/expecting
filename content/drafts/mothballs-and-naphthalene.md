Pregnancy, Birth and Baby, on toxic household products to avoid during pregnancy, lists mothballs and toilet cakes among the chemicals to be aware of, because they contain naphthalene. It doesn't give a separate rule on using them yourself.

## Why

Exposure to lots of naphthalene can cause damage to your blood cells, which leads to a condition called haemolytic anaemia. If you breathe in or swallow chemicals in pregnancy, they can enter your bloodstream and pass to your baby via the placenta.

## What to do

- **Ask someone else** to deal with mothballs, as the page advises for any task that involves chemicals, and keep your home well ventilated.
- **Store them safely.** Young children may eat mothballs, so the page says not to use them around children under 3 years.
- **If you're worried** you've been in contact with a chemical, call the Poisons Information Hotline on 13 11 26. Being exposed once is very unlikely to cause harm, the page says.
