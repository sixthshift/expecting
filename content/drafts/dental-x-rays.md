Yes, when your dentist needs them. MotherSafe says dental x-rays are safe during pregnancy. Pregnancy, Birth and Baby says your dentist may need to take them to give you the best care, and that they can usually be taken during pregnancy.

## Why

Pregnancy, Birth and Baby's general advice on x-rays is to tell your doctor and the radiology staff you're pregnant before one, and to discuss whether the test can be delayed or avoided. It says x-rays that don't directly involve your abdomen give a lower dose of radiation to your baby.

## What to do

- **Tell your dentist you are pregnant** before they take any x-rays.
- **Expect a lead apron.** MotherSafe says it is usual practice to provide one for shielding during dental x-rays.
