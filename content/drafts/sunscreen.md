Yes. MotherSafe says sunscreens contain ingredients such as oxybenzone, octocrylene and octisalate, that absorption through the skin is generally minimal, and that they don't pose a risk to pregnant people.

## Why

MotherSafe says using sunscreen is advisable, because it is just as important to avoid sunburn in pregnancy as at any other stage of life.

## What to do

- **Use sunscreen.** MotherSafe says it is advisable, and that sunscreens don't pose a risk to pregnant people.
