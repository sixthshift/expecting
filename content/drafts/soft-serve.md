## Why

Soft serve is stored at a temperature that allows listeria to grow, which is why it's on Food Standards Australia New Zealand's list of foods to avoid in pregnancy. Listeriosis is rare, but in pregnancy it can cause miscarriage, premature birth or stillbirth, and even a small amount of listeria in food can cause illness.

## What to do

- Skip the cone from the machine.
- Have frozen ice cream or gelato instead. Packaged frozen ice cream is the safer alternative: keep it frozen hard.

If you've already had one, listeriosis is rare. Symptoms usually start about 3 weeks later, but can take anywhere from 3 to 70 days, and can look like a mild flu: fever, headache, tiredness, aches. They may seem mild in pregnancy, but if you get them, seek medical attention immediately.
