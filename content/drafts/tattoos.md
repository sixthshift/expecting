Best to wait. It's generally recommended to avoid getting a tattoo while you're pregnant or breastfeeding. The safest option is to wait until after both before getting a new one.

## Why

There isn't much research, and there's no clear evidence that tattoos are either safe or unsafe in pregnancy or breastfeeding. There's a risk of infection if the tattoo is done somewhere that isn't hygienic.

Tattoos and piercings can bring a higher chance of blood-borne infections, such as hepatitis B, hepatitis C and HIV. This can happen if the work is done with contaminated equipment.

There's also a very low risk that tattoo ink could pass into breast milk; it isn't known how the smaller ink particles might affect it.

## What to do

- **Put the new tattoo off** until after pregnancy and breastfeeding.
- **When you do go ahead,** choose a reputable, registered business that uses sterile procedures and equipment. Ask how they sterilise their equipment and whether they reuse needles.
- **See your doctor** if a tattoo becomes painful, bleeds a lot or weeps pus: it may be infected and need antibiotics.
