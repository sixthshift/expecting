"Antenatal" just means before birth. The first antenatal visit is ideally around 6 to 8 weeks, and before 10 weeks.

Most people start with their GP, who can confirm the pregnancy, order early blood tests, explain the options near you and write a referral. The main paths:

- **Public care** — through a public hospital, often midwife-led, sometimes with a known midwife (midwifery group practice). Covered by Medicare.
- **GP shared care** — check-ups split between your GP and the hospital.
- **Private care** — a private obstetrician (OB) or private midwife you choose, usually with out-of-pocket costs.

A private midwife doesn't usually need a referral; most other paths start with one from your GP. Ask your GP what's available locally.
