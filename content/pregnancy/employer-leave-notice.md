Under the National Employment Standards, employees can take up to 12 months of unpaid parental leave, and request up to a further 12. Two notice deadlines apply to each working parent:

- **At least 10 weeks before leave starts:** written notice saying how much leave, and the start and finish dates.
- **At least 4 weeks before:** confirm those dates, or tell your employer about any change.

If leave is planned to start a couple of weeks before the due date, the 10-week notice lands around week 28. If a baby arrives early and notice wasn't possible, the leave is still available — give notice as soon as you can.

Check your workplace policy and award or agreement too: some employers offer their own paid leave on top of the government's.
