In Australia, folic acid and iodine supplements are recommended for everyone planning a pregnancy or pregnant.

- **Folic acid** (a form of folate, a B vitamin) lowers the chance of neural tube defects such as spina bifida. The usual advice is 0.5 mg (500 micrograms) a day, starting at least a month before trying and continuing for the first 3 months of pregnancy. Some people are advised a higher dose.
- **Iodine** helps the baby's brain and nervous system develop. The usual advice is a supplement with 150 micrograms a day — before, during and while breastfeeding.

Many pregnancy multivitamins contain both. Anyone with a thyroid condition is advised to check before taking iodine. Your GP can advise on dose before pregnancy; once pregnant, ask your midwife or OB.
