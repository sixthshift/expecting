# The Library

Everything Expecting knows: some 900 entries, a note for each of the 42
weeks and a read for each day, written from Australian sources and checked
against them. It's
opened by the question you came with, not by a table of contents.

How it's built is [`architecture.md`](../architecture.md), Library and
Content. How an entry is written is
[`content/README.md`](../../content/README.md); the review's standing
calls are [`content/REVIEW.md`](../../content/REVIEW.md).

## Why you open it

- **"Can I…?"** In the deli queue: soft cheese, a hot bath, the hair dye
  appointment.
- **"Is this normal?"** At 2 am, with a symptom: whether to wait, mention
  it, or call.
- **"What does that mean?"** A word the midwife used: effaced, CTG, anti-D.
- **"What happens at…?" and "What can we claim?"** Before an appointment,
  or when the money comes up.
- **"What do we decide?"** Public or private, screening, where to give
  birth.
- **Because the app sent you:** a task's why, a week's heads-up, a list
  line's standard, a feeling's someone to talk to.

## What it holds to

- **It explains; it never tells her what to do about her body.** Medical
  content says what usually happens and when, and anything clinical ends
  with asking the midwife or doctor. Admin is the exception: bookings,
  claims, forms and purchases are told plainly, with a when and a why.
  Booking an appointment is admin; what happens at it is medical.
- **Every claim from a source, in its own urgency.** Never softer, never
  stronger, never two sources merged into one. Australian sources first
  (healthdirect's Pregnancy, Birth and Baby, Services Australia, RANZCOG,
  the states). Exact weeks and entitlements are checked, never from
  memory, and money is re-checked each 1 July and indexation.
- **Nothing reassures where it should send you to a phone.** A symptom's
  page shows when to get help before what's usual: someone wondering
  whether to worry needs that first. Each level has its action: 000, the
  birth suite's number (or a way to add it), the midwife's and GP's, or a
  question for the next visit. Every phone number is tappable.
- **Reviewed, then shipped.** An entry goes in once it's written and
  reviewed ([`content/REVIEW.md`](../../content/REVIEW.md)); there are no
  drafts in the app.
- **Tailored by your answers and extras.** An entry for some readers only
  (private cover, a planned caesarean, the baby shower) shows to them
  alone. An unanswered question hides nothing.

## The home

It lists no entries, because nobody comes to browse 893:

- **Search** first, over titles, the questions as people ask them, aliases
  and summaries, with title and alias matches first. The box is at the top
  of every page in the Library (the home, a door, an entry), and stays as
  you go between them: typed on an entry's page, it goes on to the results
  without losing a letter, and Back returns to the entry. On a phone it is
  the Library's top bar. Bodies aren't
  searched, so the index stays small enough to search offline at once.
- **A door per type, named by its question:** Can I…?, Is this normal?,
  Nobody tells you, What does that mean?, What happens at…?, What do we
  decide?, What can we claim?, How do we…? Each is laid out for its
  question: rows by topic or trimester, symptoms with "Know these first"
  under a call-now box, cards, an A–Z glossary.
- **When it's hard,** a quiet ninth door at the foot: loss, mental health,
  special circumstances. There for whoever looks, never put in front of
  anyone.
- **The topics,** with their counts, for whoever does want to browse.

On a desktop an entry doesn't leave the page it was opened from, two ways
for two reasons:

- **In the Library** (a door, search, Right now) you came to read, so it
  opens beside the list, on the same page
  (`/library?door=can-i&entry=sprouts`): the list stays as it was (its
  chips, its groups, the search), narrower, on the left, the one open
  marked, and the entry reads on the right, scrolling on its own. Browse
  and read in one place: the next is a click in the list, taking the last
  one's place in the history, so Back is to the list alone. Its close and
  Esc put it away. "Open full page" goes to the entry's own page, the
  article alone. From Right now, the list beside it is the one it came
  from. The page keeps its shape with nothing open: beside a door's shelf
  (or a topic's), its overview, what the door is for, what a "Can I…?"
  answer's words mean, and what from it is around now, each opening in its
  place; a search opens what it found first, so it has no close.
- **Anywhere else** it's the reason for something on that page, so it opens
  in the side panel beside it: a task's Why beside To do, a day's read beside
  the week. The page stays where it was and live. "Open in full" goes to its
  page.

Never a private entry, which opens on its own page with its quick exit, as
on a phone. On a phone an entry is always its page; an address for one
beside a list opened there goes on to it.

An entry's page leads back to the door it came from. Opened from a list
(a door's whole shelf, search results, Right now, Related, Home's worth
knowing), it goes on through that list: swipe left for the next, right for
the one before, ← and → on a keyboard, or the Previous and Next links at
its foot. Each takes the
page's place in the history, so Back is still to the list. One an answer
hides is passed over.

Back out of an entry, by its link at the top or the phone's Back, and the
list is as you left it, scrolled to where you were, so a long shelf isn't
started again from the top.

## Read

Opening an entry marks it read, for whoever opened it: each of you has your
own, so a tick on your phone means you've read it, not that one of you has.
Wherever entries are listed (the doors' shelves, search, Right now, Related,
Home's worth knowing) a read one wears a quiet tick, so you can see
what's new to you. The first time you opened it is kept; there's nothing to
undo, as opening it is all it takes. A private entry is never marked: it
leaves no trace.

## The types

One item with a type, so there's one search, one page and one list for
the timeline, Planner and Home to work from.

| Type | What it is | Its page |
|---|---|---|
| **Moment** | Something that happens in a window, with tasks | The RSV vaccine, 28–36 weeks |
| **Payment** | What you can claim, when and how | Parental Leave Pay |
| **Decision** | Options and what to weigh | Public, private or midwifery group practice |
| **Can I…?** | A verdict (yes, limit, avoid, ask first) and why | Soft cheese: avoid |
| **Symptom** | When to get help, most urgent first, then what's usual | Itchy palms late in pregnancy |
| **Guide** | The long reads | Labour, feeding, recovery |
| **Card** | Nobody tells you: a headline and a paragraph | "You bleed for up to six weeks after the birth." |
| **Term** | A word you'll hear, in a sentence or two | Effaced |

- **Weeks are optional.** An entry with weeks appears on them across the
  app; without, it's in the Library only.
- **A decision becomes a task only with a decide-by week from its
  source.** Otherwise it stays reading, on its weeks.
- **A task belongs to the entry that explains it:** "Book the RSV
  vaccine" lives on the RSV vaccine. Its window (opens, best by, a deadline
  only from a source) and whose it is are the entry's to say.

## The week's note

One per week, in three parts: The baby, Your body, For the partner. It's
Home's page, and its title is the week's line on the timeline. A week
without its own note shows the latest before it.

## Private entries

Family violence and reproductive coercion. Both phones share one space,
and the other parent may be the one causing harm, so a private entry can be
read and searched like any other but leaves no trace where the other phone
would see it:

- **Nothing to save from it:** no tasks, no list lines, no tailoring, no
  extra, no questions for the appointment, and the server refuses any save
  that names one. Words someone types themselves are theirs: blocking
  "family violence" in your own question would be the app deciding what you
  may ask.
- **Never offered unasked:** not on Home, Right now or the timeline,
  and not in what an answer changes. It waits to be looked for.
- **Not in the address bar or the history,** and search words that find
  one aren't kept in the address.
- **A quick exit,** at the top of its page and on Esc, to Home,
  replacing the page in the history. Home rather than another site:
  instant, offline, works in the installed app, and looks like nothing.
- **Not a trace, so unchanged:** every entry's page is fetched for offline
  as the app installs, so a private one being there says nothing, and the
  document's title is always "Expecting".

## Discreet entries

Mental health, drugs, smoking. Shared like any other, but a question asked
from one isn't named "from *title*" on the shared list, since the title
would tell the other parent what the question's words don't. Its
suggestions ask about the need, not the disclosure.

## While something has happened

The quiet door comes first, nothing is open right now, and no entry draws
today against its weeks. Everything is still there to be looked for.

## Not doing

- **Telling her what to do about her body,** or "normal" for anything she
  keeps.
- **Renaming an id:** ticks, marks and questions point at them.
