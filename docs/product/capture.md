# The capture "+"

One way in to keep anything, from any pillar, without leaving where you
are: a movement, sickness, a craving, a note, a photo or scan, a result or
paper, how you're feeling, a first, a question for the appointment, or
something to do. Each is filed into its pillar, the Journal or the
Planner.

How it's built is [`architecture.md`](../architecture.md), The pillars (the
capture "+") and PWA (sharing in). What each thing becomes once kept is its
pillar's: [journal.md](journal.md), [planner.md](planner.md).

## What it holds to

- **Within thumb reach.** On a phone a round "+" floating bottom-right,
  just above the tab bar, which already holds six pillars and doesn't take
  a seventh slot. On a desktop, Add at the top of the sidebar, and `n`. It
  steps aside while the page scrolls down, so it never covers a row.
- **Kept where you are.** It opens a sheet over the page; once kept, the
  sheet closes and a toast says what was kept and where ("Note kept", In
  the Journal), with a way there. You stay on the page you were on.
- **A fixed order.** No "most used first" and no remembering the last pick:
  rows that move are harder to hit with a thumb.
- **Each form is written once.** The "+" draws each pillar's own form, so
  keeping a note from the "+" and from the Journal is the same thing.
- **The careful ones keep the sheet open.** A movement felt as slower,
  weaker or stopped, or can't keep fluids down, shows the call in the sheet,
  which doesn't close on its own while it shows. A craving for something
  that isn't food keeps it open on its quiet word.
- **On the pillars only,** not on settings, Emergency info, It's time,
  onboarding or the login page.

## What's offered when

- **How I'm feeling** only from a phone that's said who's holding it: a
  feeling is someone's.
- **The baby moved** from week 16, or earlier once one's been kept.
- **Sickness, cravings, photos, firsts and something to do** at any week.
- **While something has happened:** a note, a result or paper (papers are
  admin, and there are more after a loss, not fewer), a feeling and a
  question. No movement, ever, no sickness or craving, no first, and
  nothing to do, as To do is put aside. A photo can still be kept from
  inside the Journal's fold.

## Sharing in

On Android, Expecting is in the phone's share sheet once installed:
pictures, PDFs, words and links. A share opens the "+" offering only what
it can become (pictures a photo or a paper, a letter photographed being a
paper; a PDF a paper; words a note, a question or a task), straight to the
form if there's only one. It waits through logging in or the first-run
questions. An iPhone has no share target, so there it's the "+".
