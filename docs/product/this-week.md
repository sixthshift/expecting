# This week

Home: where you are, and what matters now. Opening the app asks "where are
we now, and what matters?", not "where are we in all of it" (that's the
[timeline](timeline.md)), so the landing page is the week.

How it's built is [`architecture.md`](../architecture.md), the This week
row.

## Why you open it

- **The weekly ritual:** the baby, my body, this week, often read together.
- **"What do we need to do?"** The few things to do now, each with why.
- **Because something's due:** a question Expecting needs answered, an
  extra in its week, a check-in.

## What it holds to

- **It stays about the week.** The handy things have the Drawer rather
  than crowding it, at the cost of a sixth tab on a phone.
- **Where you are, first:** the week, the baby's size, how far along.
- **To do now is short and ordered by urgency, not by week:** a deadline
  within two weeks or just missed, then what slipped in the last four
  weeks, then what's best done in the next two. Older slips are left to the
  Planner, so home never becomes a list of failures. It's the same list
  the Planner leads with, and a tick is a tick everywhere.
- **Asked, one thing at a time, when it's due.** Whoever is holding the
  phone is asked how they are today ([journal.md](journal.md#how-youre-feeling));
  what Expecting needs to know is asked in the week it starts to matter,
  as close as possible to the task it changes, and again four weeks after
  a "not sure" or "not now"; one undecided [extra](extras.md) is offered
  in its week, once.
- **The week's note is read as you,** tailored by your answers: its own,
  or the latest before it.

## What's on it

- **The week's note:** The baby, Your body, For the partner.
- **One heads-up a week:** a "nobody tells you" card, under the note.
  Walking the weeks in order, each takes the card covering it that started
  nearest and hasn't been taken, so both phones see the same card all week
  and none repeats.
- **Worth knowing:** what starts this week and belongs to it, meaning it
  asks something of you or its window is twelve weeks or less (longer
  windows are any time, so they stay in the Library). High stakes first,
  then what asks something of you; five, then "Show all". Never a card, a
  hard topic (loss, mental health, special circumstances) or a private
  entry: those wait to be looked for.
- **Coming up:** the next eight weeks after To do now's two, by week.
- **The questions waiting** for the next appointment.
- **A peek at another week** (a step back or ahead): its note, heads-up,
  what's worth knowing and what's best done then, and that week's notes and
  movements, with a way back to this week.

On a desktop, the week on the left and the doing on the right.

## While something has happened

This week is support, not a week: the guide's opening lines, its parts for
what happened (someone to talk to, and after a loss your body and how you
might feel), then the next steps when you're ready, only those that fit
the week it happened, so a miscarriage at 8 weeks isn't offered stillbirth.
No week, size, countdown, tasks, questions to answer, check-in or extras on
offer, and no peeking at another week.

## Not yet

- **The appointment waiting room:** on appointment day, your questions,
  then a few cards while you wait. Waits for appointments.
- **The quiet first weeks,** before you've told anyone.
