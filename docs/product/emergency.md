# Emergency info, and who to call

The page for when something's wrong: the calls, then a handover for
whoever is treating her, on one screen. It sits beside settings, not
among the pillars, one tap from every pillar. The most important button
in the app is personal: **your** birth suite's number, one tap away
everywhere.

How it's built is [`architecture.md`](../architecture.md), The pillars (the
call button, Emergency info).

## Why you open it

- **To call.** The birth suite, the midwife or OB, 000.
- **To hand over.** A paramedic or a new doctor asks who she is, how far
  along, allergies, blood group, medicines. The partner, holding the
  phone, shouldn't have to remember.
- **"When should we call?"** The warning signs, said plainly.
- **"Show me your birth plan."** Triage, 2 am, poor signal.

## What it holds to

- **The call button is on every pillar,** beside settings on a phone and
  in the sidebar on a desktop, once the birth suite's number is filled in.
  Until then the way to Emergency info is where the number goes. "Is this
  normal?" ends at the same number whenever the answer is "call".
- **The calls first, in one row:** 000, the birth suite, the midwife or
  OB.
- **The handover in the order a paramedic or doctor asks:** who she is and
  her age, how far along and the due date, allergies (loudest), blood
  group, this pregnancy, past pregnancies, medicines, history, where she's
  booked and her record number. All on a phone's first screen.
- **A blank never reads as none.** "No known allergies" is a tick, not an
  empty field. Blanks are left out of the handover and listed once as
  still blank: that list tells whoever's treating her what isn't known,
  and a count would hide it.
- **The answers fill in what they can,** so nothing is typed twice.
- **Hers.** It's about the carrying parent. The baby's doctor and numbers
  wait for the weeks after the birth.
- **Shared and editable by both,** and readable offline.

## What else is on it

- **Papers to hand:** the papers kept handy (the birth plan, the blood
  group, the referral, the discharge summary), opened in place, there with
  no signal too ([journal.md](journal.md#results-and-papers)).
- **When to call:** the warning signs' "straight away" list, from
  healthdirect (a change in the baby's movements, bleeding, a severe
  headache or vision changes, fluid leaking, fever, itching), each with its
  own page in the Library.
- **Everyone else to call,** and the front desk's numbers.
- **It's time,** linked at any week ([its-time.md](its-time.md)).

## While something has happened

It stays: it's admin, and there may be more calls, not fewer. After a loss
it stops counting weeks: the pregnancy ended at its weeks.

## Not yet
