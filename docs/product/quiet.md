# Something has happened

Some pregnancies end in miscarriage or stillbirth, and screening sometimes
brings bad news. An app that keeps cheerfully showing "the size of a
lemon" after a loss is cruel. So Expecting has a quiet path, designed in
from the start: it pauses the weeks for both of you and offers support in
their place. Every pillar says what it does while it's quiet.

How it's built is [`architecture.md`](../architecture.md), Something has
happened.

## What it holds to

- **Two kinds, one switch.** A loss (a miscarriage, a stillbirth, a baby
  who has died), or hard news (a worrying result, a baby in intensive care:
  the pregnancy goes on, but not as it was). Nothing else is asked: the
  week is known, and a name is yours to give or not.
- **The way in is where you'd look,** not where you'd see it every day:
  the guide's own page in the Library (where "we lost the baby" lands in a
  search) and Settings. Not on This week or in the nav. Choosing a kind and
  confirming it is all.
- **For both of you at once.** One of you says so; neither phone shows the
  weeks after that.
- **Nothing is deleted.** What's put away comes back as it was.
- **Nothing pregnancy-forward.** No week counted, no size, no countdown,
  nothing about the baby, nothing to come, no nudges, no recaps, no
  suggestions of what to keep.
- **What's admin or yours stays:** papers (there are more after a loss,
  not fewer), notes, feelings, questions for the appointment, Emergency
  info, the call button, what you've already set in motion.
- **The way back waits; it never asks whether you're ready.** Hard news can
  become a loss, never the other way. Hard news comes back with "Bring the
  weeks back"; a loss has no pregnancy to resume, so only "This was set by
  mistake". Either is confirmed once and brings everything back for both of
  you.

## Pillar by pillar

- **This week** is support: someone to talk to, and the next steps that
  fit the week it happened ([this-week.md](this-week.md#while-something-has-happened)).
- **The timeline** is paused at the week it happened.
- **The Library** opens with its quiet door first, and nothing open now.
- **The Planner** puts To do aside and lists what was already set in
  motion: claims that may still apply, bookings you might cancel. The
  questions stay.
- **The Journal** keeps the notes, letters, feelings, papers and what was
  done, and folds the rest behind one quiet line
  ([journal.md](journal.md#while-something-has-happened)).
- **The "+"** offers a note, a paper, a feeling and a question.
- **The Drawer** is put away: the names game, Who does what and Your people.
- **Emergency info** stays, and stops counting weeks after a loss.
- **It's time** isn't shown.
- **Settings** stops nudging you to answer the next question.
